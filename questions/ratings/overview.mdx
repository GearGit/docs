A rating question asks how much, how good or how likely, and hands back a point on a scale. Two things tell you which one to use. First, what a point should be: a picture on [Thumbs up or down](/questions/ratings/thumbs-up-down), [Smiley rating](/questions/ratings/smiley-rating) and [Star rating](/questions/ratings/star-rating), a number on [NPS](/questions/ratings/nps), [Opinion scale](/questions/ratings/opinion-scale) and [Numeric slider](/questions/ratings/numeric-slider), a word on [Likert](/questions/ratings/likert) and [Text slider](/questions/ratings/text-slider). Second, how long the scale is and who decides: NPS and Opinion scale are fixed at 0 to 10 so scores stay comparable, Smiley and Star offer a choice of lengths, and Likert and Text slider are exactly as long as the list you write. [Range slider](/questions/ratings/range-slider) is the odd one out, because its answer is a band rather than a point.

## Compare

| | Answers with | Can be required | Drives a loop | Scorable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Thumbs up or down](/questions/ratings/thumbs-up-down) | A thumb up or a thumb down | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [NPS](/questions/ratings/nps) | A number from 0 to 10 | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [Likert](/questions/ratings/likert) | One scale point, in your wording | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [Opinion scale](/questions/ratings/opinion-scale) | A number from 0 to 10 | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [Smiley rating](/questions/ratings/smiley-rating) | A number, which face they picked | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [Star rating](/questions/ratings/star-rating) | A number, how far along the row | Yes | Yes | Points per scale point |
| [Text slider](/questions/ratings/text-slider) | One named stop | Yes | Yes | No |
| [Numeric slider](/questions/ratings/numeric-slider) | A number on the track | Yes | Yes | Points per unit |
| [Range slider](/questions/ratings/range-slider) | A low number and a high number | Yes | No | No |

## Naming the ends, and choosing the length

Five of them caption the ends for you. [Thumbs up or down](/questions/ratings/thumbs-up-down), [NPS](/questions/ratings/nps), [Opinion scale](/questions/ratings/opinion-scale), [Smiley rating](/questions/ratings/smiley-rating) and [Star rating](/questions/ratings/star-rating) each take a **High label** and a **Low label** in the panel. On a scale of five points or fewer the caption sits under the point it belongs to; on a longer one it sits at the end of the row. NPS and Opinion scale start with **Not at all likely** and **Extremely likely** in place, so an opinion scale is worth rewording before it goes out. The rest start empty.

Length is set differently in each one. NPS and Opinion scale are fixed at eleven points. Smiley rating takes **Scale** 3 or 5, Star rating **Scale** 3, 5, 7 or 10. Likert and Text slider are as long as the list you write. The two numeric sliders take their ends from **Minimum** and **Maximum** in the **Validation** dialog, which are also the bound a respondent cannot drag past.
