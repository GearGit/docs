A ready-made question is a plain question whose job is already known. Four come with a list written for you: [Gender](/questions/ready-made/gender), [Age group](/questions/ready-made/age-group), [Education level](/questions/ready-made/education-level) and [Marital status](/questions/ready-made/marital-status) are [Single choice](/questions/choice/single-choice) questions with their options in place, every one of them yours to reword or replace. The other six take a typed answer: [Name](/questions/ready-made/name), [Mobile phone](/questions/ready-made/mobile-phone), [Email address](/questions/ready-made/email-address), [Occupation](/questions/ready-made/occupation), [Country or region](/questions/ready-made/country-or-region) and [Address](/questions/ready-made/address) are one box each, with the same [text limits](/rules/text-limits) and [patterns](/rules/patterns) behind **Validation** as [Short text](/questions/text/short-text). Take the ready-made one when the standard list saves you drafting it, or when results should treat the answer for what it is: a name or a postal address is held back from every shared report, and a typed country is charted as bars rather than as a word cloud. Take the plain kind when you want to change the hint inside the box or switch the question to another type later, because a ready-made question keeps its type.

## What the ready-made ones add

**The starter content is yours from the moment it lands.** The options are ordinary options, edited on the canvas, reordered, added to, removed and bulk edited like any other list. The question wording is not written for you: you type that, as on every question. The hint inside a typed box comes with the question type and stays as it is.

**Contact details stay off a shared report.** Answers to [Name](/questions/ready-made/name), [Mobile phone](/questions/ready-made/mobile-phone), [Email address](/questions/ready-made/email-address) and [Address](/questions/ready-made/address) are held back on every [report](/results/reports) link, with no setting to change. The plain [Email](/questions/text/email) and [Phone](/questions/text/phone) questions are held back the same way; the other ready-made questions appear like any other.

**Results already know what they are looking at.** [Country or region](/questions/ready-made/country-or-region) opens as a **Bar chart**, one bar per answer as typed, where a Short text opens as a word cloud. [Age group](/questions/ready-made/age-group) offers a **Histogram**. The four contact details open as a **Values list**.

**The type is fixed.** The panel's **Type** row reads the name of the question and does not open a picker. Pick the plain question instead when you want that freedom.
