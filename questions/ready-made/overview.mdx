A ready-made question is an ordinary question that arrives with its answers written. Four of them hand respondents a list you did not have to draft: [Gender](/questions/ready-made/gender), [Age group](/questions/ready-made/age-group), [Education level](/questions/ready-made/education-level) and [Marital status](/questions/ready-made/marital-status) behave exactly like [Single choice](/questions/choice/single-choice), options and all. The other six are typed: [Name](/questions/ready-made/name), [Mobile phone](/questions/ready-made/mobile-phone), [Email address](/questions/ready-made/email-address), [Occupation](/questions/ready-made/occupation), [Country or region](/questions/ready-made/country-or-region) and [Address](/questions/ready-made/address) behave like [Short text](/questions/text/short-text), with the same [text limits](/rules/text-limits) and [patterns](/rules/patterns) behind **Validation**. So the question that decides is the one you would ask anyway: pick from a list, or type.

## What the ready-made ones add

**The starter content is yours from the moment it lands.** The options are ordinary options, edited on the canvas, reordered, added to, removed and bulk edited like any other list. The question wording itself is not written for you: you type that, as on every question.

**Contact details stay off a shared report.** Answers to [Name](/questions/ready-made/name), [Mobile phone](/questions/ready-made/mobile-phone), [Email address](/questions/ready-made/email-address) and [Address](/questions/ready-made/address) are held back on every [report](/results/reports) link, with no setting to change. The other six appear like any other question.

**Results already know what they are looking at.** [Occupation](/questions/ready-made/occupation) opens as a word cloud, [Country or region](/questions/ready-made/country-or-region) as a bar chart, [Age group](/questions/ready-made/age-group) offers a histogram, and the contact details open as a values list rather than as a distribution nobody would read.

**The type is fixed.** The panel's **Type** row reads the name of the question and does not open a picker, so a ready-made question is not switched into another kind. Pick the plain question instead when you want that freedom.
