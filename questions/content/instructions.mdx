An instruction block puts a note on a screen and asks for nothing back. Use it to say how a question wants to be answered, to warn people that a section is about to change subject, or to hand them a rule they need to hold in mind for the questions that follow.

{/* screenshot: An instruction block as a respondent sees it, above the questions it applies to. */}

## What it is

The same rich text area a [text block](/questions/content/text-block) is, under a different name. A respondent sees the two identically, and they take the same formatting, the same images and dividers, and the same piped answers.

The difference is what you reach for them for, and that matters when you come back to a long survey and read the canvas. A text block carries the prose of the study: the introduction, the section headers, the explanations. An instruction carries a direction to the person filling it in. Naming the two apart keeps a survey readable to whoever edits it after you.

Like a text block it takes no answer, so it has no **Required** toggle, no **Validation** row and no logic of its own, and it never becomes a column in your results.

## Add it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a gap on the canvas">
    On the **Build** tab, hover between two questions and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Instructional">
    In **Add to survey**, type to filter and click **Instructional**, in the **Content** group. It arrives empty, with the placeholder **Write something…**.

    {/* screenshot: An instruction block on the builder canvas, with the formatting toolbar docked above it. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the instructions">
    Click the block and type. The toolbar appears above it while you are writing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

The panel beside the rail names the block, offers **Preview this instructional** and **Delete**, and sets nothing else. Everything is on the toolbar above the text, and on the `/` menu you get by typing a slash at the start of an empty line.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Text style | **Heading**, **Paragraph**, **Bullet list** and **Numbered list**. A numbered list is the usual shape for a set of steps. |
| **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Link** | The three marks and a link, applied to the selection. |
| Alignment | **Align left**, **Align centre** and **Align right**, per paragraph. |
| **Insert** | The **+** button adds an **Image**, picked from your [media library](/design/media-library), or a **Divider**. |
| **@** | Prints an answer from an earlier screen, so an instruction can name what they told you. See [Piping](/logic/piping). |
