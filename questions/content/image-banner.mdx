An image banner puts one picture on a screen and asks for nothing back. Use it for a product shot the questions that follow are about, a stimulus a respondent has to look at, or a header that sets the tone of a section.

{/* screenshot: An image banner as a respondent sees it, above the questions on the same screen. */}

## What it is

A single picture in a box you size. It carries no caption and no title of its own, so when a picture needs words under it, put a [text block](/questions/content/text-block) above or below it. It takes no answer, so it has no **Required** toggle, no **Validation** row and no logic of its own.

## Add it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a gap on the canvas">
    On the **Build** tab, hover between two questions and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Image">
    In **Add to survey**, type to filter and click **Image**, in the **Content** group. It arrives as an empty box, and the panel says **Add the image directly on the canvas block.**

    {/* screenshot: An empty image banner on the builder canvas, showing the Add image slot. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the image">
    Click **Add image** on the box. The picker opens on three tabs: **Library**, everything your organization has already uploaded, **Upload**, for a new file, and **Link**, for an address you paste. Uploading is on a paid plan. A link works on every plan. Hovering a picture you have already chosen offers **Replace** and **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

Select the block on the canvas and the panel beside the rail shows everything you can set.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Aspect ratio** | The shape of the box: **Original**, **16:9**, **4:3**, **1:1 (Square)** or **3:1 (Wide)**. |
| Size | Drag the right edge of the box to set its width, from a fifth of the width available up to all of it. On **Original**, the bottom edge drags too and sets the height. Any other ratio works the height out from the width, so only the right edge is live. |
| **Fit** | How the picture fills the box: **Cover** crops it to fill, **Contain** fits the whole of it in, **Fill** stretches it. |
| **Focus point** | Drag the dot to the part that must stay in frame. It decides what survives when **Cover** crops. |
| **Alt text** | What a screen reader says in place of the picture. The box reads **Describe the image**. |

**Fit**, **Focus point** and **Alt text** appear once there is a picture to apply them to.
