A text block puts words on a screen and asks for nothing back. Use it for an introduction, an explanation between two sections, or a paragraph of context a respondent needs before the next question makes sense.

{/* screenshot: A text block as a respondent sees it, above the question it introduces. */}

## What it is

One rich text area, edited in place on the canvas. It holds headings, paragraphs, bulleted and numbered lists, links, images and dividers, and it can print an answer from an earlier screen. It takes no answer, so it has no **Required** toggle, no **Validation** row and no logic of its own, and it never becomes a column in your results.

It is the same editor the welcome screen and every end screen use for their own text, so anything you can write in one you can write in the others.

## Add it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a gap on the canvas">
    On the **Build** tab, hover between two questions and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Content">
    In **Add to survey**, type to filter and click **Content**, in the **Content** group. It arrives empty, with the placeholder **Write something…**.

    {/* screenshot: A text block on the builder canvas, with the formatting toolbar docked above it. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the text">
    Click the block and type. The toolbar appears above it while you are writing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

The panel beside the rail names the block, offers **Preview this content** and **Delete**, and sets nothing else. Everything a text block can do is on the toolbar above the text, and on the `/` menu you get by typing a slash at the start of an empty line.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Text style | **Heading**, **Paragraph**, **Bullet list** and **Numbered list**, from the dropdown at the left of the toolbar or from the `/` menu. The two list buttons on the bar, **Bulleted list** and **Numbered list**, do the same as the last two entries. |
| **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline** | The three marks, applied to whatever you have selected. |
| **Link** | Turns the selection into a link and asks for the address. |
| Alignment | **Align left**, **Align centre** and **Align right**. It applies to the paragraph the caret is in, so one block can align its parts differently. |
| **Insert** | The **+** button adds an **Image**, picked from your [media library](/design/media-library), or a **Divider**. |
| **@** | Prints an answer from an earlier screen. See [Piping](/logic/piping). |
