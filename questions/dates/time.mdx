Time asks for a clock time and nothing else. Respondents pick the hour and the minute from two dropdowns, with an **AM** and **PM** dropdown beside them unless you switch the question to a 24-hour clock. The picker opens at 09:00, and the answer is stored once they set it.

{/* screenshot: A time question as a respondent sees it, an hour dropdown, a minute dropdown and AM or PM. */}

## When to use it

- The day is already known or does not matter: what time they usually eat, start work, or log in.
- You are collecting a preferred slot rather than a dated appointment.
- You want the answers charted hour by hour, which is what this question gives you without any setup.

**Consider instead:** [Date and time](/questions/dates/date-and-time) when the day matters as much as the hour, [Time range](/questions/dates/time-range) when the answer is a window with two ends, [Single choice](/questions/choice/single-choice) when named slots such as Morning, Afternoon and Evening are all you need.

## Add it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a gap on the canvas">
    On the **Build** tab, hover between two questions and click **Add**. A gap sits above the first question, between every pair, and after the last one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Time">
    In **Add to survey**, type to filter and click **Time**, under the **Date & Time** heading. The question lands where the gap was, already showing its dropdowns and with no content to clear out first.

    {/* screenshot: A time question on the builder canvas, edited in place. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the question">
    The canvas is the real survey, so you type the question and its description straight onto it. There is no option list here: the dropdowns are the answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

Select the question on the canvas and the panel beside the rail shows everything you can set.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **24-hour clock** | Off, the hour list runs 01 to 12 with an **AM** and **PM** dropdown beside it. On, the hour list runs 00 to 23 and the AM and PM dropdown goes away. |
| **Minute step** | How far the minute list advances: **1**, **5**, **10**, **15** or **30**. At 15 the list is 00, 15, 30 and 45, which is four rows to scan instead of sixty. |
| **Type** | Switches the question to another kind. The other five date and time questions are in the list. A switch that would drop something asks first. |

Below those the panel holds **Required** and the three logic rows every question has: **Show/hide question**, **Skip from here** and **Set a variable**.

{/* screenshot: The question panel for a time question, with the 24-hour clock and minute step rows. */}

## Rules you can set

| Rule | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Required](/rules/required) | They cannot move on until they set a time. |

Required is the whole of it. Every time the dropdowns can produce is a real time of day, so there is nothing left to constrain, and a time question has no **Validation** row in the panel. **Minute step** does the work a rule would otherwise do: set it to 15 and no answer can arrive at 09:07. If you need the answer to fall inside a window, use [Time range](/questions/dates/time-range) for the window itself, or split the day into named slots with [Single choice](/questions/choice/single-choice).

## Logic that works with it

The answer is one time of day. Conditions read it the way they read a date: **is**, **is not**, **is after**, **is before**, **is between**, **is answered** and **is not answered**, so "is after 18:00" is a rule you can write. It cannot be a [carry-forward](/logic/carry-forward) source or target, because there is no list of answers to hand on. It can [drive a loop](/logic/loops), which gives one pass for the time they gave. It does not [score](/logic/scoring): points are typed next to an answer you wrote, and this question has no answers to write them against, so it has no **Scoring** row. [Piping](/logic/piping) it into later wording prints the clock time, as 14:30. Like any other [condition](/logic/how-logic-runs) source it has to sit on an earlier screen than the rule that reads it.

## How it shows in results

Per question the default is a **Timeline**, which for a time of day is one bar per hour, labeled 09:00, 10:00 and so on, in clock order rather than in size order. Only the hours somebody answered appear. **Bar chart** and **Values list** are the other two entries in **Chart type**.

In every export the question becomes one column, holding the time that was given.
