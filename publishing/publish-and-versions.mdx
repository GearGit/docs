Publishing is the action that makes your survey available to respondents. This page explains what happens when you publish, how the versioning system works, and how to review the history of changes across versions.

## What Happens When You Publish

When you click **Publish**, OnlineSurvey compiles your current draft into an immutable, version-pinned snapshot. That snapshot is what respondents see and fill in. The draft you work on in the builder is kept separate, so you can continue editing at any time without disturbing the live survey.

Each publish action creates a new numbered version: Version 1 the first time, Version 2 the next, and so on. The version number appears in the Publish panel so you always know which snapshot is currently live.

![The Publish panel showing the current draft ready to be published as Version 1](/images/placeholder.png)

<Note>
  Publishing is instant. As soon as you click Publish, new respondents who open your share link will see the freshly compiled version.
</Note>

## How to Publish Your Survey

<Steps>

  ### Open the Publish Panel

  From the builder, click **Publish** in the top-right toolbar. The Publish panel slides in on the right side of the screen and shows a summary of your current draft, including the question count and any active logic rules.

  ![The Publish panel open on the right side of the builder canvas](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Review the Summary

  Read through the summary to confirm the survey looks correct. If OnlineSurvey detects any issues, such as a branching rule that points to a deleted question, a warning banner will appear here before you proceed.

  ### Confirm the Publish

  Click **Publish Now**. OnlineSurvey compiles the snapshot and assigns it the next version number. The panel updates to confirm the version is live and shows the share link you can copy straight away.

  ![The Publish panel after a successful publish, showing "Version 2 is now live" and the share link](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

## Understanding Version Numbers

Every time you publish, the version counter increments by one. Version numbers are permanent and never reused. If you publish three times, you will have Version 1, Version 2, and Version 3 in your history, regardless of how minor the changes were between them.

The version number is also embedded in the response data, so when you export results you can always tell which snapshot a given response was collected against.

## Respondents and Version Pinning

When a respondent opens your share link, they are assigned to the version that is live at that moment. If you publish a new version while they are mid-survey, they finish on the version they started. Their session is pinned to that snapshot until they submit or abandon it.

This means responses collected before and after a publish are always clearly attributed to a specific version, making it straightforward to compare data across fieldwork periods.

<Tip>
  If you need to run a clean before-and-after comparison, note the response count before you publish the new version. Any responses submitted after that count was recorded belong to the newer version.
</Tip>

## Viewing Version History

<Steps>

  ### Open Version History

  In the Publish panel, click **Version history**. A list appears showing every version you have published, along with the date and time it was published and the team member who triggered it.

  ![The version history list showing three published versions with dates and author names](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Preview a Past Version

  Click on any version in the list to open a read-only preview of that exact snapshot. This lets you see precisely what respondents saw when they filled in that version, which is useful for auditing or resolving disputes about question wording.

  ### Compare Changes

  Next to each version entry, click **What changed** to see a plain-language summary of the differences between that version and the one before it. Added questions, removed questions, and edited question text are each listed separately.

  ![The "What changed" panel showing that one question was added and one answer option was edited between Version 2 and Version 3](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

## What Makes a Version Immutable

Once a version is published, nothing can alter it. The question text, answer options, logic rules, and survey settings are all locked. This protects the integrity of responses already collected. If you need to change any of those things, edit the draft and publish a new version.

<Info>
  The immutability guarantee applies to the snapshot itself. Your share link, passwords, open and close dates, and response caps can still be updated at any time without creating a new version.
</Info>
