Email campaigns let you send your survey directly to a list of contacts without leaving OnlineSurvey. You write the email, pick your contact list, schedule the send, and monitor delivery alongside your response data in one place. This page walks through creating and sending a campaign and explains the metrics available after the send.

## Email Allowances by Plan

The number of emails you can send each calendar month depends on your plan.

| Plan | Monthly Email Allowance |
|------|------------------------|
| Free | Not included |
| Basic | 1,200 emails per month |
| Pro | 5,000 emails per month |
| Custom | Set by agreement |

<Note>
  The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month. If you need to send more emails in a single month than your plan allows, consider upgrading or contacting [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) about a Custom plan.
</Note>

## Create an Email Campaign

<Steps>

  ### Open the Distribute Tab

  Open your survey in the editor and click **Distribute** in the top navigation bar. Then click **Email campaigns** in the left panel.

  ![The Distribute tab with Email campaigns selected in the left navigation panel](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Create a New Campaign

  Click **New campaign**. A campaign setup form opens. Give the campaign a descriptive internal name so you can tell it apart from other campaigns in the list.

  ### Select a Contact List

  In the **Contact list** field, choose an existing contact list from the dropdown, or click **Upload a new list** to import a CSV file. The list must contain at minimum one column with a header of `email`.

  If you need to create or manage contact lists before setting up a campaign, see [Contact Lists and Mail Merge](/distributing/contact-lists-and-mail-merge).

  ![The campaign setup form with the Contact list dropdown open showing two saved lists](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Write the Subject Line

  Type a subject line in the **Email subject** field. Keep it concise and specific: tell recipients what the survey is about and, if relevant, how long it takes. A clear subject line improves open rates.

  ### Write the Email Body

  Click into the **Email body** editor and write your message. Explain to recipients why you are reaching out, what the survey covers, and approximately how long it takes to complete.

  When you are ready to include the survey link, place your cursor where the link should appear and click **Insert survey link**. This inserts a `{{survey_link}}` token into the body. At send time, OnlineSurvey replaces the token with each recipient's unique, trackable survey URL.

  You can also personalise the email using mail-merge tokens drawn from your contact list columns. For example, type `{{first_name}}` to insert each recipient's first name. See [Contact Lists and Mail Merge](/distributing/contact-lists-and-mail-merge) for full details.

  ![The email body editor with the survey link token and a first name merge token visible in the text](/images/placeholder.png)

  <Warning>
    Every campaign email must contain the `{{survey_link}}` token. If you send without it, recipients will receive the email but will have no way to open the survey. OnlineSurvey will warn you if the token is missing when you try to schedule or send.
  </Warning>

  ### Choose a Sender Name and Address

  In the **From name** field, type the name you want recipients to see as the sender, for example the name of your organisation or research team. The sending address is managed in your account settings.

  ### Schedule or Send

  Choose when to send the campaign.

  - Click **Send now** to dispatch the campaign immediately to all recipients in the list.
  - Click **Schedule send** to pick a future date and time. Use the date picker to select the date and set the hour and minute. The time is shown in your local time zone.

  ![The scheduling section with a future date and time selected and the Schedule send button highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Confirm the Send

  Review the summary: recipient count, subject line, scheduled time, and the email allowance remaining on your plan. Click **Confirm** to finalise.

</Steps>

## Monitoring Campaign Delivery

After a campaign sends, click its name in the campaigns list to open the delivery dashboard. The dashboard refreshes in real time and shows aggregate metrics at the top.

![The campaign delivery dashboard showing send, open, click, and bounce counts as a summary row](/images/placeholder.png)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Send" icon="paper-plane">
    The total number of emails dispatched. This should match the size of your contact list, minus any addresses that were flagged as duplicates or invalid before sending.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opened" icon="envelope-open">
    The number of recipients who opened the email, tracked via a transparent pixel. Open tracking requires images to be enabled in the recipient's email client.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clicked" icon="arrow-pointer">
    The number of recipients who clicked the survey link in the email. A click indicates the recipient opened the survey, regardless of whether they completed it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bounced" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The number of emails that could not be delivered. Hard bounces indicate a permanently invalid address. Soft bounces indicate a temporary issue such as a full inbox. See the [Delivery Tracking](/distributing/delivery-tracking) page for details on bounce types.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Below the summary, a per-recipient table shows the status of every contact in the list alongside their survey disposition: completed, screened out, quota full, or not yet started. You can filter by any status and resend to non-openers directly from this view. Full details are available on the [Delivery Tracking](/distributing/delivery-tracking) page.
