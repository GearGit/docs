The share link is the simplest way to get your survey in front of respondents. OnlineSurvey generates a unique URL the moment you publish, and you can distribute it anywhere: email, messaging apps, social media, or printed materials. This page covers how to copy the link, protect it with a password, schedule access, cap the number of responses, and generate a QR code.

## Where to Find Your Share Link

After publishing, open the **Distribute** tab at the top of the survey editor. The Share Links section is the first panel you see. Your link is displayed prominently and can be copied with one click.

![The Distribute tab with the Share Links panel open, showing the survey URL and a Copy button](/images/placeholder.png)

<Note>
  The share link points to the latest published version. If you publish a new version, the same link automatically routes new visitors to the updated snapshot. You do not need a new link after each publish.
</Note>

## Copy the Link

<Steps>

  ### Open the Distribute Tab

  Click **Distribute** in the top navigation bar of the survey editor. The Share Links panel loads automatically.

  ### Copy the Link

  Click **Copy link**. The URL is copied to your clipboard and ready to paste wherever you need it.

  ![The Share Links panel with the Copy link button highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

## Add a Password

Protect your survey from unintended respondents by requiring a password. Respondents see a password prompt before the welcome screen loads.

<Steps>

  ### Enable Password Protection

  In the Share Links panel, toggle on **Password protect**. A text field appears.

  ### Set the Password

  Type the password you want respondents to use. There are no complexity requirements, but choose something memorable if you plan to share it verbally or in print.

  ![The Share Links panel with the Password protect toggle enabled and a password typed in the field](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Save the Setting

  Click **Save**. The password takes effect immediately for anyone who opens the link from this point on.

</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can change or remove the password at any time without publishing a new version. The change applies to new visitors immediately.
</Tip>

## Schedule an Open and Close Date

Control exactly when your survey accepts responses by setting a scheduled open date, a scheduled close date, or both.

<Steps>

  ### Open the Scheduling Section

  In the Share Links panel, expand **Scheduling**.

  ### Set the Open Date

  Toggle on **Open on a specific date** and pick a date and time using the date picker. Before that date and time, anyone who opens the link will see a "survey not yet open" message.

  ### Set the Close Date

  Toggle on **Close on a specific date** and pick a date and time. After that date and time, anyone who opens the link will see a "survey is now closed" message.

  ![The Scheduling section with both an open date and a close date selected](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Save the Settings

  Click **Save**. The schedule is active immediately.

</Steps>

<Info>
  All dates and times are stored in UTC. OnlineSurvey displays them in your browser's local time zone for convenience. If your team spans multiple time zones, confirm the UTC equivalent before finalising the schedule.
</Info>

## Set a Response Cap

Limit the total number of responses the survey accepts. Once the cap is reached, new visitors see a "survey is now closed" message.

<Steps>

  ### Open the Response Cap Section

  In the Share Links panel, expand **Response cap**.

  ### Enable the Cap

  Toggle on **Limit responses** and enter the maximum number you want to collect.

  ![The Response cap section with the limit set to 500 responses](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Save the Setting

  Click **Save**. The cap is active immediately and the current response count is shown next to the limit so you can monitor progress.

</Steps>

## Generate a QR Code

A QR code encodes your share link as a scannable image, useful for printed materials, event posters, packaging inserts, or on-screen displays.

<Steps>

  ### Open the QR Code Section

  In the Share Links panel, expand **QR code**.

  ### Download the Code

  Click **Download QR code**. OnlineSurvey generates a high-resolution PNG of the QR code and saves it to your downloads folder.

  ![The QR code section showing a generated QR code with a Download button below it](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

<Tip>
  Test the QR code by scanning it yourself before printing. Make sure the destination survey loads correctly and that any password prompt you have set is clearly communicated near the code.
</Tip>
