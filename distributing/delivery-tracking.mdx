Delivery tracking gives you a per-recipient view of how your email campaign performed and what each contact did after receiving your survey. This page explains the statuses you will see, how to filter and act on them, and how to interpret bounce types. Delivery tracking is available on the Pro plan.

<Info>
  Delivery tracking is a Pro plan feature. If you are on a Basic or Free plan, you will see aggregate send, open, click, and bounce counts but not the per-recipient breakdown. Upgrade to Pro to unlock the full per-recipient table and resend functionality.
</Info>

## Opening the Delivery Tracking View

<Steps>

  ### Go to Email Campaigns

  In the survey editor, click **Distribute**, then click **Email campaigns** in the left panel.

  ### Open a Campaign

  Click the name of a campaign that has already been sent. The campaign detail page opens with aggregate metrics at the top and the per-recipient table below.

  ![The campaign detail page showing summary metrics at the top and a scrollable per-recipient table below](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

## Per-Recipient Statuses

Each row in the per-recipient table shows one contact from your list alongside their current status. Statuses update in real time as events occur.

<Accordion title="Sent">
  The email was accepted by OnlineSurvey's sending infrastructure and dispatched to the recipient's mail server. This status does not confirm the email reached the inbox; it confirms it left the system without an immediate error.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Opened">
  The recipient opened the email. Open tracking is based on a transparent tracking pixel loading in the email client. This status may undercount opens for recipients who have image loading disabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Clicked">
  The recipient clicked the survey link in the email. A click is a stronger engagement signal than an open and means the recipient at least viewed the survey landing page or welcome screen.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bounced">
  The email could not be delivered. See the Bounce Types section below for an explanation of hard and soft bounces and what action to take for each.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Completed">
  The recipient clicked the link and submitted a complete response. Their disposition in OnlineSurvey is marked as Complete.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Screened Out">
  The recipient clicked the link and triggered a screen-out rule during the survey. Their response is recorded in OnlineSurvey with a Screened Out disposition.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Quota Full">
  The recipient clicked the link but reached a quota that had already met its target. Their response is recorded with a Quota Full disposition.
</Accordion>

## Filtering by Status

<Steps>

  ### Open the Filter Panel

  Above the per-recipient table, click **Filter by status**. A dropdown appears listing all available statuses.

  ![The per-recipient table with the Filter by status dropdown open and "Opened" selected](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Select a Status

  Click the status you want to view. The table updates instantly to show only recipients with that status. You can select multiple statuses at once by clicking each one in the dropdown.

  ### Clear the Filter

  Click **Clear filter** or deselect all statuses in the dropdown to return to the full recipient list.

</Steps>

## Resending to Non-Openers

If a segment of your list has not opened the email a few days after the initial send, you can send a follow-up to that group directly from the delivery tracking view.

<Steps>

  ### Filter to Non-Openers

  Apply the **Sent** status filter to show all recipients whose email was delivered but who have not yet opened it.

  ![The per-recipient table filtered to "Sent" showing recipients who have not yet opened](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Select Recipients

  Use the checkbox in the table header to select all filtered recipients, or check individual rows to resend to a specific subset.

  ### Resend the Campaign

  Click **Resend to selected**. A confirmation dialog appears showing the count of recipients and the email subject. You can edit the subject line for the follow-up if you want to differentiate it from the original, for example by adding "Reminder:" at the start.

  Click **Confirm resend**. The follow-up emails are queued and dispatched. Each resend counts toward your monthly email allowance.

  ![The Resend confirmation dialog showing 47 selected recipients and an editable subject line field](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

<Tip>
  Wait at least 48 to 72 hours after the initial send before resending to non-openers. Some recipients open emails several days after receiving them, and resending too soon may result in duplicates reaching people who were planning to respond.
</Tip>

## Bounce Types

When an email bounces, the per-recipient table shows a bounce type label in the status column. Understanding bounce types helps you maintain a clean contact list.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Hard Bounce" icon="circle-xmark">
    A permanent delivery failure. The email address does not exist, the domain is invalid, or the recipient's mail server has permanently rejected delivery. Remove hard-bounced addresses from your contact lists promptly. Repeatedly sending to hard-bounce addresses can damage your sending reputation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Soft Bounce" icon="rotate">
    A temporary delivery failure. Common causes include a full inbox, a temporarily unavailable mail server, or a message size limit on the recipient's server. OnlineSurvey automatically retries soft-bounce addresses for up to 72 hours. If the message cannot be delivered within that window, the status changes to a final bounce.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

![The per-recipient table with a "Hard bounce" and a "Soft bounce" label visible in the status column](/images/placeholder.png)

<Warning>
  A high bounce rate, typically above 5%, can cause future campaigns to be deprioritised or blocked by receiving mail servers. Before uploading a large contact list, validate it using an email verification service to remove stale or invalid addresses.
</Warning>

## Exporting the Tracking Data

To export the full per-recipient tracking table, click **Export tracking data** above the table. OnlineSurvey generates a CSV file containing every recipient, their delivery status, open timestamp, click timestamp, and survey disposition. This file is useful for reporting to stakeholders or for identifying contacts to follow up with outside of OnlineSurvey.
