Support is one address, support@onlinesurvey.ai, read by people who can open your organization with you and see what you see.

{/* screenshot: The support session strip across the top of the app, reading Support session as, with Exit at its end. */}

## What it does

**One address.** Every support line in the app leads to support@onlinesurvey.ai. In **Compare plans**, **Talk to us** under a plan and **Talk to us about Enterprise** open a message to it. On a managed agreement, **Email support** on **Plan and billing** does the same. The error page's "if it persists, contact support" means the same address. There is no other channel.

**What we can do.** Clear a publish that is stopped on our side, look at a response that does not read right, move your organization to another plan, and, with your permission, open your organization as you to see exactly what you are seeing.

**Support access.** When you agree to it, one of us starts a support session on your account. You get an email saying who opened it, when, why and until when. You see a strip across the top of the app reading "Support session as {email}. Ends at {time}." with **Exit** at its end. The session also appears on your account page under **Active sessions** with a **Support access** badge, where **Sign out** ends it. Anything done during the session is recorded in **Audit** as "{operator}, support, acting for {actor}" with a **Support access** tag, so the trail always says who did what.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Common issues first">
    The messages the app shows most often, and what each one means, are on [Common issues](/help/common-issues). Many of them have a way through that does not need us.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather what we will ask for">
    The survey's address from your browser while it is open in the builder, or its respondent link from **Copy link** on **2 Publish**. The name of your organization, which is the sidebar's header. What you clicked and what you saw, in order. The exact sentence on the screen, and a screenshot of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write to support@onlinesurvey.ai">
    Send it from the address you sign in with, so we find your organization straight away. If a publish is stopped, the details already say what to send: the survey link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Support access if we ask">
    While the session runs, the strip stays on screen. Click **Exit** on the strip, or **Sign out** on that row under **Active sessions**, and the session ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**Your plan and your payment are handled by admins.** If you are a member and the question is about a limit, a seat or a charge, an organization admin has to raise it or make the change. The **Members** page names your **Organization admin**. See [Billing](/account/billing).

**A support session is time-limited and fenced.** It ends at the time the strip states, whether or not anyone clicks **Exit**, and clicking around does not extend it. During it we cannot change your password or your name, sign out your other sessions, move money on **Plan and billing**, or delete the organization.

**Nothing about your data changes because you asked.** Writing to us starts a conversation. The only way we touch your organization is a support session you agreed to, and that session leaves its own lines in **Audit**.
