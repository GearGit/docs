OnlineSurvey support is handled by a real team who reads every message. This page explains how to get in touch, what information to include so the team can help you quickly, and where to send security or privacy requests.

## How to Reach Support

The primary support channel is email.

<Tip>
  Email the support team at **[support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai)**. A person reads every message, usually within one business day.
</Tip>

There is no chatbot. When you email support, a member of the team reads your message and replies directly. Pro and Custom plan users receive priority handling, meaning their messages are addressed before the general queue.

## What to Include in Your Message

The more context you include upfront, the faster the support team can help. When writing your message, try to include the following.

**Your survey name.** Include the exact name of the survey you are asking about. If you are asking about multiple surveys, name each one.

**A description of the issue.** Explain what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A clear before-and-after description is more helpful than a general statement like "it isn't working."

**Any error messages you saw.** If the platform displayed an error message, copy the exact text into your email. A screenshot attached to the email is also helpful.

**The steps to reproduce the issue.** If the problem happens consistently when you follow certain steps, list those steps. This helps the team reproduce the issue on their end.

Here is an example of a well-formed support message:

> Survey name: Q3 Brand Tracker
>
> Issue: The branching rule on question 5 is not redirecting respondents who answer "No" to question 8. Instead, they are proceeding to question 6 as if no rule exists.
>
> Steps to reproduce: Open the preview, answer "No" on question 5. Expected: jump to question 8. Actual: proceeds to question 6.
>
> The rule was set up yesterday and worked in preview. It stopped working after I added a new question 6 this morning.

## Security and Privacy Requests

Security disclosures, privacy inquiries, and data deletion requests all go to the same address: [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai). Include "Security" or "Privacy" in the subject line so the message is routed to the right person quickly.

For Custom plan customers with a Data Processing Agreement, DPA-related questions can also be directed to this address.
