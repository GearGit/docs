This page covers the most common issues researchers run into while building, running, and analyzing surveys in OnlineSurvey. Expand the issue that matches what you are experiencing to find a resolution.

<Note>
  If none of the issues below match your situation, or you need hands-on help, email [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) with your survey name and a description of the problem.
</Note>

## Common Issues

<Accordion title="My survey is not accepting new responses">
  There are three reasons a survey stops accepting responses.

  **Response cap reached.** Every plan has a monthly response limit. When you hit that limit, new responses stop being recorded until your billing cycle resets or you upgrade. Check your current usage in **Settings > Plan**. If your limit is close to or at its maximum, upgrade your plan or wait for the reset.

  **Survey closed by schedule.** If you set a close date or a maximum response count on the survey itself, the survey closes automatically when that threshold is reached. Open the survey in the builder, go to **Publish Settings**, and check the **Close conditions** section. Remove or adjust the condition to reopen the survey.

  **Monthly account limit hit.** Even if the survey has no close condition, your account's monthly response ceiling applies across all surveys. See above for how to address this.

  ![The Publish Settings panel showing close conditions with a date and response count field](/images/placeholder.png)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A logic rule is not working as expected">
  Logic rules in OnlineSurvey follow two important principles: they are **forward-only** and they operate on a **first-match-wins** basis.

  **Forward-only** means a rule can only branch a respondent to a question that comes after the question the rule is attached to. You cannot use a rule to send a respondent back to an earlier question.

  **First-match-wins** means that if multiple rules could apply at the same point, only the first matching rule fires. Review your rule list and make sure the rules are ordered from most specific to least specific.

  **The referenced question must appear before the rule.** A display logic or branching rule that references a question's answer will not work if that question appears later in the survey than the rule itself. Reorder questions in the builder to ensure the referenced question comes first.

  To review and reorder rules, open the question in the builder, click the **Logic** tab in the right-hand panel, and check the order of your conditions.

  ![The Logic tab on a question showing a list of rules with drag handles for reordering](/images/placeholder.png)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Respondents are skipping questions I marked required">
  There are two common causes.

  **The required setting is on the wrong element.** In grid and matrix questions, the required rule must be set on the grid as a whole, not on individual rows. Open the question in the builder and confirm that the **Required** toggle in the question settings panel is turned on for the correct element.

  **Display logic is hiding the question.** If a display logic condition causes the question not to appear for some respondents, the required check is bypassed automatically. A hidden question cannot block survey progression. Review any display logic conditions on the question to confirm they are working as intended. If you want all respondents to see and answer the question, remove the display logic condition.

  ![The question settings panel with the Required toggle highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My export file looks different from what I see in the results grid">
  Differences between the export and the on-screen grid usually come down to one of three settings.

  **Codes vs. labels.** The export can output numeric codes (the internal values assigned to each option) or the full text labels that respondents saw. If your export shows numbers instead of text, open the export dialog and switch the **Answer format** setting from **Codes** to **Labels**, or download the data dictionary to map codes to labels.

  **Loop orientation.** For surveys that use loops, each loop iteration can be exported as additional rows or as additional columns. Check the **Loop layout** option in the export dialog and choose the orientation that matches your analysis tool.

  **Export options not matching your view.** The results grid applies any active filters you have set in the analysis view. Your export may or may not include those filters depending on your **Filtered data** setting in the export dialog.

  ![The export dialog showing Answer format, Loop layout, and Filtered data options](/images/placeholder.png)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Quota cells are not filling">
  Quotas work by matching a respondent's answer to a condition you define. If the condition does not match exactly, the respondent is not counted toward that quota cell.

  **Check option labels and codes.** Quota conditions match against the exact answer option labels or codes you specified when setting up the quota. If you have since edited the answer option text in the question, the quota condition no longer matches. Open the quota setup in **Settings > Quotas** and verify that each condition still references the correct option.

  **Check logic conditions.** If the quota uses a multi-condition rule (for example, age range AND region), all conditions must be satisfied simultaneously for the respondent to count. Confirm that each part of the condition is correct and that the question order allows all referenced questions to be answered before the quota is evaluated.

  **Confirm the quota is active.** A quota cell set to "Pause" or "Close" status will not fill further. Review the status column in the quota table.

  ![The Quotas table showing quota cells with their current counts and status indicators](/images/placeholder.png)
</Accordion>
