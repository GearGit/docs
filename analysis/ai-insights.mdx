AI Insights reads your collected responses and produces a structured narrative summary so you spend less time writing up findings and more time acting on them. This page explains when the feature becomes available, how to trigger it, what the output looks like, and how it draws on your AI credit balance.

## When AI Insights Becomes Available

The AI Insights feature becomes active once your survey has collected enough responses to produce statistically meaningful patterns. The exact threshold depends on the number of questions and the complexity of your routing, but as a general guide you will see the **Generate insight** button become active once you have at least 30 complete responses.

If you have fewer than the threshold, the button remains visible but shows a tooltip explaining how many more responses are needed.

<Note>
  AI Insights reads the responses that match your current filter state. If you have a filter applied in the Results view when you trigger an insight, the AI reads only the filtered subset. The insight output will state the base it was generated from.
</Note>

## How to Trigger an Insight

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Results View">
    Go to your survey and click the **Results** tab. Apply any segment or date filters you want the AI to focus on. You can generate insights for your full dataset or for a specific subgroup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate Insight">
    Click the **Generate insight** button in the top-right corner of the Results view. A confirmation dialog appears showing the number of responses the AI will read and the number of AI credits the generation will cost.

    ![The Results view with the Generate insight button highlighted and the confirmation dialog open](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and Wait">
    Click **Confirm** to start the generation. The AI reads all question-level results, open-ended verbatims, and cross-tabulation patterns. Generation typically takes between 15 and 60 seconds depending on survey length and response volume. A progress indicator appears while the AI works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Output">
    When generation is complete, the insight appears as a panel below the Results header. The output is structured in three parts:

    - **Executive summary:** a single sentence that states the most important conclusion from the data.
    - **Key findings:** a numbered list of the strongest patterns the AI identified, each tied to the relevant question and supported by a statistic from the data.
    - **Notable patterns:** observations about subgroup differences, unexpected distributions, or open-ended themes that may warrant further investigation.

    ![The AI Insights panel showing an executive summary sentence followed by a numbered list of key findings](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding the Output

Each finding in the numbered list cites a specific question and a specific number. The AI does not invent figures: every statistic in the output can be verified against the corresponding chart in the Results view.

The executive summary is deliberately concise. It is written to be usable as the opening line of a stakeholder email or a report headline without editing. If the summary does not capture the angle most relevant to your audience, you can copy the key findings and write your own summary around them.

<Tip>
  You can paste the AI Insights output directly into the report builder and edit it as your own narrative. This is often faster than writing a findings section from scratch.
</Tip>

## AI Credits

Each time you generate an insight, the action spends AI credits from your plan's monthly allowance. The cost in credits scales with the number of questions and responses being analyzed, and the confirmation dialog always shows the exact cost before you commit.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free plan" icon="circle">
    10 AI credits per month. Sufficient for one or two short-survey insights.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Basic plan" icon="circle-check">
    250 AI credits per month. Suitable for regular use across several active surveys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro plan" icon="circle-check">
    1,000 AI credits per month. Designed for teams running multiple concurrent studies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom plan" icon="circle-check">
    Credit volume agreed at contract time. Contact your account manager to adjust.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. Unused credits do not carry over. For a full explanation of AI credit costs across all AI features, including survey generation and inline question editing, see the [AI Credits](/ai/how-credits-work) page.

<Warning>
  If your account reaches zero AI credits, the Generate insight button will be disabled until your credits reset or you upgrade your plan. You will not be charged automatically for overages.
</Warning>

## Generating Multiple Insights

You can generate more than one insight for the same survey, for example, one for the full dataset and one for a filtered segment. Each generation is independent and each costs its own credits. Previous insights are saved in the Insights history panel, which you can open by clicking **View past insights** beneath the current insight output.
