The responses grid is your live table of every submission your survey has collected. This page explains how to navigate the grid, search and filter responses, and replay an individual respondent's path through your survey.

## What the Grid Shows

Each row in the grid represents one respondent. Each column represents a question or a piece of metadata such as start time, completion time, and disposition. You can scroll horizontally to see all columns, or use the column picker to show only the fields you care about.

Answers are shown exactly as the respondent gave them, using the option labels that were live at the time of their submission. If you later edit an option label in the survey draft, the grid still shows the original label for any response collected before that change. This keeps your historical data consistent and interpretable.

## Searching the Grid

Use the search bar above the grid to find a specific respondent by any text that appears in their answers. The search runs across all open-ended columns simultaneously. Matching cells are highlighted so you can see at a glance which answers triggered the result.

For a more targeted lookup, click a column header and choose **Search this column** to restrict the search to a single question.

![The search bar above the responses grid with a search term entered](/images/placeholder.png)

## Filtering by Disposition

Every response carries one of four dispositions:

- **Complete:** The respondent reached a valid ending and submitted the survey.
- **Screened Out:** A branching or screen-out rule ended their session before the full survey was completed.
- **Quota Full:** The respondent was turned away because a quota cell they belong to had already reached its target.
- **Dropped Off:** The respondent started but did not finish or submit.

To filter by disposition, open the **Filters** panel and check one or more disposition values. The row count at the top of the grid updates immediately to reflect the active filter.

<Tip>
  Use the **Complete** filter alone when you want to run analysis on clean, finished responses only. Add **Screened Out** back in when you need to audit your screen-out logic.
</Tip>

## Filtering by Date Range and Survey Version

If your survey has been live for a long time, you may want to look at only the responses collected during a specific window. Open the **Filters** panel, expand **Date range**, and set a start date, an end date, or both.

Because OnlineSurvey publishes surveys as immutable, version-pinned snapshots, different batches of respondents may have filled different versions of your questionnaire. Use the **Version** filter to isolate responses collected against a particular published version. This is especially useful when you have changed routing logic or question wording mid-field and want to keep version cohorts separate in your analysis.

## Replaying a Respondent's Full Path

To see every screen a respondent encountered, in the order they encountered it, click anywhere on their row to open the **Response Detail** panel. The detail panel shows each question and answer in sequence, including any questions the respondent skipped because of display logic or branching.

At the top of the detail panel you will see a **path trace**: a numbered list of the pages and logic nodes the respondent passed through. This lets you verify that your routing logic behaved as expected for that individual.

![The response detail panel open on the right side, showing a respondent's full question-and-answer path](/images/placeholder.png)

<Note>
  The path trace is available for all responses collected after the current version was published. Responses collected on earlier versions show the answers recorded but may not include a full path trace if the version pre-dates path logging.
</Note>
