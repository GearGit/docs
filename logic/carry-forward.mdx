**Carry forward** builds a question's answers out of an earlier answer: the options they chose, the ones they did not, or all of them.

<Frame caption="Carry forward: the question to read from, and which of its choices to take.">
  <img src="/images/logic/carry-forward/dialog.png" alt="The Carry forward dialog" />
</Frame>

## What it does

You name a question to carry **From**, then say whether to carry the choices that were **Chosen**, **Not chosen** or **All**. On a grid you also say where they land, its **Rows** or its **Columns**.

The carried list replaces the question's own, so on the canvas it is read-only and carries a link back: **Edit these on Q2**. Edit the wording where it was written, and every question carrying it follows.

The items keep their identity as they travel, so anything already pointing at them keeps working. [Scoring](/logic/scoring) on a carried list is keyed to the source question's options, a [pipe](/logic/piping) into a carried option shows the wording the respondent read, and a condition on the later question compares the same answers.

A chain is allowed. A question can carry from a question that itself carries from a third.

If nothing carries, for example because the respondent chose nothing to carry, the question asks for nothing and is not [Required](/rules/required). A [selection count](/rules/selection-counts) drops to the number that actually arrived, so "at least 3" never traps somebody who only carried two.

## Example

<Frame caption="A carried list on the canvas, read-only, with a link back to where it is written.">
  <img src="/images/logic/carry-forward/canvas.png" alt="Carried options on the canvas with an Edit these on link" />
</Frame>

In the logic showcase, "Which of these have you used in the last month?" feeds three later questions:

- "And which of those do you use most?" takes the ones they **chose** as its options
- "Which of these applied to each channel?" takes the ones they **chose** as its grid **rows**
- "Tell us one thing about each of these" takes the ones they **did not choose** as its grid **columns**

A fourth carries **all** of the items from "Put these in order, most important first".

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Select the later question and click **Carry forward** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the source and the mode">
    Pick the question under **From**, then **Chosen**, **Not chosen** or **All**. On a grid, choose **Rows** or **Columns** under **Into**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Saving adds a [page break](/survey/page-breaks) after the source question if there is not one already, because the answer has to be in before the later screen is built. The dialog tells you before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview with a real answer">
    Pick two or three options upstream and watch them arrive downstream.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

The questions that can be carried **from** are the ones whose answer is a set of choices: [Single choice](/questions/choice/single-choice), [Multiple choice](/questions/choice/multiple-choice), [Dropdown](/questions/choice/dropdown), the image choices, both ranking questions and the ready-made choice questions such as [Gender](/questions/ready-made/gender) and [Age group](/questions/ready-made/age-group).

Lists can be carried **into** those same questions, plus [Constant sum](/questions/ranking/constant-sum), and into a grid's rows or columns.

A scale is a measurement rather than a set of choices, so [Likert](/questions/ratings/likert), the ratings, [NPS](/questions/ratings/nps) and [Yes or no](/questions/choice/yes-or-no) carry nothing in either direction.

Order still applies on top. A carried list can be shuffled, and "Only some" counts up to what carried.
