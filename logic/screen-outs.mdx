A screen-out is [branching](/logic/branching) whose destination is an ending you tagged **Screened out**. The respondent finishes on a screen you wrote, and your numbers keep them apart from the people who qualified.

<Frame caption="End at an ending, picked by the name you gave it.">
  <img src="/images/logic/screen-outs/picker.png" alt="The destination picker showing End at an ending" />
</Frame>

## What it does

There is one way for logic to end a survey: **End at**, naming an ending. The outcome is read off that ending, not off the rule, so a rule and its outcome can never say different things.

Every ending carries one of four outcomes, and each one does something different to your numbers:

| Outcome | Counts toward your plan | Counts toward the survey's response cap | Fills a quota cell | Answers kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completed | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Screened out | Yes | No | No | Yes |
| Quota full | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Quality reject | No | No | No | Yes |

So a screen-out keeps the answers the respondent did give, and holds them back from the cap you set and from every quota cell. Someone holding a reserved [quota](/logic/quotas) slot gives it up when they screen out, so the slot goes to the next person who qualifies.

**Screened out and Quality reject are not the same thing.** Screened out means they never qualified. Quality reject means they did qualify and you are setting the response aside anyway. Keeping them apart is what keeps an incidence rate honest.

A survey can have as many endings as it has outcomes, and several can share one. Write "Sorry, this study is looking for drivers" for one screener and "Thanks all the same" for another, and both can be tagged Screened out.

The logic showcase screens people out twice, both times at the ending named **Not this time**:

- On "Which of these have you used in the last month?", if they include **Phone support**, then **End at Not this time**
- As the **Screening** block ends, if "Which of these best describes you?" is **Just browsing**, then **End at Not this time**

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the ending first">
    In the rail, open **Endings**, click **Add an end screen** and **Pick an outcome**, then choose **Screened out**. Name it and write what these respondents should read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point a rule at it">
    Open **Skip from here** on the screening question, or **When this block ends** on the screening block. Add the condition, then set the destination to **End at** your new ending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the counts">
    Take the screened-out path in a [preview](/design/preview), then look at [Responses](/survey/responses-and-sessions). The row is there, marked Screened out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

Screen out as early as you can. A respondent who answers eight screens before being screened out still counts toward your plan allowance, because you keep their answers.

Respondents are never told which outcome they landed on. They read the ending you wrote, and every ending looks the same to them.

Deleting an ending does not break the rules pointing at it. The builder counts them for you first and tells you what happens: those rules send people to the default end screen instead, where they are counted as Completed.
