A loop repeats a whole block, once per item. Ask about each brand they named, each visit they made, or each of three channels you chose yourself.

{/* screenshot: Loop this block: what to repeat for, and how many times. */}

## What it does

A loop belongs to a block, so the block is the body: everything inside it runs again on every pass.

**Repeat this block for each** offers five drivers:

| Driver | One pass for |
|---|---|
| **For each answer** | Each of an earlier question's answers. **Which of its answers** takes **chosen**, **all** or **not chosen** |
| **Repeat N times** | A number that comes from an earlier question: a number they typed, or the count of choices they picked |
| **A list** | Each item of a list you type, one per line |
| **A variable** | Each item of a **List of text** [variable](/logic/variables) |
| **A number** | A fixed number of passes |

**Loop value** is what one pass is on, and the dialog names it along with what it will be: "An answer picked on Q6", "An item from your list", "1, 2, 3, one per pass". Its help line reads "Changes on every pass". Use it in a [pipe](/logic/piping) to title a question with the thing being asked about, and as a condition source inside the body.

**Stop early when** is an optional condition, checked between passes.

The items are worked out and frozen as the respondent enters the loop. Changing the answer that drives it afterwards, by going back, does not reshape a loop already running.

Question numbers do not multiply. A looped question keeps the number it was authored with, and shows it on every pass.

## Example

{/* screenshot: A looped block is marked as one on the canvas. */}

The logic showcase loops five blocks, one per driver:

- **Per screen** repeats for each screen they chose on "Which screens would you use?", and stops early once the [score](/logic/scoring) reaches 40
- **Per visit** repeats as many times as they ranked items on "Put these in order, most important first"
- **Per channel** repeats over a typed list: Email, Chat, Phone
- **Per topic** repeats over the **Topics** variable
- **Per feature** repeats a fixed three times

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put the questions in a block of their own">
    Everything that should repeat goes in one block. See [blocks and screens](/survey/blocks-and-screens).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Loop this block">
    Select the block and click **Loop this block** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the driver">
    Pick one of the five, then fill in what it needs: a question, a list, a variable or a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name each pass in the questions">
    Inside the body, type `@` in a question title and choose **Loop value**, so every pass says what it is about.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

The questions offered as a driver are the ones whose answer is something a respondent can read back: a choice, a number, a rating, a piece of text. A grid, a [Range slider](/questions/ratings/range-slider), a [Constant sum](/questions/ranking/constant-sum), [Match pairs](/questions/ranking/match-pairs) and the image questions answer with a structure rather than an item, so they are not offered.

**Repeat N times** needs a question that answers with a number or with a list of choices. The dialog says so when there is not one yet: "Add a question that answers with a number, or with a list of choices, before this block."

Looped answers come out of an [export](/results/exports) either way. **Looped questions** offers **Wide (columns)**, a column per pass, and **Long (rows)**, a row per pass.
