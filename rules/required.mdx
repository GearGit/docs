**Required** is a single toggle on the question. Turn it on and a respondent cannot leave the screen until that question is answered.

<Frame caption="Required sits in the question panel, above the row that opens Validation.">
  <img src="/images/rules/required/toggle.png" alt="The Required toggle in the question panel" />
</Frame>

## What it does

A required question has to be answered before the screen is submitted. The check runs on our servers on every submit, so it holds however the answer arrives.

What counts as answered depends on the question:

- A grid needs every row answered. A [Dropdown grid](/questions/grids/dropdown-grid) needs every cell.
- A ranking question needs every item placed, and [Match pairs](/questions/ranking/match-pairs) needs every item on the left paired.
- An "Other" option needs the text box beside it filled in. Picking Other and typing nothing reads as a half answer, because the text is the answer.
- Everything else needs a value that is not blank.

Two cases where a required question does not block anyone, both on purpose:

- A question hidden by [show and hide](/logic/show-and-hide), or sitting in a block nobody entered, is not validated at all. A hidden required question must never stop a respondent.
- A question whose options are [carried forward](/logic/carry-forward) and has nothing to carry is not required. There is nothing on screen to pick.

Leave a question optional and respondents can also un-answer it. Clicking a selected rating again clears it, and they can move on with it blank.

The default message is "This field is required." To write your own, see [Custom messages](/rules/custom-messages).

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the question">
    Click the question on the canvas. The scope panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Required">
    Under **validation**, switch **Required** on. The canvas marks the question straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Open a preview and press Next without answering. The message appears under the question, and the screen does not advance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which questions have it

Every question takes it, whatever family it belongs to: text, choice, ratings, grids, ranking, images, dates and the ready-made questions.

Content blocks take no answer, so they have no toggle: [Text block](/questions/content/text-block), [Image banner](/questions/content/image-banner), [Video](/questions/content/video) and [Instructions](/questions/content/instructions).

[Consent](/questions/content/consent) is the exception in the other direction. It is always required, and on a welcome screen it gates the Start button. A respondent who has not ticked it sees "You must agree to continue."
