**Custom error** replaces the sentence a respondent reads when their answer is refused. It is the last field in the **Validation** dialog, under **message**.

{/* screenshot: Custom error sits at the bottom of the Validation dialog, across its full width. */}

## What it does

Leave it blank and each rule uses its own default: "This field is required.", "Must be at least 20 characters.", "Please select at least 2 options.", and so on.

Type something and that sentence is used instead, for whichever rule the answer broke.

**There is one message per question, not one per rule.** A question with a minimum length, a pattern and a required toggle has three ways to fail and one sentence to explain them, so write a sentence that covers the question as a whole. "Enter a whole number of visits, up to 60." works for a number question with a range and a whole-number rule. "Must be at least 20 characters." does not, if the question can also fail for being blank.

**It replaces the wording and nothing else.** The rule itself is unchanged, and so is the reason recorded behind the scenes, so a custom sentence never affects what your results say about why an answer was refused.

The rest of what a respondent reads is fixed: the Next and Back buttons, the progress bar, and the message shown when a page cannot be loaded. Those are not editable.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Validation">
    Select the question, then click the **Validation** row in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the sentence">
    In **Custom error**, type the wording you want. The field is a full-width box, because a sentence needs the room. Its placeholder reads "Shown when this answer is invalid".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Break the question on purpose">
    In a preview, give the question an answer it must refuse, and read your own sentence back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which questions have it

Every question with at least one rule to fail, which is every question that takes an answer. The field is in the same **Validation** dialog whatever the family: text, numbers, choice, grids, dates, ranking and images.

[Text block](/questions/content/text-block), [Image banner](/questions/content/image-banner), [Video](/questions/content/video) and [Instructions](/questions/content/instructions) take no answer, so they have no Validation dialog and no message.

[Consent](/questions/content/consent) always reads "You must agree to continue."
