Four rules bound the length of a typed answer: **Min characters**, **Max characters**, **Min words** and **Max words**. They live in the **Validation** dialog, under **length**.

<Frame caption="The length rules on a long text question, in the Validation dialog.">
  <img src="/images/rules/text-limits/dialog.png" alt="Min characters, Max characters, Min words and Max words in the Validation dialog" />
</Frame>

## What it does

Each of the four is its own rule, and each has its own message:

| Rule | What a respondent sees when it fails |
|---|---|
| Min characters | Must be at least 20 characters. |
| Max characters | Must be at most 500 characters. |
| Min words | Enter at least 3 words. |
| Max words | Enter at most 5 words. |

Set any one of them on its own, or combine them. A question with a minimum but no maximum is fine.

Counting follows what the respondent can see. Spaces at the start and end of the answer are ignored, and invisible characters do not count toward a minimum. Words are whatever is separated by spaces, so "New York" is two words.

Characters are checked before words, and one message shows at a time. If an answer is both too short and one word, the character message is the one they read.

The rules are checked on our servers on every submit, not only in the browser.

Nothing else shortens the answer. It is stored in full, shows in full in the Responses grid, and comes out in full in CSV and Excel [exports](/results/exports). The one exception is the SPSS `.sav` format, which cannot hold more than 255 bytes in a text column and shortens anything longer.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Validation">
    Select the question, then click the **Validation** row in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the bounds you want">
    Fill in any of **Min characters**, **Max characters**, **Min words** or **Max words**. Clear a field to remove that rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it your own wording, if you want one">
    **Custom error** at the bottom of the dialog replaces the default sentence. See [Custom messages](/rules/custom-messages).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which questions have it

Every question whose answer is typed text:

- [Short text](/questions/text/short-text) and [Long text](/questions/text/long-text)
- [Email](/questions/text/email), [Phone](/questions/text/phone) and [URL](/questions/text/url)
- The ready-made text questions: [Name](/questions/ready-made/name), [Mobile phone](/questions/ready-made/mobile-phone), [Email address](/questions/ready-made/email-address), [Occupation](/questions/ready-made/occupation), [Country or region](/questions/ready-made/country-or-region) and [Address](/questions/ready-made/address)

[Number](/questions/text/number) is not one of them. A typed number is bounded by its value, not its length, so it takes [number bounds](/rules/number-bounds) instead.
