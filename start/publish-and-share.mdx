Publishing takes a copy of your survey, numbers it, and freezes it. That frozen copy is what respondents answer, and your link starts working the moment it exists.

<Frame caption="The Publish tab: the live version, the respondent link, and the Share card.">

</Frame>

## What it does

**2 Publish** on the lifecycle strip is where a survey goes live. Before your first publish it reads **Publish your survey**, and the respondent link is listed as inactive. Afterwards it reads **Your survey is live**, and the step in the strip changes from **Publish** to **Published** once the live version matches what you have in the editor.

Publishing does not stop you editing. The draft stays yours to change, and respondents keep seeing the published version until you publish again.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Publish tab">
    Click **2 Publish**. **Before you share** states whether the link is active and whether the survey has a scheduled close, and the settings summary beneath it lists the response limit and how repeat responses are treated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish now**. The dialog says: *Publishing opens "your title" for live responses and locks this version. You can keep editing the draft and publish updates later.* Confirm with **Publish as v1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    The **Share** card holds the respondent link with **Copy link** and **Open**, a QR code with **Download PNG**, and an **Embed** snippet in two shapes, **Inline (iframe)** and **Popup button**, behind **Copy snippet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or share it from Distribute">
    **3 Distribute** has the same link and QR code under **Link** and **QR code** tabs, plus a source tag. Type a tag and the link carries `?c=`, so responses from your newsletter and responses from your website are told apart. **New campaign** sends the survey by email to a recipient list. See [Share links](/share/share-links).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish an update">
    Keep editing, then click **Republish**. The banner tells you how many edits are waiting and that respondents will not see them until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**The Publish button only re-arms when something changed for respondents.** Renaming the survey or editing your internal notes does not count as an unpublished change. Editing a question, a rule, the theme or a setting a respondent feels does.

**Responses stay attached to the version they were collected on.** Publishing an update never rewrites what you already have, and anyone who is part way through finishes the version they started.

**Closing is separate from publishing.** On **Settings**, the lifecycle card has **Close survey**: new responses stop, sessions already in progress finish, and you can reopen at any time. A response limit on the same card closes the survey automatically once it is reached.
