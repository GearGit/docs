Responses arrive as they are collected. **4 Responses** is the raw data, **5 Analytics** is what it adds up to, and clicking any response ID replays that one person's run through the survey.

</Frame>

## What it does

**Responses** is a spreadsheet. One row is one session. The scrollable columns are your questions in order, then any variables you set (a score is one of these), then session data such as **Status**, **Started**, **Duration**, **LOI**, **Device**, **Browser**, **OS**, **Location** and **Timezone**. Turn the session columns on and off with **Session data**.

**Status** is the one word for what happened to that person:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | They qualified and reached a Completed ending. |
| **Screened out** | Your own logic ended their run early. |
| **Quota full** | They qualified, but their group was already full. |
| **Quality reject** | Their answers came in too fast to keep. |
| **Partial** | They started, answered, and left without finishing. |
| **In progress** | They are still filling it in. |

The filter above the grid offers **All** and each of those, with the count beside it.

**Analytics** is one page in two halves. **Fieldwork** is who arrived, how far they got, where they dropped off and what devices they came from. **Results** is a chart per question, with the base count under each. **Variables** follows if you have any.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Responses">
    Click **4 Responses**. Rows appear as sessions finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow it down">
    Use **Filters** to pick an outcome, a date range or a response type. **Showing X of Y responses** tells you what the numbers cover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open one response">
    Click an ID. The page rebuilds that session screen by screen: what was shown, what was skipped and why, what they answered, and which rule ended their run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the totals">
    Click **5 Analytics** for the funnel and a chart per question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**Answers are written when a respondent reaches an ending.** Someone who drops off part way has a row, with a status of **Partial**, and no answers in it. That is why a partial row looks empty.

**Test responses are real traffic, not made-up rows.** **Generate test responses** walks your actual logic and writes real sessions, kept apart from your live data and from your plan allowance.

**Preview never creates a response.** Walking your own survey from the builder records nothing at all.

**Date filters run on the start time.** Both ends are included, and the dates are read in UTC.
