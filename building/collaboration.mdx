OnlineSurvey supports real-time collaborative editing, so your whole research team can work on the same survey at the same time without stepping on each other's work. This page explains how to see who else is editing, how edits are merged automatically, and what happens if two people edit the same place at once.

Collaborative editing is available on **Basic** and **Pro** plans. On the Free plan, only one author can edit a survey at a time.

## Seeing Who Else Is Editing

When a teammate opens the same survey as you, their avatar appears in the top bar of the builder. Each active editor is shown as a coloured circle with their initials or profile picture.

On the canvas itself, each editor's position is shown as a coloured caret with a small label displaying their name. As they click, type, or navigate between questions, their caret moves in real time so you always know where they are working.

![The builder top bar showing two teammate avatars and a coloured remote caret visible on a question card on the canvas](/images/placeholder.png)

<Tip>
  If the survey is busy with several editors, you can click any avatar in the top bar to jump the canvas to that editor's current position. Useful when you want to see what a colleague is working on or ask them a question in your team chat.
</Tip>

## How Character-Level Merging Works

OnlineSurvey automatically merges edits at the character level. This means that if two editors are typing in different parts of the same question at the same time, both sets of changes are applied and neither is lost.

For example, if you are editing the question text while a colleague is adding answer choices to the same question, both sets of edits land correctly. You will see your colleague's additions appear as they type, and they will see yours.

![A question card showing two simultaneous edits: one editor updating the question title and another adding an answer choice](/images/placeholder.png)

<Note>
  Character-level merging handles most concurrent editing gracefully. In the rare case of a genuine conflict (two editors deleting the same word at exactly the same moment), OnlineSurvey resolves the conflict automatically and shows a brief notification so you can review the result.
</Note>

## Collaborative Undo

Each editor has their own independent undo history. Pressing **Ctrl+Z** (or **Cmd+Z** on Mac) undoes your own most recent action, not the last action made by anyone in the survey. This means you can safely undo your own mistakes without accidentally reversing a colleague's work.

<Warning>
  Undo is scoped to your editing session. If you close the survey and return later, your undo history for that session is cleared. Edits that were already saved to the survey remain.
</Warning>

## Editing the Draft While Responses Come In

Published surveys are frozen snapshots. Once you publish, respondents fill that version, and their data is tied to it. Your team can continue editing the draft in the builder at any time, and those changes do not affect the live survey or the data being collected.

When you are ready to apply draft changes to a live survey, you publish a new version. Existing responses are kept against the previous version and can be viewed separately in the results view.

![The builder showing a "Published" badge on a survey alongside an indicator that the draft has unpublished changes](/images/placeholder.png)

<Note>
  All collaborative edits apply to the draft, regardless of whether a version of the survey is currently live. You and your teammates are always editing the same draft together.
</Note>

## Leaving Comments for Teammates

You can leave comments on any question to flag a concern, ask a question, or note something for review. To add a comment, hover over a question and click the **Comment** icon that appears on its right edge. Type your note and press **Enter** to post it.

Teammates with access to the survey will see an indicator on the question that has comments. Comments are visible to everyone with edit access and can be resolved once the issue is addressed.

![A question card with a comment thread open on the right, showing two messages and a text input field](/images/placeholder.png)

## Access and Permissions

Collaborative editing is available to any workspace member who has been given edit access to the survey. Survey owners can manage access from the survey's **Share** settings, assigning roles of **Viewer** (can see the builder and results, cannot edit) or **Editor** (can edit the draft and view results).

<Tip>
  For large projects with multiple workstreams, consider assigning different team members as owners of different survey sections and using comments to coordinate handoffs.
</Tip>
