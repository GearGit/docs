Before you publish a survey, OnlineSurvey lets you preview it using the same fill engine your respondents will use. What you see in preview is exactly what they will experience, including question layout, logic paths, validation messages, and your applied theme.

This page explains how to open the preview, how to test branching and logic, and how to share the preview with a colleague for sign-off.

## Opening the Preview

Click the **Preview** button in the top-right corner of the builder. The preview opens in a new tab, rendering your survey from the first question exactly as a respondent would see it.

![The builder with the Preview button highlighted in the top-right corner](/images/placeholder.png)

The preview is always based on the current state of your draft. If you make a change in the builder and want to see it reflected, switch back to the builder, make your edit, and return to the preview tab. Refreshing the preview tab picks up the latest draft.

<Note>
  The preview does not record any responses. Answers you submit while testing are discarded and never appear in your results data.
</Note>

## Switching Between Device Views

The preview window includes a device toggle at the top. Use it to switch between desktop, tablet, and mobile views. This lets you catch layout issues (such as a grid question that is too wide on a small screen) before your survey goes live.

![The preview window with the device toggle showing desktop, tablet, and mobile options, and a mobile view of the survey displayed](/images/placeholder.png)

<Tip>
  Always check the mobile view before publishing, especially for grid questions and image-heavy surveys. A layout that looks clean on desktop can become cramped or require excessive scrolling on a phone.
</Tip>

## Testing Logic Paths

The preview fully executes your logic rules. As you answer questions, branching conditions route you to the correct follow-up questions, display logic shows and hides questions as configured, and screen-out conditions take you to the appropriate ending.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the preview">
    Click **Preview** in the builder. The survey starts at page 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer questions to test a specific path">
    Fill in answers that correspond to the logic path you want to verify. For example, if branching logic routes respondents who answer "No" to a skip, answer "No" and confirm you arrive at the correct question.

    ![The preview showing a skip in action, with a question that would normally appear next missing from the flow](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart to test another path">
    Click **Restart Preview** at the top of the preview window to return to page 1. This resets all answers so you can walk through a different set of responses without closing and reopening the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the logic map as a companion">
    If your survey has complex branching, open the **Logic Map** in the builder (available from the **Logic** tab) alongside the preview. The map gives you a bird's-eye view of all branches so you can plan which paths to test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The preview tests the current draft, not the published version. If your survey is already live and you have made unpublished changes to the draft, the preview will reflect those changes, which respondents are not yet seeing. Use the **Published version** toggle at the top of the preview to switch to the live version if you need to verify what respondents are currently experiencing.
</Warning>

## Checking Validation Messages

To test validation, try advancing past a required question without answering, submitting a number outside a defined range, or entering a malformed email address. The preview displays the same error messages your respondents will see, so you can review the wording and adjust it in the settings panel if needed.

![A preview page showing a validation error message displayed below a question the tester tried to skip](/images/placeholder.png)

## Sharing the Preview with a Colleague

You can share the preview with a stakeholder or colleague for feedback without giving them edit access to the survey.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Share Preview dialog">
    In the preview window, click **Share Preview** in the top bar. A dialog opens with a shareable link.

    ![The Share Preview dialog with a copy link button and an optional expiry setting](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Click **Copy Link**. Send the link by email, Slack, or any other channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an expiry (optional)">
    By default, the preview link is valid for 7 days. Use the expiry dropdown to extend this to 30 days or set a custom date, or shorten it if you want the link to expire sooner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The recipient can view and interact with the survey in the preview but cannot see any results data, edit the survey, or submit responses that are recorded. The link reflects the current draft at the time it is opened, so if you make further edits after sharing, the recipient will see the updated version when they next open the link.

<Tip>
  Use the preview link during a stakeholder review call so both you and the reviewer are looking at the same thing. You can walk through the survey together and make edits in the builder in real time while they watch the preview update.
</Tip>

## What the Preview Cannot Test

The preview covers the full respondent experience for most use cases, but a small number of features behave differently or are unavailable in preview mode.

<Accordion title="Email distribution personalisation">
  If your survey uses personalisation tokens drawn from an email campaign contact list (such as inserting the respondent's first name into a question), the preview replaces those tokens with sample placeholder text rather than real contact data.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Panel handoff screen-outs">
  Screen-out endings that redirect respondents to a panel provider's URL will show the ending page in preview, but the redirect itself will not execute. You will see the ending message but will not be taken to an external URL.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Quota-based routing">
  Quota limits are not enforced in preview mode. You can reach any ending regardless of whether quotas would normally be full.
</Accordion>
