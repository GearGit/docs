OnlineSurvey lets you customise how your survey looks so it feels like a natural extension of your brand. This page covers how to select and apply a theme, customise colours and fonts, upload a logo, and preview the result before you publish.

## Opening the Theme Panel

In the builder, click the **Design** tab at the top of the right-hand panel. The Theme panel opens, showing your current theme settings and a live preview of how changes will appear to respondents.

![The Design tab open in the right-hand panel with the current theme colours visible](/images/placeholder.png)

## Selecting a Theme

OnlineSurvey includes several built-in themes to give you a starting point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme picker">
    In the Design panel, click **Browse Themes**. A gallery of pre-built themes appears, each shown as a small preview card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a theme">
    Click any theme card to apply it to your survey. The canvas updates instantly so you can see how your questions look with the new colours and fonts.

    ![The theme gallery showing several preset options with a selected theme highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise from the base theme">
    Once a theme is applied, you can adjust individual settings without changing the whole theme. Use the colour, font, and logo controls described in the sections below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customising Colours

The colour panel lets you control the key visual areas of your survey independently.

| Colour setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Primary colour** | Button backgrounds, selected option highlights, progress bar fill, and other interactive accents. |
| **Background colour** | The page background behind the survey card. |
| **Card colour** | The surface colour of each question card. |
| **Question text colour** | The colour of question titles and body text. |
| **Answer text colour** | The colour of answer option labels. |

To change any colour, click its swatch and either use the colour picker or type a hex code directly. Changes are reflected on the canvas in real time.

![The colour picker open with a hex code field and a question card updating behind it](/images/placeholder.png)

<Tip>
  If your brand uses a dark background, switch the **Question text colour** and **Answer text colour** to a light tone to maintain readable contrast. The panel displays a contrast ratio indicator to help you meet accessibility guidelines.
</Tip>

## Customising Fonts

Click **Font** in the Design panel to choose a typeface. OnlineSurvey provides a curated selection of Google Fonts covering serif, sans-serif, and slab options. Select a font family for question text and, optionally, a different family for answer option text.

Font size is set to a responsive default that works well on both desktop and mobile. You can adjust the base size in small increments if your chosen typeface reads differently at default sizes.

## Uploading a Logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the logo uploader">
    In the Design panel, scroll to the **Logo** section and click **Upload Logo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Select a PNG, JPG, or SVG file from your device. For best results, use a PNG or SVG with a transparent background. Maximum file size is 2 MB.

    ![The logo upload area with a drag-and-drop zone and a Browse button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the logo position">
    Choose whether the logo appears at the **top** of every survey page, in the **header bar**, or in the **footer**. A preview updates on the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the logo size">
    Use the size slider to scale the logo. Sizes are constrained so the logo does not crowd the question area.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing the OnlineSurvey Watermark

By default, a small "Powered by OnlineSurvey" mark appears at the bottom of every survey. This branding can be removed on **Basic** and **Pro** plans.

To remove it, go to the Design panel, scroll to **Branding**, and turn off the **Show OnlineSurvey branding** toggle.

<Note>
  The branding removal toggle is visible on all plans but can only be activated on Basic and Pro. If you are on the Free plan, upgrade your account first. Visit your account settings or contact [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) to upgrade.
</Note>

## Previewing Your Theme

Every change you make in the Design panel is reflected on the builder canvas in real time. For a full-page view of exactly what respondents will see, click **Preview** in the top navigation bar to open the preview mode.

The preview renders your survey using the same fill engine respondents use, including your theme, logo, and branding settings. You can navigate through all pages and test the layout on simulated mobile and desktop screen sizes using the device toggle at the top of the preview window.

![The preview window with a device toggle switching between desktop and mobile views](/images/placeholder.png)

<Tip>
  Use the preview link to share your theme with a stakeholder for sign-off before publishing. The link is read-only and does not record responses. See [Preview Your Survey](/building/preview) for details.
</Tip>

## Saving a Theme for Reuse

If you want to reuse the same theme across multiple surveys, click **Save as Theme** at the bottom of the Design panel and give the theme a name. It will appear in your **My Themes** section in the theme gallery, available to apply to any survey in your workspace.
