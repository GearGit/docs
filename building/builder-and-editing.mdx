The survey builder is where you design every question in your study. This page covers how the canvas works, how to add and edit questions, how to reorder them, and how multiple authors can work on the same survey at the same time.

## The Builder Canvas

When you open a survey in the builder, each question appears as the same interactive widget your respondents will see. There is no separate "preview mode" to toggle back and forth: every question is rendered through the real fill engine, so you can spot layout or spacing issues the moment you create them.

Clicking any question on the canvas opens its settings panel on the right, where you can change the question text, answer options, validation rules, and display logic without leaving the page.

![The builder canvas showing a question card and its settings panel open on the right](/images/placeholder.png)

## Adding a Question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the question picker">
    Scroll to the bottom of your question list and click the **+ Add Question** button, or click the **+** icon that appears between two existing questions to insert one at a specific position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a question type">
    A panel slides open showing all 50 question types organised into six families: Closed, Scales, Grids, Ranking, Open Ends, and Dates and Times. Click any type to add it to the canvas.

    ![The question type picker showing six family tabs](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your question text">
    The new question is added in edit mode. Type your question text directly into the question field. You can apply bold, italic, and hyperlinks using the small formatting toolbar that appears above the text field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure answer options">
    Depending on the question type, you will see fields for answer choices, scale labels, rows and columns, or other type-specific settings. Fill these in using the settings panel on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click anywhere outside the active question or press **Escape** to confirm your edits. Changes are saved automatically: there is no separate save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Question Text Inline

To edit any existing question, click directly on its text in the canvas. The field becomes editable immediately, so you can retype, correct a typo, or restructure a sentence without opening a separate dialog. Answer choices work the same way: click any option label to edit it in place.

![A question card with its text field active and a cursor visible inside it](/images/placeholder.png)

<Tip>
  You can also use the AI edit feature to rewrite a question from a one-line instruction. Select the question, click the **AI** icon in the settings panel, and describe what you want changed, for example "make this more neutral" or "shorten to under ten words."
</Tip>

## Reordering Questions

To move a question, hover over its left edge until the drag handle (a grid of dots) appears, then click and drag it to the new position. A blue insertion line shows where the question will land when you release.

![The drag handle visible on the left edge of a question card, with a blue line showing the drop position](/images/placeholder.png)

You can also use the **Move up** and **Move down** arrow buttons in the question's settings panel if you prefer keyboard-friendly controls.

## Duplicating and Deleting Questions

To duplicate a question, open its settings panel and click **Duplicate**. The copy appears directly below the original and includes all answer options, validation settings, and display logic.

To delete a question, open its settings panel and click **Delete**. A confirmation prompt appears before the question is removed, since deleting a published question can affect response data.

<Warning>
  Deleting a question that is referenced by branching logic, piping, or carry-forward rules will also remove those rules. Review your logic carefully before deleting.
</Warning>

## Organising Questions into Pages

Surveys can span multiple pages, which creates natural breaks in the respondent's flow. To add a page break, click **+ Add Page** at the bottom of any page. You can drag questions across pages using the same drag handle described above.

Page titles are optional but useful for your own reference inside the builder. Respondents see only a progress bar, not the page titles.

## Real-Time Collaborative Editing

Multiple authors can work on the same survey at the same time. Each collaborator's cursor appears as a coloured caret labelled with their name, so you can see exactly where teammates are working. Edits at the character level are merged automatically, so two people editing different parts of the same question will not overwrite each other.

![The canvas showing two coloured remote cursors from different team members](/images/placeholder.png)

Collaborative editing is available on **Basic** and **Pro** plans. On the Free plan, only one author can edit at a time.

<Note>
  Real-time collaboration applies to the draft. Once a survey is published, respondents fill a frozen version of that draft. Your team can keep editing the draft without interrupting live data collection.
</Note>
