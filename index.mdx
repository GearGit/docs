## Start here

New to OnlineSurvey? Follow these four steps to run your first study.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your survey">
    Open the builder and add your questions, or describe your research goal and let AI draft the survey for you. See [Build your first survey](/getting-started/build-your-first-survey).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add logic">
    Screen respondents, branch on answers, and set quotas so the right people fill the right cells. See [Branching and routing](/logic/branching-and-routing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Publish a version snapshot and send the link, embed it on a page, or launch an email campaign. See [Publish and share](/getting-started/publish-and-share).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Watch responses arrive live, explore per-question charts, and export to CSV, Excel, or SPSS. See [Read your first responses](/getting-started/read-your-first-responses).
  </Step>
</Steps>
