## Built for people who run research.

Studies with a sample size, a screener and a stakeholder waiting for the answer.

 Separate concept cells, with purchase intent read the same way in each one.

- Usage and attitudes

 Long grids and multi-selects that still have to stay readable on a phone.

- Customer research

 Post-purchase waves that have to be comparable to the last one.

- Brand research

 Awareness and consideration measured against a set of competitors.

- Satisfaction studies

 NPS and satisfaction tracked wave on wave, with the verbatims kept.
