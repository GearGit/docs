## The 10 Best Poll Everywhere Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

Poll Everywhere is built for live session engagement; OnlineSurvey.ai is built for research data that lasts. For educators and event professionals who need to move beyond real-time audience voting and into meaningful data analysis (course evaluations, post-event surveys, longitudinal research), OnlineSurvey.ai is the natural complement or replacement.

**Best for:** Educators and event teams that need both in-session engagement and post-event research and analysis.

**Key features:**

- AI-powered survey and poll creation
- Advanced analytics with AI sentiment analysis
- Pre-event, live, and post-event survey support
- Cross-tab reporting by respondent segments
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- LMS-friendly data export

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Mentimeter

Mentimeter is the most popular Poll Everywhere alternative and covers nearly the same use case with a more modern interface and lower pricing. It's particularly strong for presentations with visual poll results: word clouds, bar charts, and live Q&A displays look polished on-screen.

**Best for:** Presenters and educators who want visually impressive live polls inside presentations.

**Key features:**

- Polls, quizzes, word clouds, and Q&A
- Presentation slide builder
- PowerPoint and Google Slides import
- Quiz competition mode with leaderboards
- Audience anonymity options

**Pricing:** Free (2 questions per presentation). Paid from $11.99/month.

### 3\. Slido

Slido is the dominant live polling platform for corporate meetings, webinars, and large-scale events. It integrates natively with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and PowerPoint, making it the corporate event standard for polling and Q&A.

**Best for:** Corporate events, webinars, and town halls where Zoom and Teams integration is required.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, quizzes, and Q&A
- Native Zoom, Teams, Webex, and Meet integrations
- PowerPoint and Google Slides plugin
- Anonymous Q&A with upvoting
- Post-event analytics export

**Pricing:** Free plan (basic). Paid from $14.99/month.

### 4\. AhaSlides

AhaSlides is the budget-friendly alternative that covers Poll Everywhere's core use cases (live polls, word clouds, quizzes, and Q&A) at a significantly lower price point. For cost-sensitive educators and event teams, it delivers strong value.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious educators and small event teams who need live polling without enterprise pricing.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, word clouds, quizzes, and Q&A
- Spinner wheel and open-ended questions
- Embedded response visualization
- Anonymous participation
- Team collaboration

**Pricing:** Free (50 participants). Paid from $7.95/month.

### 5\. Vevox

Vevox is positioned as a secure enterprise alternative to Poll Everywhere. It offers similar audience interaction features with stronger SSO, data governance, and Microsoft Teams integration, meeting enterprise IT requirements that Poll Everywhere sometimes doesn't satisfy.

**Best for:** Enterprise L&D and communications teams with strict IT security requirements.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, surveys, and Q&A
- SSO and enterprise security controls
- Microsoft Teams and Webex native support
- Moderation controls
- Branded event pages

**Pricing:** From $16.99/month per host. No free plan.

### 6\. Wooclap

Wooclap is the most educationally-focused Poll Everywhere alternative. With 20+ question types, including matching, drawing, fill-in-the-blank, and Venn diagrams, it's designed specifically for teaching and training rather than general presentations.

**Best for:** University professors and corporate trainers who need the widest range of pedagogical question formats.

**Key features:**

- 20+ interactive question types
- LMS integrations (Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard)
- Offline mode for low-connectivity environments
- Real-time response visualization
- Class session reports

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $9.99/month.

### 7\. Kahoot!

Kahoot! turns live polling into a game-show experience. Where Poll Everywhere focuses on capturing honest opinion and knowledge data, Kahoot! prioritizes energy and engagement, ideal for training sessions, onboarding, and educational contexts where motivation matters.

**Best for:** Trainers and teachers who want gamified live quizzes to boost participation and energy.

**Key features:**

- Game-show format with leaderboards
- Ghost Mode for asynchronous play
- Shared question bank across teams
- Microsoft Teams and Zoom integration
- Reports on participant accuracy

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Business from $20/month.

### 8\. Typeform

Typeform is the best Poll Everywhere alternative for asynchronous use cases. When you can't get everyone in a room at the same time but still need structured responses, Typeform's conversational format drives better completion than traditional survey links.

**Best for:** Asynchronous audience feedback, pre-event surveys, and post-conference follow-ups.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question flow
- High completion rates
- Logic jumps for routing
- Video response questions
- HubSpot and Zapier integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 9\. Google Forms

Google Forms handles basic polling at zero cost. For educators who just need multiple choice questions and a bar chart of results, it's the simplest free Poll Everywhere replacement, especially if you're already in Google Workspace.

**Best for:** Budget-zero education settings that need simple, free polling.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited responses (free)
- Auto-graded quizzes with answer keys
- Google Classroom integration
- Real-time response charts
- Google Sheets auto-sync

**Pricing:** Free.

### 10\. Crowdpurr

Crowdpurr is built for live event entertainment: trivia nights, team competitions, and high-energy group games. It's not designed for academic or corporate polling, but for event producers, venue hosts, and conference entertainment organizers who want audience interaction with competitive energy.

**Best for:** Event entertainment hosts, trivia nights, and team-building experiences.

**Key features:**

- Live trivia game show format
- Mobile-first participant experience
- Team vs. individual modes
- Branded display screens
- Host controls and scoring

**Pricing:** From $29/month. No free plan.

## Conclusion

Poll Everywhere remains solid for education and events, but strong alternatives exist at every price point. For **budget live polling**, **AhaSlides** is the value leader. For **presentations**, **Mentimeter** leads on visual polish. For **corporate webinars**, **Slido** has the best integrations. For **education breadth**, **Wooclap** offers the most question types. For teams that need polling alongside ongoing survey research and analytics, **OnlineSurvey.ai** covers the full lifecycle.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Poll Everywhere alternative?

AhaSlides (50 participants free) and Mentimeter (2 questions per presentation free) are the strongest free alternatives with similar live polling functionality. Google Forms is free with unlimited responses but lacks live visualization.

Which Poll Everywhere alternative works best in PowerPoint?

Mentimeter and Slido both have native PowerPoint plugins. Poll Everywhere pioneered PowerPoint integration, but Slido's plugin is now widely considered the most seamless.

Is Poll Everywhere good for higher education?

Poll Everywhere was built for higher education, but Wooclap and Mentimeter now offer broader question types and better LMS integrations. Wooclap's offline mode is particularly useful for large lecture halls with unreliable WiFi.
