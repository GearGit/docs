## The 10 Best Interact Quiz Builder Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai combines quiz-style engagement with research-grade survey infrastructure. Where Interact focuses almost exclusively on marketing lead funnels, OnlineSurvey.ai supports quizzes, scored assessments, NPS surveys, and research projects in one platform, with AI analysis baked in.

**Best for:** Research teams, educators, and marketers who need both quiz capabilities and real analytics.

**Key features:**

- AI-powered question generation
- Scored and graded quiz formats
- Branching logic for outcome-based results
- Real-time analytics with sentiment analysis
- CRM integrations for lead capture
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Typeform

Typeform is the most popular quiz alternative to Interact for marketers. Its conversational format, one question at a time with smooth animations, makes product recommendation quizzes, personality tests, and lead qualification flows feel natural and engaging.

**Best for:** Marketing teams building lead generation quizzes and product recommendation flows.

**Key features:**

- Conversational, one-question-at-a-time UI
- Outcome/result screens with logic jumps
- Custom branding and video backgrounds
- HubSpot, Mailchimp, and Klaviyo integrations
- Calculator fields for scored quizzes

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 3\. Outgrow

Outgrow is the most purpose-built lead generation quiz and calculator platform. It's designed specifically for interactive content marketing (quizzes, calculators, assessments, and recommendation engines) with deep marketing stack integrations.

**Best for:** Content marketers, demand gen teams, and agencies that need high-converting interactive quizzes.

**Key features:**

- ROI calculators, graded quizzes, and assessments
- 1,000+ templates across industries
- A/B testing for quiz variants
- 40+ CRM and email integrations
- Analytics on per-question drop-off

**Pricing:** From $22/month (limited features). Full features from $45/month. No free plan.

### 4\. ProProfs Quiz Maker

ProProfs has the #3 market share in quiz software and is particularly strong in education, training, and HR assessments. It offers timed quizzes, certificates, randomized questions, and detailed performance reports, features that pure lead gen tools like Interact don't prioritize.

**Best for:** Educators, L&D professionals, and HR teams building graded assessments and certifications.

**Key features:**

- Timed quizzes with pass/fail thresholds
- Automatic certificate generation
- Question bank with randomization
- Anti-cheating controls
- Detailed learner performance reports
- LMS integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $20/month.

### 5\. Opinion Stage

Opinion Stage specializes in embedded interactive content: quizzes, polls, and surveys that live inside existing web pages and articles. It's especially popular with publishers and media companies who want audience engagement embedded directly in content.

**Best for:** Publishers, media sites, and content marketers who want quizzes and polls embedded in articles.

**Key features:**

- Embeddable quiz and poll widgets
- Personality quizzes and trivia formats
- Lead capture with email gates
- Sponsored content and brand quiz formats
- Audience analytics

**Pricing:** Free plan (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 6\. Riddle

Riddle is a premium quiz builder designed for media publishers, sports brands, and entertainment companies. It produces engaging viral quizzes with advanced customization, GDPR compliance, and a no-third-party-data-selling policy that makes it popular in Europe.

**Best for:** Media publishers, sports teams, and brands that need GDPR-compliant viral quizzes.

**Key features:**

- 15+ content types (quiz, poll, list, battle, etc.)
- White-label embedding
- Lead generation with CRM export
- GDPR and privacy-first data handling
- Real-time leaderboards

**Pricing:** From $59/month. No free plan.

### 7\. Jotform

Jotform isn't a dedicated quiz builder, but its conditional logic, scoring fields, and result pages make it capable of building effective quizzes, with the added benefit of 10,000+ templates and payment processing if you need to monetize quiz outcomes.

**Best for:** Teams that need quizzes with payments, document generation, or complex workflows attached.

**Key features:**

- Scoring fields and calculated quiz results
- Conditional logic for branching outcomes
- Payment integrations
- 10,000+ templates
- E-signature and document generation

**Pricing:** Free (100 monthly submissions). Paid from $34/month.

### 8\. Involve.me

Involve.me is an interactive funnel builder that includes quizzes, surveys, calculators, and outcome flows. It focuses on converting visitors through multi-step interactive experiences rather than standalone quizzes.

**Best for:** Marketing teams building multi-step interactive funnels with quiz, survey, and form stages combined.

**Key features:**

- Multi-step interactive funnels
- Outcome pages with custom results
- Lead scoring and CRM sync
- Embed on websites and landing pages
- A/B testing

**Pricing:** From $29/month. No free plan.

### 9\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey offers a quiz format with scoring and feedback, useful for knowledge checks, training assessments, and customer education. It's not a marketing quiz tool, but for internal training and HR use cases, it's a capable and familiar option.

**Best for:** HR and L&D teams that already use SurveyMonkey and want to add quiz assessments without a new tool.

**Key features:**

- Scored quizzes with automatic feedback
- 250+ templates including quiz formats
- Team workspaces
- Reporting and analytics
- Certification-style completions

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 10\. Fyrebox

Fyrebox is a simple, affordable quiz builder aimed at small businesses and educators. It doesn't have the marketing depth of Interact or Outgrow, but it covers basic lead gen quizzes, personality tests, and knowledge assessments at a low price point.

**Best for:** Small businesses and solopreneurs who need a simple quiz tool without complexity.

**Key features:**

- Multiple quiz types (scored, personality, trivia)
- Lead capture with email collection
- Embed on websites
- Basic analytics
- Multilingual support

**Pricing:** Free plan (limited). Paid from $19/month.

## Conclusion

Interact Quiz Builder is dominant in market share, but it's overkill for some use cases and underpowered for others. For marketing-led lead gen, **Outgrow** and **Typeform** are the strongest alternatives. For education and training, **ProProfs** is the category leader. For teams that need quizzes alongside real research and feedback infrastructure, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most versatile choice.
