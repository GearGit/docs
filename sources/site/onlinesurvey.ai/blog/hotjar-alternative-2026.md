## The 10 Best Hotjar Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the strongest Hotjar alternative for teams whose primary need is survey research rather than behavioral analytics. Where Hotjar's feedback tools are add-ons to its heatmap core, OnlineSurvey.ai is purpose-built for research, with AI survey creation, advanced branching, sentiment analysis, and GDPR-compliant data collection.

**Best for:** Product, UX, and research teams that need research-grade surveys and in-product feedback beyond what Hotjar offers.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder: generate full surveys from a brief
- NPS, CSAT, and CES templates
- Branching logic and follow-up question routing
- Sentiment analysis and AI text coding
- Real-time response dashboards
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. FullStory

FullStory is the premium behavioral analytics alternative to Hotjar. It goes deeper than heatmaps with session intelligence, error tracking, and conversion funnel analysis, and it's designed for enterprise teams that need finer-grained behavioral data than Hotjar provides.

**Best for:** Enterprise product and analytics teams that need deep session intelligence and behavioral data at scale.

**Key features:**

- Complete session replay with search
- Error and rage-click detection
- Conversion funnel analysis
- AI-powered behavioral insights
- Privacy controls and data masking
- Enterprise data exports

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 3\. Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity is a completely free behavioral analytics tool that covers heatmaps, session recordings, and click maps, the core of what Hotjar offers, at zero cost. For small teams and startups that use Hotjar primarily for heatmaps, Clarity is a compelling free replacement.

**Best for:** Startups and small teams that want Hotjar's heatmap and session recording functionality for free.

**Key features:**

- Heatmaps and session recordings
- Click and scroll maps
- Rage-click and dead-click detection
- Google Analytics integration
- 100% free with no response limits

**Pricing:** Free.

### 4\. Mouseflow

Mouseflow combines heatmaps, session recordings, and feedback widgets in a similar package to Hotjar, but with stronger funnel analysis and a more affordable entry price for higher traffic sites. It's a functional Hotjar replacement for teams sensitive to traffic-based pricing.

**Best for:** E-commerce and SaaS teams that want Hotjar-equivalent analytics at lower cost for high-traffic sites.

**Key features:**

- Session recordings and heatmaps
- Funnel analysis with step-by-step drop-off
- Form analytics
- On-page feedback widgets
- Conversion optimization reports

**Pricing:** Free (500 sessions/month). Paid from $31/month.

### 5\. Survicate

Survicate is the best Hotjar alternative when the priority is in-product survey research rather than behavioral tracking. It deploys targeted NPS, CSAT, and custom surveys inside web apps based on user behavior, and connects results to your CRM and product analytics stack.

**Best for:** SaaS product teams that want behavioral-triggered in-app surveys with deep integrations.

**Key features:**

- In-app, email, and website feedback widgets
- Behavioral trigger rules
- NPS, CSAT, and CES tracking
- Segment, Intercom, and HubSpot integrations
- AI response tagging

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan.

### 6\. Pendo

Pendo is a full product analytics and user engagement platform. Beyond Hotjar's behavior tracking, Pendo adds in-app guides, onboarding checklists, NPS surveys, and roadmap tools, making it the choice for product managers who want to act on behavioral data, not just observe it.

**Best for:** Product managers who need behavioral analytics AND in-app guidance, NPS, and product roadmap tools.

**Key features:**

- In-app behavioral analytics
- In-app guides and onboarding flows
- NPS and user feedback surveys
- Product roadmap and feedback collection
- Cohort analysis and retention

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 7\. UserTesting

UserTesting is a moderated user research platform, the opposite of Hotjar's passive observation approach. It lets you run real-time usability tests with recruited participants, hear them think aloud as they use your product, and get qualitative insights that no heatmap can provide.

**Best for:** UX researchers and product teams that need qualitative usability testing with real users.

**Key features:**

- Moderated and unmoderated user tests
- Live video sessions with think-aloud protocols
- Participant recruitment from panel of 1M+ testers
- AI highlight reel generation
- Transcript and sentiment analysis

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 8\. Typeform

Typeform covers Hotjar's survey and feedback use cases with a much better respondent experience. For product teams that use Hotjar primarily for feedback widgets rather than behavioral analytics, Typeform's conversational surveys drive significantly higher completion rates.

**Best for:** Teams that use Hotjar for feedback surveys and want better completion rates and respondent experience.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question-at-a-time format
- High completion rates vs. traditional widgets
- Logic jumps and branching
- Video questions and responses
- CRM integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 9\. LogRocket

LogRocket combines session replay with product analytics and error tracking in one platform. It's developer and product-team focused, with more technical depth than Hotjar, tracking JavaScript errors, API performance, and Redux state alongside user behavior.

**Best for:** Engineering and product teams that need session replay alongside JavaScript error tracking and performance monitoring.

**Key features:**

- Session replay with error tracking
- Redux and GraphQL state logging
- Performance monitoring (API latency, load times)
- Product analytics with funnels
- User feedback surveys (basic)

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan (free trial available).

### 10\. Sprig

Sprig is purpose-built for in-product research. It combines behavioral segmentation with in-product surveys and AI analysis to help product teams understand the "why" behind behavioral patterns, and it can trigger surveys based on specific product actions with millisecond precision.

**Best for:** Product teams at high-growth SaaS companies that need in-context user research tied to specific product events.

**Key features:**

- In-product micro-surveys triggered by product events
- AI-powered response analysis
- Heatmaps inside specific product flows
- Recruitment for research sessions within the product
- Integration with analytics tools (Amplitude, Mixpanel)

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

## Conclusion

The best Hotjar alternative depends on what you actually need. For **heatmaps and session recordings on a budget**, **Microsoft Clarity** is free and excellent. For **in-app research surveys**, **Survicate** or **Sprig** lead. For **qualitative user testing**, **UserTesting** is the standard. For teams that use Hotjar primarily for feedback surveys and want a full research platform, **OnlineSurvey.ai** provides the best survey and analysis capabilities at an accessible price.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Hotjar alternative?

Which Hotjar alternative is best for SaaS product teams?

Survicate or Sprig for in-product survey research. Pendo if you also need in-app guides and onboarding. FullStory if deep behavioral analytics is the primary need.

Does OnlineSurvey.ai replace Hotjar's behavioral tracking?

OnlineSurvey.ai doesn't offer heatmaps or session recordings. It replaces Hotjar's survey and feedback widget functionality with significantly more capable research tools. Many teams run both: Microsoft Clarity (free) for behavioral tracking and OnlineSurvey.ai for research surveys.
