## The 10 Best Formstack Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

For organizations that use Formstack primarily for surveys and feedback rather than document workflows, OnlineSurvey.ai is a more capable and more affordable alternative. Its AI-powered survey builder, advanced analytics, and GDPR/HIPAA compliance make it the right choice for research teams, HR departments, and customer feedback programs.

**Best for:** Organizations replacing Formstack for surveys, NPS tracking, employee feedback, and research.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder: generate full surveys from a prompt
- Advanced branching logic and skip patterns
- Sentiment analysis and cross-tab reporting
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- White-label and custom domain options

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Jotform

Jotform is the most direct Formstack competitor. It covers forms, workflows, document generation, e-signatures, and payment processing, often at a lower cost and with a more intuitive builder. With 10,000+ templates and 150+ integrations, it handles the full range of business form use cases.

**Best for:** Operations, HR, and marketing teams that need Formstack-like workflow automation without the price.

**Key features:**

- 10,000+ form templates
- Conditional logic and calculations
- Payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal)
- Document generation and e-signatures
- HIPAA compliance on paid plans
- 150+ integrations including Salesforce

**Pricing:** Free (100 submissions/month). Paid from $34/month.

### 3\. Typeform

Typeform is the best Formstack alternative when the priority is respondent experience. Its conversational, one-question-at-a-time format produces completion rates 3x higher than traditional forms, important for customer surveys, lead gen, and research where drop-off kills data quality.

**Best for:** Customer-facing surveys, lead qualification forms, and research with completion rate goals.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question flow
- Video questions and responses
- Logic jumps and branching
- HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zapier integrations
- Custom branding and themes

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 4\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey offers a robust replacement for teams using Formstack primarily for surveys and feedback collection. Its NPS benchmarks, team workspaces, and 250+ templates give HR, research, and CX teams a more survey-optimized tool than Formstack.

**Best for:** HR teams, market researchers, and CX teams who use Formstack mainly for surveys.

**Key features:**

- 250+ expert-designed survey templates
- NPS, CSAT, and CES benchmarking
- Team workspaces and shared results
- Enterprise SSO and HIPAA (Enterprise tier)
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 5\. Cognito Forms

Cognito Forms is the best Formstack alternative for forms with complex calculations: pricing estimators, registration forms with fees, and conditional calculation logic. It's significantly cheaper than Formstack and offers HIPAA compliance on higher plans.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need forms with payment calculation, conditional pricing, and fee collection.

**Key features:**

- Powerful calculation fields
- Stripe payment processing
- Repeating sections for multi-item forms
- HIPAA compliance (higher plans)
- Conditional logic

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $15/month.

### 6\. Fillout

Fillout is a modern AI-native form builder that integrates natively with databases like Airtable, Notion, and Supabase, making it useful for product and ops teams that need forms to sync directly with their data layer. Its AI builder can generate a complete form from a description.

**Best for:** Product and engineering teams that need forms connected to databases, not just email inboxes.

**Key features:**

- AI form builder from plain-English prompt
- Native Airtable, Notion, and Supabase sync
- Scheduling and payment forms
- Conditional logic and branching
- Custom subdomain hosting

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $25/month.

### 7\. QuestionPro

QuestionPro is a full enterprise research platform that covers surveys, panels, and advanced analytics. For organizations using Formstack for internal research and surveys, QuestionPro offers significantly more research-grade capabilities: MaxDiff, conjoint, longitudinal tracking.

**Best for:** Enterprise research teams that need a full market research platform, not just a form builder.

**Key features:**

- Advanced survey logic and piping
- MaxDiff and conjoint analysis
- Survey panel access
- Enterprise SSO and GDPR compliance
- Offline data collection app

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Research plans from $99/month.

### 8\. Wufoo

Wufoo (now part of SurveyMonkey) is the old-school form builder that many businesses use for simple, reliable forms. It's not as powerful as Formstack, but for basic lead capture, event registration, and contact forms, it gets the job done at a lower price.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need simple business forms (contact, registration, payments) without complexity.

**Key features:**

- Drag-and-drop form builder
- Payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal)
- Rules-based branching
- Entry manager dashboard
- Salesforce integration

**Pricing:** Free (5 forms). Paid from $14.08/month.

### 9\. Gravity Forms

Gravity Forms is the leading WordPress-native form builder. If your organization runs on WordPress, Gravity Forms offers deep CMS integration, conditional logic, payment processing, and a massive ecosystem of add-ons, at a flat annual fee rather than a monthly per-seat subscription.

**Best for:** WordPress websites that need powerful forms with CMS-native integration.

**Key features:**

- WordPress native: forms appear inside WP admin
- Conditional logic and calculations
- Add-ons for payments, registration, surveys
- Large developer ecosystem
- Flat annual pricing

**Pricing:** From $59/year (single site). No free plan.

### 10\. Paperform

Paperform blends form building with landing page design, creating a hybrid tool that's popular with small businesses, coaches, and creators who want forms that look like branded landing pages. It's simpler than Formstack but covers core use cases at a lower price.

**Best for:** Small businesses and solopreneurs who want forms that function as lightweight branded landing pages.

**Key features:**

- Mixed content and form design (text, images, forms on one page)
- Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
- Conditional questions
- Product and booking forms
- Zapier and Make integrations

**Pricing:** From $24/month. No free plan (14-day trial available).

## Conclusion

Formstack is powerful but expensive for teams that don't need its full document-workflow suite. For **survey and feedback use cases**, OnlineSurvey.ai is the most capable and cost-effective replacement. For **workflow and document automation**, Jotform covers most of Formstack's use cases at lower cost. For **WordPress teams**, Gravity Forms is the natural choice.
