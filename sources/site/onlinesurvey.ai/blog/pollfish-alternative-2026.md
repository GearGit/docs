## The 10 Best Pollfish Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

Pollfish provides the respondent panel; OnlineSurvey.ai provides the survey platform and analysis engine. For research teams that have their own respondent lists, CRM data, or access to panels through other providers, OnlineSurvey.ai delivers significantly more powerful survey design, branching logic, and AI analysis than Pollfish's interface allows.

**Best for:** Research teams that bring their own respondents and need a powerful survey platform with AI analysis, not a respondent marketplace.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder for rapid research design
- Advanced branching logic and question piping
- Conjoint, MaxDiff, and advanced question formats
- Sentiment analysis and cross-tab reporting
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- Export to SPSS, Excel, CSV
- White-label and API access

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. SurveyMonkey Audience

SurveyMonkey Audience is the most accessible consumer panel option. You build your survey in SurveyMonkey and purchase targeted responses from their panel, with demographic targeting (age, gender, income, region) at a transparent per-response price. It's easier to use than Pollfish but slightly more expensive per response.

**Best for:** Market researchers and marketers who want a consumer panel with transparent per-response pricing and simple demographic targeting.

**Key features:**

- Integrated with SurveyMonkey's survey builder
- Demographic targeting (age, gender, income, region, employment)
- Trusted panel with quality controls
- Real-time response tracking
- Automated results charts

**Pricing:** From $1.50/response depending on targeting complexity.

### 3\. Qualtrics Panels

Qualtrics offers panel access as part of its enterprise XM platform. For large organizations that need premium panel respondents with advanced audience segmentation and integration with Qualtrics' powerful analytics, it's the highest-quality panel option, at a corresponding price.

**Best for:** Enterprise research teams that need premium panel quality integrated with Qualtrics' analytics engine.

**Key features:**

- Global panel access across 50+ countries
- Advanced audience segmentation
- Integrated with Qualtrics XM analytics
- Panel quality auditing
- Custom audience profiling

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing.

### 4\. QuestionPro

QuestionPro offers both a survey platform and panel access, similar to Pollfish's combined model. Its research-grade analytics (MaxDiff, conjoint, cross-tab) are stronger than Pollfish's basic reporting, and its panel pricing is competitive.

**Best for:** Research teams that want a survey platform and panel access in one tool, with genuine research-grade analytics.

**Key features:**

- Survey panel access (proprietary + partner networks)
- MaxDiff and conjoint analysis
- Advanced branching and piping
- Cross-tab and sentiment analysis
- GDPR compliance

**Pricing:** Free plan for surveys. Panel pricing varies. Platform from $99/month.

### 5\. Lucid

Lucid (now Cint) operates a programmatic sample marketplace, essentially an exchange where you can access respondents from multiple panel providers in one place. For researchers who need large sample sizes or niche audience targeting, Lucid's marketplace aggregates more panels than any single provider.

**Best for:** Professional market researchers who need large, flexible sample across multiple panel sources.

**Key features:**

- Programmatic sample marketplace
- 100M+ panelists across global networks
- Real-time bidding for respondents
- Quality scoring and fraud detection
- API for programmatic panel access

**Pricing:** Custom pricing based on sample requirements.

### 6\. Dynata

Dynata is one of the world's largest first-party data and panel companies. It's used by major brands and research agencies for brand tracking, concept testing, and ad effectiveness studies at scale. It's significantly more expensive than Pollfish but offers higher-quality, more carefully recruited panels.

**Best for:** Brand research teams and research agencies that need premium, large-scale consumer panels with quality guarantees.

**Key features:**

- 60M+ proprietary panelists globally
- B2B and consumer panels
- Advanced audience profiling
- Brandtracker and ad measurement studies
- API integration with survey platforms

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing.

### 7\. Kantar

Kantar is a global marketing data company with one of the world's largest consumer research panels. For multinational brands running global consumer research, Kantar's reach across 100+ countries and depth of consumer profiling data go well beyond what Pollfish can deliver.

**Best for:** Global brands and agencies running multinational consumer research programs.

**Key features:**

- 100+ country panel coverage
- Deep consumer segmentation data
- Brand equity tracking
- Retail and media research specializations
- Full-service research support available

**Pricing:** Custom pricing.

### 8\. Typeform

Typeform isn't a panel provider, but its conversational format drives higher response rates when distributed to your own audience, making it effective for reducing the need for expensive panel respondents when you have access to an existing user base, customer list, or email list.

**Best for:** Brands and researchers that want higher-quality responses from their own audience without panel costs.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question format
- High completion rates
- Logic jumps and branching
- Video responses
- HubSpot and CRM integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 9\. Google Surveys (Consumer Surveys)

Google Surveys reaches consumers through Google's survey wall. Users answer questions to access premium web content. It's one of the cheapest panel options available, though with limited demographic targeting and shorter surveys than Pollfish supports.

**Best for:** Researchers who need cheap, fast consumer opinions on simple questions with minimal targeting.

**Key features:**

- Reach via Google's publisher network
- Fast turnaround (hours to days)
- Basic demographic targeting (age, gender, geography)
- Google Analytics integration
- Low per-response cost

**Pricing:** From $0.10/response (US). Higher for international or targeted samples.

### 10\. Toluna

Toluna is a global DIY panel research platform with a community of 40M+ panelists. It's positioned between budget panel tools and enterprise providers, offering reasonable sample quality, fast turnaround, and self-serve targeting at accessible prices for mid-size research budgets.

**Best for:** Mid-market research teams and agencies that need fast consumer panels with self-serve targeting at reasonable cost.

**Key features:**

- 40M+ proprietary panelists globally
- Self-serve targeting interface
- Real-time data dashboard
- Integrated survey builder
- Panel community with high engagement rates

**Pricing:** Custom pricing based on sample size and targeting.

## Conclusion

Pollfish serves a specific need, fast mobile consumer panels, and does it reasonably well. But for teams that need better survey analytics, higher sample quality, or B2B research panels, alternatives are clearly superior. For **premium panel quality**, **Dynata** or **Kantar** lead. For **accessible self-serve panels**, **SurveyMonkey Audience** or **Toluna** offer transparent pricing. For **flexible multi-source sampling**, **Lucid** aggregates the most options. For a powerful survey platform to use alongside any panel, **OnlineSurvey.ai** provides the best research infrastructure.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best cheap Pollfish alternative for consumer panels?

Google Surveys is the cheapest per-response option for simple consumer opinions. SurveyMonkey Audience offers a good balance of targeting and price transparency for more complex research.

Does OnlineSurvey.ai provide panel access?

OnlineSurvey.ai doesn't provide a respondent panel. It's a survey platform and analysis tool. For research that requires panel respondents, the best approach is to use OnlineSurvey.ai for survey design and analysis while sourcing respondents through a panel provider like SurveyMonkey Audience, Lucid, or Toluna.

Which Pollfish alternative is best for B2B research?

Dynata has dedicated B2B panels with hard-to-reach professional segments. QuestionPro also supports B2B panel research. For B2B research, LinkedIn surveys are worth considering as a complement to panel-based methods.
