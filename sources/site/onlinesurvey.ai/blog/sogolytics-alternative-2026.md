## The 10 Best Sogolytics Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the most direct modern alternative to Sogolytics, built for the same research, HR, and CX use cases but with AI survey creation, sentiment analysis, and modern UX that Sogolytics lacks. It's also significantly more affordable for teams that don't need Sogolytics' legacy feature depth.

**Best for:** HR, CX, and research teams that want modern AI-powered survey tools with compliance at a competitive price.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder: generate complete surveys from a brief
- Advanced branching logic and skip patterns
- Sentiment analysis and cross-tab reporting
- NPS, CSAT, and employee engagement templates
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- White-label and custom branding

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the most recognizable alternative to Sogolytics for mid-market teams. With AI question suggestions, 250+ templates, NPS benchmarking, and team workspaces, it covers Sogolytics' core use cases with better integrations and a more modern interface.

**Best for:** HR, research, and CX teams that want a proven, widely-integrated survey platform with benchmarking data.

**Key features:**

- AI-assisted survey creation
- 250+ templates across industries
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking data
- Team workspaces and shared results
- 100+ integrations
- Enterprise HIPAA compliance

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 3\. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is Sogolytics' primary enterprise competitor. For organizations using Sogolytics and growing into more complex XM programs (driver analysis, predictive modeling, 360 feedback, or employee lifecycle surveys), Qualtrics offers capabilities Sogolytics can't match.

**Best for:** Large enterprises running comprehensive employee and customer experience management programs.

**Key features:**

- Customer and employee XM in one platform
- Driver analysis and predictive modeling
- Advanced text analytics (iQ engine)
- 360-degree feedback programs
- Enterprise compliance and SSO

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 4\. QuestionPro

QuestionPro competes directly with Sogolytics for mid-market research buyers. It has stronger academic and market research credentials, MaxDiff and conjoint capabilities, and a survey panel, features Sogolytics doesn't offer.

**Best for:** Market research firms, academics, and enterprise research teams that need research-grade analytical tools.

**Key features:**

- MaxDiff and conjoint analysis
- Survey panel access
- Advanced survey logic and piping
- Offline data collection app
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Research plans from $99/month.

### 5\. Typeform

Typeform is the respondent-experience alternative to Sogolytics. Where Sogolytics uses traditional survey layouts, Typeform's one-question-at-a-time conversational approach drives significantly higher completion rates, particularly for customer-facing surveys.

**Best for:** Customer-facing surveys, lead gen, and feedback forms where completion rate is critical.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question flow
- Logic jumps and branching
- Custom branding and themes
- Video questions and responses
- CRM and marketing integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 6\. Zoho Survey

Zoho Survey is the natural choice for organizations already using the Zoho ecosystem (CRM, Desk, Analytics). It offers basic survey capabilities at a low price with seamless integration into other Zoho products, making it a pragmatic alternative for Zoho-centric businesses.

**Best for:** Small businesses and teams already using Zoho CRM or Zoho Desk that want integrated survey capabilities.

**Key features:**

- Zoho CRM, Desk, and Analytics integration
- 250+ templates
- NPS surveys
- Multi-language support
- Offline surveys

**Pricing:** Free plan (10 surveys, 100 responses). Paid from $25/month.

### 7\. SurveySparrow

SurveySparrow is a conversational survey platform that covers employee engagement, customer feedback, and NPS with a chat-style survey experience that Sogolytics doesn't offer. Its automation features (recurring surveys, drip sequences, and Slack notifications) are also stronger.

**Best for:** HR and CX teams that want chat-style surveys with automation and recurring pulse survey scheduling.

**Key features:**

- Chat-style and conversational survey formats
- Recurring pulse surveys
- NPS, CSAT, and employee engagement dashboards
- 360-degree feedback module
- 1,500+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan (up to 50 responses). Paid from $19/month.

### 8\. Medallia

Medallia is the enterprise VoC alternative for Sogolytics customers who need to scale into omnichannel customer experience programs with AI text analytics and executive-level dashboards.

**Best for:** Large enterprises building omnichannel VoC programs that require AI analytics at scale.

**Key features:**

- Omnichannel feedback collection
- AI text analytics at scale
- Executive dashboards and role-based views
- Kiosk, SMS, email, and in-app delivery
- Enterprise data governance

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 9\. Survicate

Survicate covers Sogolytics' CX survey use case with better in-app delivery and behavioral triggering. For SaaS and product companies using Sogolytics primarily for web-based NPS and CSAT, Survicate's in-product survey engine is more precisely targeted.

**Best for:** SaaS product teams that need behaviorally triggered in-app NPS and micro-surveys.

**Key features:**

- In-app, email, and website survey delivery
- Behavioral targeting rules
- NPS, CSAT, and CES tracking
- Intercom, HubSpot, and Segment integrations
- AI response tagging

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan.

### 10\. Google Forms

For organizations using Sogolytics for basic internal surveys on a tight budget, Google Forms handles simple data collection needs at zero cost with unlimited responses and Google Workspace integration.

**Best for:** Budget-constrained teams that need basic survey data collection with no analytics requirements.

**Key features:**

- Free with unlimited responses
- Google Sheets auto-sync
- Basic branching logic
- Google Classroom integration
- File upload questions

**Pricing:** Free with a Google account.

## Conclusion

Sogolytics is a capable mid-market survey tool, but it's been outpaced by more modern platforms. For **AI-powered survey research**, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most modern and cost-effective upgrade. For **professional benchmarking**, **SurveyMonkey** leads. For **enterprise XM**, **Qualtrics** or **Medallia** scale further. For **conversational employee surveys**, **SurveySparrow** offers the best automation features.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best Sogolytics alternative for education?

SurveyMonkey and QuestionPro both serve education well. For academic research specifically, QuestionPro has stronger research credentials. For course evaluations and student surveys, OnlineSurvey.ai's templates and FERPA-aware data handling make it a strong choice.

Which Sogolytics alternative is best for employee surveys?

SurveySparrow's pulse survey automation and 360 feedback module are strong for employee engagement. OnlineSurvey.ai covers employee engagement, onboarding feedback, and exit surveys with AI analysis. Qualtrics is the enterprise standard for full employee XM programs.

Is Sogolytics HIPAA compliant?

Sogolytics offers HIPAA compliance on its paid plans. OnlineSurvey.ai also offers HIPAA compliance at a lower starting price, making it a viable alternative for healthcare survey needs.
