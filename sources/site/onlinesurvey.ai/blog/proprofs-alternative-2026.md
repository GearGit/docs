## The 10 Best ProProfs Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the best ProProfs alternative for teams that need both quizzes and surveys in one place. Where ProProfs treats surveys and quizzes as separate products, OnlineSurvey.ai unifies scored assessments, research surveys, and feedback collection under one AI-powered platform.

**Best for:** HR teams, training departments, and researchers who need assessments alongside ongoing feedback surveys.

**Key features:**

- AI-powered question generation for both quizzes and surveys
- Scored and graded quiz formats
- Branching logic and outcome routing
- Real-time analytics with AI sentiment analysis
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- Team workspaces for shared management

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Typeform

Typeform's conversational format makes quizzes feel engaging rather than like a test. For lead gen quizzes, personality assessments, and customer education quizzes, its design-forward approach drives significantly higher completion rates than ProProfs.

**Best for:** Marketing teams and course creators who want engaging, beautifully designed quiz experiences.

**Key features:**

- One-question-at-a-time format with animations
- Outcome/result pages with logic jumps
- Score calculation fields
- Customizable branding
- HubSpot and Zapier integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 3\. Interact Quiz Builder

Interact is the #1 market share holder in quiz software, particularly for lead generation quizzes. Unlike ProProfs' education focus, Interact is built around marketing outcomes: capturing email addresses, segmenting leads, and integrating with marketing automation stacks.

**Best for:** Marketing teams and content creators building personality quizzes for lead capture.

**Key features:**

- Outcome-based personality and product quizzes
- Email gate and CRM sync
- 40+ email and marketing integrations
- A/B testing
- Custom branded quiz results

**Pricing:** From $27/month. No free plan.

### 4\. Google Forms

For educators and trainers who just need basic, free quizzes, Google Forms handles the fundamentals well (multiple choice, short answer, auto-grading, and response feedback) at zero cost.

**Best for:** Educators who need simple, free graded quizzes without advanced features.

**Key features:**

- Auto-grading with answer key
- Point values per question
- Response feedback on correct/incorrect
- Google Classroom integration
- Unlimited responses (free)

**Pricing:** Free with a Google account.

### 5\. Kahoot!

Kahoot! is the dominant platform for live group learning. Rather than individual assessment (ProProfs' strength), Kahoot! excels at real-time group competitions that boost engagement in classrooms and training sessions.

**Best for:** Teachers and corporate trainers who run live group learning sessions and need high engagement.

**Key features:**

- Game-show format with real-time leaderboards
- Ghost Mode for asynchronous quizzes
- Shared question bank
- Kahoot! for Business with team plans
- Teams and Zoom integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Business from $20/month.

### 6\. Formative

Formative is purpose-built for K-12 and higher education. It allows teachers to assign real-time formative assessments, see live student responses as they type, and intervene immediately when students struggle, a capability ProProfs doesn't offer.

**Best for:** K-12 and university teachers who need real-time assessment visibility into student progress.

**Key features:**

- Live view of student responses as they type
- Import from Google Docs, PDFs, and PowerPoint
- Drawing response questions
- Standards alignment tracking
- LMS integration (Google Classroom, Canvas)

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $15/month.

### 7\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey's quiz format with automatic scoring is a capable ProProfs alternative for corporate training teams that already use SurveyMonkey for HR surveys. It lets you run knowledge checks and post-training assessments in the same platform as your pulse surveys.

**Best for:** HR and L&D teams that want to consolidate training assessments and employee surveys in one tool.

**Key features:**

- Scored quiz format with automatic feedback
- 250+ templates including training assessments
- Team workspaces
- Post-training completion reports
- Integration with HRIS and LMS platforms

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 8\. iSpring Suite

iSpring Suite is a premium eLearning authoring tool with industry-leading quiz creation capabilities. If your organization needs SCORM-compliant quizzes for an LMS, iSpring is the best ProProfs alternative with far more eLearning-specific features.

**Best for:** Corporate L&D teams building SCORM-compliant eLearning content for an LMS.

**Key features:**

- 14 quiz question types
- SCORM and xAPI compliance
- Branching scenarios
- Detailed learner analytics
- Direct PowerPoint integration for course creation

**Pricing:** From $770/year. No free plan.

### 9\. ClassMarker

ClassMarker is a dedicated online testing platform for professional certifications, job candidate screening, and compliance training. It offers secure, proctored testing that ProProfs only partially supports.

**Best for:** Organizations running high-stakes certification tests and candidate assessments.

**Key features:**

- Secure test delivery with IP restrictions
- Time limits and randomized questions
- Certificate generation on pass
- Webhook results export
- Custom test links per user

**Pricing:** From $39.95/month. No free plan.

### 10\. Quizlet

Quizlet is primarily a flashcard and study tool, but its Learn and Test modes function as low-stakes knowledge checks. It's popular in education for vocabulary and content review, a different use case than ProProfs' graded assessments but worth considering for learning-focused teams.

**Best for:** Students and educators who want flashcard-style learning alongside light quiz formats.

**Key features:**

- Flashcard decks with spaced repetition
- Test and match modes
- Collaborative study sets
- AI-generated study questions (Quizlet Plus)
- Classroom management tools

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Plus from $35.99/year.

## Conclusion

ProProfs Quiz Maker is reliable but aging in an increasingly AI-powered world. For **marketing quizzes**, **Interact** and **Typeform** lead. For **live learning engagement**, **Kahoot!** is the standout. For **SCORM and LMS compliance**, **iSpring Suite** is the professional standard. For teams that want quizzes and surveys unified with AI analysis, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most modern and versatile replacement.
