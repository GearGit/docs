## The 10 Best Opinion Stage Alternatives, Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

Opinion Stage covers basic audience engagement; OnlineSurvey.ai covers the full research and feedback lifecycle. For publishers and content teams that need to move beyond simple "which answer do you pick?" polls into research-grade audience insights (sentiment analysis, cross-tab reporting, longitudinal comparison), OnlineSurvey.ai is the natural upgrade.

**Best for:** Publishers and content teams that want audience polls AND research-quality data collection in one platform.

**Key features:**

- AI survey and quiz creation
- Embeddable survey and poll widgets
- Advanced analytics with AI sentiment analysis
- Branching logic for complex audience journeys
- GDPR compliance
- CRM and marketing stack integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Typeform

Typeform is the most popular Opinion Stage alternative for lead generation and audience engagement. Its conversational one-question format feels more natural than Opinion Stage's traditional embedded widget, and its CRM integrations (HubSpot, Mailchimp, Klaviyo) make it the better choice for content marketers who want to convert quiz respondents into leads.

**Best for:** Content marketers and publishers who want lead-generating quizzes with CRM integrations.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question flow
- Logic jumps and outcome routing
- Video questions and backgrounds
- Custom branding
- HubSpot, Mailchimp, and Zapier integrations
- Embed in any website

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 3\. Interact Quiz Builder

Interact holds 48.55% of the quiz software market and is purpose-built for the personality quiz and outcome-based lead capture use case that Opinion Stage also targets. Its marketing stack integrations are deeper, and its lead conversion focus is stronger than Opinion Stage's publisher-first approach.

**Best for:** Marketing teams and bloggers building personality quizzes and product recommendation tools for lead capture.

**Key features:**

- Outcome-based quiz logic
- Email gate and CRM sync (40+ integrations)
- Quiz analytics and lead tracking
- A/B testing
- Customizable result pages

**Pricing:** From $27/month. No free plan.

### 4\. Riddle

Riddle is the closest equivalent to Opinion Stage for publishers who prioritize GDPR compliance and media-grade embedded content. It supports 15+ content types (trivia, personality tests, polls, lists, tournaments) and is used by major media brands across Europe.

**Best for:** European publishers and media brands that need GDPR-compliant embedded quizzes and polls.

**Key features:**

- 15+ interactive content formats
- White-label embedding
- Lead capture with CRM export
- GDPR-compliant with no third-party data selling
- Real-time leaderboards

**Pricing:** From $59/month. No free plan.

### 5\. Outgrow

Outgrow is the most marketing-optimized alternative, built for interactive lead generation content that goes beyond polls and quizzes into ROI calculators, product recommenders, and assessment funnels. It's more expensive than Opinion Stage but delivers stronger conversion-focused features.

**Best for:** Marketing teams and agencies building conversion-focused interactive content (quizzes, calculators, assessments).

**Key features:**

- Quizzes, calculators, and assessment builders
- 1,000+ industry templates
- A/B testing
- 40+ CRM and email integrations
- Per-question drop-off analytics

**Pricing:** From $22/month (limited). Full features from $45/month.

### 6\. Tally

Tally is a free form and survey builder with clean embedding support. For publishers and bloggers who need embedded audience polls and feedback surveys without a subscription, Tally's unlimited free plan is the best no-cost Opinion Stage alternative.

**Best for:** Bloggers and small publishers who want free, embeddable surveys and polls.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited free forms and responses
- Logic jumps
- Embed in websites
- Notion-style editor
- Zapier integration

**Pricing:** Free. Pro at $29/month.

### 7\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the professional survey standard, and its embed functionality makes it a capable Opinion Stage alternative for organizations that need audience research rather than entertainment-style polls. Its brand recognition also increases completion rates on embedded surveys.

**Best for:** Research and CX teams that need embeddable surveys with professional analytics.

**Key features:**

- Embeddable survey widgets
- 250+ templates
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking
- Team workspaces
- AI-assisted analysis

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 8\. Jotform

Jotform's embed functionality and 10,000+ templates make it a strong Opinion Stage alternative for publishers who need more than basic polls: event registration, contact forms, and payment-enabled interactions embedded in their site.

**Best for:** Publishers and content sites that need a broad range of embeddable form types, not just polls.

**Key features:**

- 10,000+ templates
- Embed on any website
- Payment integrations
- Conditional logic
- Booking and registration forms

**Pricing:** Free (100 submissions/month). Paid from $34/month.

### 9\. Google Forms

For publishers who need simple embedded polls and surveys without a budget, Google Forms provides basic embed functionality with unlimited free responses. The design is minimal, but for internal feedback or simple audience questions, it works.

**Best for:** Budget-zero publishers and educators who need basic embeddable forms.

**Key features:**

- Free embedding on any site
- Unlimited responses
- Basic branching
- Google Sheets sync

**Pricing:** Free.

### 10\. Crowdsignal

Crowdsignal (formerly Polldaddy, by Automattic) is a legacy poll and survey tool deeply integrated with WordPress. For WordPress publishers and bloggers, it remains a natural fit. Polls can be added directly from the WordPress editor with native plugin support.

**Best for:** WordPress publishers who want native CMS-integrated polls and surveys.

**Key features:**

- WordPress block editor integration
- Polls, surveys, and quizzes
- Real-time results display
- Custom styling
- Export to CSV

**Pricing:** Free plan (limited). Paid from $29/month.

## Conclusion

Opinion Stage works well for casual audience engagement on content sites, but it's limited for deeper research, lead gen, or analytics. For **lead generation quizzes**, **Interact** and **Typeform** lead. For **GDPR-compliant publisher content**, **Riddle** is purpose-built. For **free embedded polls**, **Tally** is the best no-cost option. For teams that need audience polling alongside real research and analytics, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most complete platform.
