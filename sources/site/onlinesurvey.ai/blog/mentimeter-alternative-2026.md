## The 10 Best Mentimeter Alternatives, Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai goes well beyond live polling. It combines AI-powered survey creation, real-time feedback collection, and deep analytics in one platform. Where Mentimeter focuses on audience interaction during presentations, OnlineSurvey.ai is built for teams that need to act on data, not just display it.

**Best for:** Research teams, customer feedback programs, employee engagement, and anyone who needs data they can actually analyze.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder that writes questions from a prompt
- Real-time response dashboards
- Branching logic and skip patterns
- Cross-tab analysis and sentiment analysis
- HIPAA and GDPR compliance
- CRM and Zapier integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid plans from $49/month.

### 2\. Slido

Slido (owned by Cisco) is the dominant tool for meeting and webinar polling. It integrates natively with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and PowerPoint, making it easy to embed polls directly into video calls and slide decks.

**Best for:** Corporate meetings, webinars, and town halls where the goal is audience Q&A and live voting.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, quizzes, and Q&A
- Native Zoom, Teams, and Webex integrations
- Word clouds and rating questions
- Anonymous participation option
- Post-event analytics export

**Pricing:** Free plan (basic). Paid plans from $14.99/month.

### 3\. Poll Everywhere

Poll Everywhere is particularly strong in higher education and corporate training. It supports a wide range of question types and integrates with PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides, allowing instructors and presenters to embed polls directly into their decks.

**Best for:** Universities, instructors, and corporate trainers running live in-person or hybrid sessions.

**Key features:**

- Clickable image questions
- Presenter controls with slide integrations
- LMS integrations (Canvas, Blackboard)
- Moderation for responses
- Group competition mode

**Pricing:** Free for up to 25 participants. Paid from $25/month.

### 4\. Typeform

Typeform offers a conversational, one-question-at-a-time format that drives higher completion rates than traditional form layouts. While not a live polling tool, it's an excellent Mentimeter alternative for teams that need beautiful, branded survey experiences with logic flows.

**Best for:** Marketing teams, user research, and lead generation surveys.

**Key features:**

- Conversational UI with high completion rates
- Logic jumps and branching
- Custom branding and themes
- Zapier and HubSpot integrations
- Video questions

**Pricing:** Free plan (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 5\. Vevox

Vevox is a dedicated meeting and event polling platform designed for enterprise use. It positions itself as a more secure, compliance-friendly alternative to Mentimeter and Slido, with SSO and enterprise-grade data controls.

**Best for:** Large enterprises that need secure, controlled live polling in meetings and events.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, Q&A, and surveys
- SSO and enterprise security
- Integration with Microsoft Teams and Webex
- Anonymous polling mode
- Moderation controls for Q&A

**Pricing:** Paid plans from $16.99/month per host. No meaningful free tier.

### 6\. Wooclap

Wooclap is built specifically for education. It supports a wide variety of interactive question types (MCQs, matching, ranking, word clouds, and more) and is designed to work seamlessly in classrooms with both in-person and remote students.

**Best for:** Educators, professors, and trainers who need interactive classroom engagement tools.

**Key features:**

- 20+ question types
- Moodle and Microsoft Teams integration
- Collaborative brainstorming activities
- Real-time response visualization
- Offline mode for low-connectivity environments

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $9.99/month.

### 7\. Google Forms

Google Forms is free, simple, and available to anyone with a Google account. It doesn't match Mentimeter's live presentation capabilities, but for teams that just need basic polls and surveys without budget, it's the default fallback.

**Best for:** Internal team surveys, simple data collection, and budget-zero use cases.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited responses
- Google Sheets auto-sync
- Basic branching logic
- File upload questions
- 100% free

**Pricing:** Free with a Google account.

### 8\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is one of the most established survey platforms globally. It doesn't do live event polling like Mentimeter, but it's a strong choice when you need professional survey infrastructure: panels, NPS tracking, benchmarking data, and team collaboration.

**Best for:** HR teams, market researchers, and customer experience teams that need proven, enterprise-ready survey tools.

**Key features:**

- 250+ survey templates
- AI-assisted question suggestions
- Benchmarking data for NPS and CSAT
- Team workspaces
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan (10 questions, 40 responses). Paid from $25/month.

### 9\. Kahoot!

Kahoot! gamifies quizzes and learning through a competitive, game-show format. It's primarily an education and training tool rather than a polling platform, but it's excellent for knowledge checks, employee onboarding, and engagement in large group settings.

**Best for:** Teachers, L&D teams, and anyone who needs gamified trivia-style quizzes.

**Key features:**

- Game-show format with leaderboards
- Question banks and shared content library
- Kahoot! for Business with team plans
- Integrations with Microsoft Teams and Zoom
- Ghost Mode for re-playing challenges

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Business plans from $20/month.

### 10\. AhaSlides

AhaSlides is a budget-friendly Mentimeter alternative with nearly identical live polling features (word clouds, spinner wheels, live Q&A, and quiz games) at a significantly lower price point. It's a strong choice for teams that love Mentimeter's format but find it too expensive.

**Best for:** Small teams, educators, and freelancers who want Mentimeter-style features at a lower cost.

**Key features:**

- Word clouds, polls, quizzes, and spinners
- Presentation slide builder
- Live Q&A with upvoting
- Embed-in-website support
- Team collaboration

**Pricing:** Free plan (50 participants). Paid from $7.95/month.

## Conclusion

Mentimeter is a great presentation engagement tool, but it's limited when your needs expand beyond live audience interaction. For research-grade data and AI-powered analysis, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most capable alternative. For budget-conscious live polling, **AhaSlides** matches Mentimeter's core features at a fraction of the cost. For meetings and corporate webinars, **Slido** is the category leader.

## Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Mentimeter alternative?

Yes, AhaSlides, Wooclap, Google Forms, Poll Everywhere (up to 25 participants), and OnlineSurvey.ai all offer free plans. AhaSlides is the closest free alternative to Mentimeter's presentation-poll format.

What's the best Mentimeter alternative for research teams?

OnlineSurvey.ai is purpose-built for research and feedback teams that need more than live event polling, with AI analysis, cross-tabulation, and enterprise compliance features that Mentimeter doesn't offer.

Does Slido replace Mentimeter for meetings?

Slido is often the preferred choice for corporate meetings and webinars because of its tighter native integration with Zoom, Teams, and Webex. For presentations and classrooms, Mentimeter, Poll Everywhere, and Wooclap remain stronger.
