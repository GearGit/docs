## Why Agencies Are Leaving SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the default tool many agencies start with. It is familiar, easy to set up, and covers basic survey needs. But as agencies scale their research operations, SurveyMonkey's limitations become real friction points.

### No White Labelling on Standard Plans

SurveyMonkey's branding is present on surveys and reports unless you are on an enterprise plan. For agencies running client research, presenting a SurveyMonkey-branded survey to end respondents undermines the professional image clients are paying for. White labelling (custom domain, client logo, custom colours) requires a Business or Enterprise tier, which scales the cost significantly.

### Client Management Is Clunky

SurveyMonkey is built around individual accounts, not multi-client workspaces. Agencies managing 10, 20, or 50 client projects simultaneously find themselves building workarounds (separate accounts, shared logins, manual organisation) rather than having a purpose-built client management layer.

### Insight Reports Still Require Manual Work

SurveyMonkey gives you charts and data tables. Translating those into a polished, client-ready insight report, the actual deliverable most agencies promise clients, is still manual work: exporting data, building slides, writing narrative summaries. For agencies running multiple client projects simultaneously, this manual analysis overhead compounds quickly.

### Costs That Scale Poorly with Volume

SurveyMonkey's pricing scales with responses, seats, and features in ways that create real cost spikes as agency volume grows. Teams running 20+ active client surveys simultaneously quickly find that the value equation tips against them.

QuestionPro is the strongest choice for agencies doing research-grade client work: market research, customer satisfaction studies, segmentation studies, and concept testing. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Survey Tools for Agencies

### What is the best white-label survey tool for agencies?

Alchemer offers the most comprehensive white-label configuration for agencies: custom domain, complete branding removal, and full white-label reporting. SurveySparrow's Business tier is a strong alternative at lower cost, particularly for CX and NPS programmes. For agencies that prioritise AI-generated insight reports alongside branding control, onlinesurvey.ai provides white-label survey delivery with automated narrative analysis.

### Can agencies run surveys for multiple clients from one platform?

Yes, most agency-grade platforms support multi-project management. onlinesurvey.ai, QuestionPro, Alchemer, and SurveySparrow all offer workspace or project structures that let agencies manage multiple client surveys simultaneously. The key difference is whether the platform supports separate client-facing branding per project. Alchemer and SurveySparrow are strongest for per-client branding isolation.

### What is the best survey tool for delivering client research reports?

onlinesurvey.ai is the strongest choice for agencies where the deliverable is an insight report rather than raw data. Its AI-generated narrative reports, with key findings, patterns, and recommendations, are shareable directly with clients and can be exported in board-ready formats. This eliminates the manual slide-building and narrative-writing step that most other tools leave to the agency.

### How do I choose between onlinesurvey.ai and Typeform for agency use?

Choose onlinesurvey.ai if your primary value proposition to clients is insight quality and analysis speed. The AI-generated reports are the differentiator. Choose Typeform if your primary value proposition is survey design quality and completion rates. Its conversational format produces the best respondent experience in the market. For most full-service research agencies, onlinesurvey.ai's research output is more directly relevant; for brand-experience-focused agencies, Typeform's design quality can be the selling point.

### Does SurveyMonkey support white labelling for agencies?

Yes, but only on Business and Enterprise tiers. SurveyMonkey's standard paid plans include SurveyMonkey branding on surveys and reports. White labelling (custom domain, client logo, removal of SurveyMonkey branding) requires upgrading to Business (~$99/month) or Enterprise (custom). At that price point, agency-focused alternatives like Alchemer and onlinesurvey.ai are worth evaluating.

### What should agencies look for in a survey tool beyond white labelling?

Beyond white labelling, the most agency-relevant features are: automated insight generation (cutting report-writing time), predictable flat pricing as volume grows (agencies run many surveys simultaneously), team collaboration (multiple people working on one client project), and CRM integrations (passing insights into client CRM systems). onlinesurvey.ai covers all four; most white-label platforms cover branding but leave analysis and reporting as manual steps.
