## What to Look for in a Free Survey Tool

Before comparing tools, it helps to know what actually matters. Most free survey tools market themselves on ease of use, but the details that determine whether a tool is actually useful, not just technically free, come down to five things:

- **Response limits**: How many responses can you collect before hitting a paywall?
- **Question types and logic**: Can you add skip logic, branching, and rating scales without upgrading?
- **Analytics depth**: Do you get charts only, or something that explains what the data means?
- **AI capabilities**: Can the tool help you write better questions and surface insights automatically?
- **Distribution**: Can you send via email, embed, or share a link, all from the free plan?

With those criteria in mind, here are the ten best free survey tools available in 2026.

## The 10 Best Free Survey Tools in 2026

### 1\. onlinesurvey.ai: Best Free Survey Tool with AI Insights

**Free plan includes:** 250 responses a month, all 50 question types, full logic and branching, AI survey generation with a monthly allowance, shareable link.

onlinesurvey.ai is an AI-native survey platform built around a simple idea: surveys should tell you what your data means, not just show you what happened. Instead of starting with a blank form, you describe your research goal in plain language, such as "I want to understand why customers churn in the first 30 days," and the AI generates a complete, logically structured questionnaire with appropriate question types and branching.

The free plan includes 250 responses a month plus a monthly AI allowance for generating surveys, enough to run meaningful customer feedback, NPS, or product validation surveys without paying anything. For comparison, Typeform's cheapest paid plan starts at about $25 a month; onlinesurvey.ai's free plan includes 250 responses a month at $0. Unlike most free tools, it also includes full logic and branching out of the box.

Where onlinesurvey.ai stands apart from every other tool on this list: the AI-powered insights layer. On paid plans, it generates an executive summary from your responses, categorized into key findings, opportunities, patterns, and concerns, with confidence levels and margin of error included. Even on the free plan, you get per-question breakdowns that go beyond raw counts.

**Best for:** Product managers, UX researchers, and customer success teams who want surveys designed around outcomes, not just forms.

**Limitations:** Full AI insights (executive summaries, statistical context) start on the Basic plan at $49/month, as do branding removal and email campaigns. The free plan comes with a monthly AI allowance rather than open-ended generation.

### 2\. Google Forms: Best Completely Free Option

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited surveys, unlimited responses, unlimited questions, no storage cap for text responses.

Google Forms is the most genuinely free tool on this list. There are no response limits, no question caps, and no hidden paywalls on the core form-building experience. If you have a Google account, you have access to the full product at no cost.

It handles the basics well: multiple choice, checkboxes, short answer, linear scales, and simple branching based on section logic. Responses flow automatically into Google Sheets, where you can run your own analysis.

The honest limitation is that Google Forms is a form builder, not a research tool. It shows you raw data and basic charts. There is no AI question generation, no automated insight layer, no statistical context. You get what people answered, and you figure out what it means yourself. For teams that need speed and simplicity with no budget, it is hard to beat. For teams that need actionable insight, it is just the starting point.

**Best for:** Internal surveys, quick polls, event signups, teams already deep in Google Workspace.

**Limitations:** No AI features, no conditional logic beyond section branching, no analytics beyond basic charts.

### 3\. Tally: Best Free Survey Maker for Modern Teams

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited forms, unlimited responses, unlimited questions, conditional logic, file uploads.

Tally takes the unlimited approach further than Google Forms and packages it in a far more modern interface. Building a survey in Tally feels closer to writing a Notion document than filling out a web form. You type, hit slash, and pick your question type. The result is a clean, distraction-free experience that respondents tend to complete at higher rates.

Conditional logic is included at no cost, which is genuinely rare among free tools. You can show or hide questions based on previous answers without upgrading. The analytics are functional but minimal. You get response counts, completion rates, and individual answer breakdowns, but no narrative summary or insight generation.

**Best for:** Startups, indie researchers, and small teams who want an unlimited, no-fuss survey builder with a better UX than Google Forms.

**Limitations:** No AI question generation or AI insights. Analytics are basic.

### 4\. Qualtrics (Free Account): Best Free Tool for Research Rigor

**Free plan includes:** 3 active surveys, 30 questions per survey, 500 responses, 8 question types, skip and display logic.

Qualtrics is the enterprise research standard, and its free account punches significantly above its weight. You get skip logic, display logic, branch logic, and 8 question types, including the full NPS, rating scale, and matrix question formats that other tools lock behind paid plans. 500 responses is a usable number for real research.

The free plan does not include AI insights, advanced analytics, or export capabilities beyond basic downloads. But if you need methodologically sound surveys with proper logic and a platform your stakeholders will recognize, the Qualtrics free tier is a legitimate starting point.

**Best for:** Academic researchers, consultants, and enterprise teams prototyping before a full Qualtrics license.

**Limitations:** 3 active surveys only, no data export in advanced formats, no AI features.

### 5\. Jotform: Best Free Survey Tool for Templates

**Free plan includes:** 5 surveys, 100 responses/month, 500 total submission storage, conditional logic, 100+ question types, full feature access.

Jotform's free plan is notable because it unlocks nearly the full feature set. You are limited by quantity (5 forms, 100 monthly responses), not by which features you can use. That means conditional logic, 100+ field types, payment integrations, and e-signatures are all available before you spend a dollar.

The template library is one of the largest in the industry, which makes Jotform particularly useful for teams that want to launch surveys fast using pre-built structures for NPS, CSAT, HR onboarding, and more.

**Best for:** Teams that need flexible form logic and a rich template library on a tight budget.

**Limitations:** 100 responses/month is low for active research. No AI generation or insight analysis.

### 6\. SurveyMonkey (Free Plan): Most Recognized, Most Restrictive

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited surveys, 10 questions per survey, 25 responses per survey, no data export, no skip logic, SurveyMonkey branding.

SurveyMonkey is the most recognized survey brand in the world, and its free plan is the most restrictive on this list. Ten questions per survey and 25 responses is barely enough to pilot a survey, let alone run research you can act on.

It is included here because many teams default to SurveyMonkey by name recognition, and it is worth being clear about what the free plan actually offers. If you want SurveyMonkey's reporting or analytics, you will need to pay. The AI features, Genius AI for question generation and insight summaries, are also gated behind paid tiers.

**Best for:** A quick test run before committing to a paid plan. Not recommended for ongoing free use.

**Limitations:** 25 responses per survey is a hard cap. No logic, no export, branded survey experience.

### 7\. Typeform: Best Free Option for Conversational Surveys

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited surveys, 10 questions per form, 10 responses per month.

Typeform pioneered the one-question-at-a-time, conversational survey format, and on mobile, it still delivers the best respondent experience of any tool on this list. The problem is the free plan's 10-response-per-month cap, which makes it impractical for collecting data at any real scale.

If your goal is to test a concept survey, gather a handful of early responses from friends or beta users, or build something visually impressive to share in a demo, Typeform's free plan works. For production research, the math does not work.

**Best for:** Early-stage validation, design-focused teams, or situations where respondent experience is the top priority.

**Limitations:** 10 responses per month on the free plan is extremely low for any meaningful research.

### 8\. Zoho Survey: Best Free Survey Tool for Zoho Users

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited surveys, 10 questions per survey, 100 responses per survey, basic reporting.

Zoho Survey's free tier is a reasonable middle ground. 100 responses per survey is workable for small internal studies, and the reporting includes cross-tabulations and response trend charts that most free plans skip. If your team already uses Zoho CRM, Zoho Desk, or Zoho Analytics, the integration value is genuine.

Outside the Zoho ecosystem, there is less reason to choose it over Tally or Google Forms. The interface is functional but dated, and there is no AI question generation or insight summarization at any price point comparable to onlinesurvey.ai.

**Best for:** Teams already using Zoho products who want native CRM-to-survey integration.

**Limitations:** 10-question cap per survey on free plan. No AI features.

### 9\. LimeSurvey: Best Free Open-Source Survey Tool

**Free plan includes:** Self-hosted, unlimited surveys, unlimited responses, 80+ question types, complex logic, multilingual support.

LimeSurvey is the open-source option for teams with technical resources and serious research requirements. The self-hosted version is genuinely unlimited: no response caps, no question caps, no feature gates. It supports 80+ question types, quota management, complex skip logic, and surveys in 80+ languages.

The tradeoff is setup complexity. You host it yourself, which means server administration, updates, and security patches are your responsibility. The cloud-hosted version has a free tier with significant limits. For a non-technical team, LimeSurvey's cost is time, not money.

**Best for:** Academics, NGOs, and technical teams who need maximum control and zero per-response costs at scale.

**Limitations:** Self-hosting requires technical setup. Cloud free tier is limited. No AI features.

### 10\. Microsoft Forms: Best Free Survey Tool for Microsoft 365 Teams

**Free plan includes:** Unlimited surveys, unlimited responses, unlimited questions, basic branching, real-time results dashboard, Microsoft 365 integration.

Microsoft Forms is the Google Forms equivalent for teams in the Microsoft ecosystem. It is included with every Microsoft 365 subscription, so for organizations already paying for Office, it is effectively free. Unlimited responses, clean branching, and automatic result collection in Excel make it a capable baseline tool.

It does not offer AI question generation, narrative insights, or advanced analytics, but it integrates natively with Excel, Power Automate, and Teams, which gives it a structural advantage for enterprise teams already living in Microsoft's suite.

**Best for:** Enterprises on Microsoft 365 who want a no-cost internal survey tool with native Excel and Teams integration.

**Limitations:** Requires a Microsoft 365 account for full features. No AI insights or generation.

## How to Choose the Right Free Survey Tool

**If you need unlimited responses at zero cost:** Go with Google Forms or Tally. Both are genuinely unlimited and handle the most common survey use cases without restriction. onlinesurvey.ai's free plan includes 250 responses a month, which covers most single studies, and adds AI survey generation on top.

**If you need AI to help you write questions and understand results:** onlinesurvey.ai is the only tool on this list that combines AI question generation with structured, narrative insights. Describing your research goal and getting a complete questionnaire in seconds, plus responses summarized into key findings, opportunities, and concerns, is a different category of tool entirely.

**If you need methodological rigor:** Qualtrics free account gives you proper skip and display logic, 500 responses, and the credibility of an enterprise platform, worth it for research that needs to hold up to scrutiny.

**If you're already in an ecosystem:** Microsoft Forms for Microsoft 365 teams, Zoho Survey for Zoho CRM users, Jotform for teams that need a large template library.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the best completely free survey tool with no response limits?

Google Forms and Tally both offer unlimited responses on their free plans with no hidden caps. Google Forms is better for teams inside Google Workspace; Tally is better for modern interface and conditional logic.

Which free survey tool has the best AI features?

onlinesurvey.ai offers AI survey generation on its free plan, with a monthly AI allowance included, where you describe your research goal and the AI builds a complete questionnaire. No other tool on this list offers AI-powered question generation on a free tier.

Is SurveyMonkey actually free?

SurveyMonkey's free plan is limited to 10 questions and 25 responses per survey with no skip logic, no data export, and SurveyMonkey branding on your survey. It is more of a trial than a functional free product. Most teams outgrow it immediately.

Can I collect NPS scores with a free survey tool?

Yes. onlinesurvey.ai, Qualtrics (free), Jotform, and Google Forms all support NPS question formats on their free plans. onlinesurvey.ai includes AI-generated interpretation of NPS results on paid plans.

What free survey tool works best for product teams and UX researchers?

onlinesurvey.ai is purpose-built for this use case: AI-designed surveys from a research goal, structured insights, and drop-off analytics. Qualtrics free tier is a strong alternative for teams that need research-grade logic and question types.

Do free survey tools have skip logic?

Not all. Tally, Jotform, Qualtrics (free), and onlinesurvey.ai include conditional logic on their free plans. SurveyMonkey and Typeform lock skip logic behind paid tiers.

Is there a free survey tool that gives me an analysis of my results?

Most free tools show charts and response counts. onlinesurvey.ai's Basic plan generates an AI-powered executive summary with categorized findings, patterns, and confidence levels. On the free plan, you get per-question breakdowns. No other free-tier tool generates narrative insight summaries automatically.

How many responses can I get for free?

It varies significantly: Google Forms and Tally offer unlimited responses on their free plans; onlinesurvey.ai gives 250 a month; Qualtrics gives 500 total; Jotform gives 100/month; Typeform gives 10/month; SurveyMonkey gives 25 per survey.
