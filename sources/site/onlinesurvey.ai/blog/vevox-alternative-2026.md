## The 10 Best Vevox Alternatives, Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

For organizations that use Vevox for pre-meeting, post-meeting, or training feedback rather than real-time session polling, OnlineSurvey.ai provides significantly more capable survey infrastructure: AI generation, sentiment analysis, cross-tab reporting, and GDPR compliance that Vevox doesn't approach.

**Best for:** Organizations that need live feedback AND post-event research, not just in-meeting polls.

**Key features:**

- AI survey and poll creation
- Real-time dashboards and sentiment analysis
- NPS, CSAT, and engagement tracking
- Pre-event and post-event survey flows
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM and HR system integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Slido

Slido (owned by Cisco) is the most direct and feature-comparable Vevox competitor. It has deeper integrations with Zoom, Teams, Webex, and Google Meet than Vevox, a more recognizable brand for participants, and a lower entry price. For most organizations considering Vevox, Slido should be evaluated first.

**Best for:** Corporate meetings, town halls, and webinars requiring native video conferencing integration.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, Q&A, and quizzes
- Native Zoom, Teams, Webex, Meet integrations
- Anonymous Q&A with upvoting
- PowerPoint and Google Slides plugin
- Post-event analytics

**Pricing:** Free plan (limited). Paid from $14.99/month.

### 3\. Mentimeter

Mentimeter is the presentation-centric alternative to Vevox. Where Vevox focuses on meeting Q&A and governance, Mentimeter prioritizes visual engagement: word clouds, live charts, and quiz games displayed on screen during presentations.

**Best for:** Presenters and facilitators who want visually engaging live polls and word clouds embedded in their presentations.

**Key features:**

- Polls, quizzes, word clouds, rating scales
- Built-in presentation builder
- PowerPoint import
- Anonymous voting
- Export results as PDF

**Pricing:** Free (2 questions per presentation). Paid from $11.99/month.

### 4\. Poll Everywhere

Poll Everywhere is Vevox's most direct competitor in the education and hybrid events space. It predates Vevox and has a larger market presence, with native integrations for PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides alongside LMS integrations used widely in universities.

**Best for:** Universities, hybrid events, and corporate training with in-person and remote participants.

**Key features:**

- PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides plugins
- LMS integrations (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle)
- Clickable image questions
- Moderation and response filtering
- Live pace and participant reports

**Pricing:** Free (25 participants). Paid from $25/month.

### 5\. AhaSlides

AhaSlides is the budget alternative that covers Vevox's core meeting interaction features at a much lower price. For teams that don't have enterprise meeting requirements but want live polls, Q&A, and quizzes in their meetings without significant spending, AhaSlides delivers clear value.

**Best for:** Small businesses, educators, and freelancers who want live polling at low cost.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, quizzes, word clouds
- Q&A with upvoting
- Spinner wheel
- Embed on websites
- Anonymous participation

**Pricing:** Free (50 participants). Paid from $7.95/month.

### 6\. Wooclap

Wooclap is the education-first alternative with the broadest range of question types. It supports 20+ interactive formats including matching, ranking, fill-in-the-blank, drawing, and Venn diagrams, making it more pedagogically flexible than Vevox.

**Best for:** University instructors and L&D teams that need diverse pedagogical question formats.

**Key features:**

- 20+ question types
- Moodle, Canvas, and Teams integration
- Offline participation mode
- Class session reports
- Collaborative brainstorming activities

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $9.99/month.

### 7\. Kahoot!

Kahoot! brings game-show energy to meetings and training sessions. It's not a meeting management tool like Vevox, but for knowledge check activities within training and onboarding sessions, its competitive quiz format drives engagement that passive polls don't match.

**Best for:** Corporate trainers and L&D teams who want gamified live knowledge checks in their sessions.

**Key features:**

- Game-show format with live leaderboards
- Kahoot! for Business with admin controls
- Ghost Mode for async play
- Teams and Zoom integrations
- Learning reports

**Pricing:** Free plan. Business from $20/month.

### 8\. Microsoft Forms

Microsoft Forms is built into Microsoft 365, which most enterprise organizations already own. For Teams-based meeting polls, Microsoft Forms integrates natively. Survey results appear in Teams channels, and forms are accessible without any additional subscription.

**Best for:** Microsoft 365 organizations that want free, native Teams-integrated polling without extra tools.

**Key features:**

- Native Microsoft Teams integration
- Free with any Microsoft 365 subscription
- Real-time response view in Teams
- Basic branching logic
- Excel export

**Pricing:** Free with Microsoft 365 subscription.

### 9\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey isn't a live polling tool, but for Vevox customers who primarily use the platform for pre- and post-meeting surveys rather than real-time voting, SurveyMonkey offers significantly more professional survey capabilities with NPS benchmarks, templates, and team collaboration.

**Best for:** Teams using Vevox for feedback surveys rather than live session engagement.

**Key features:**

- 250+ survey templates
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking
- Team workspaces
- AI-assisted survey creation
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 10\. Typeform

Typeform is the best Vevox alternative for asynchronous meeting follow-up surveys. Its conversational format produces significantly higher completion rates than traditional survey links when sent post-meeting for feedback, reflection, or knowledge checks.

**Best for:** Post-meeting surveys, asynchronous training assessments, and follow-up feedback.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question flow
- High completion rates
- Logic jumps for routing
- Custom branding
- Zapier and CRM integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

## Conclusion

Vevox is a competent enterprise meeting tool, but it's outcompeted in almost every category by alternatives. **Slido** is the strongest direct replacement for corporate meetings. **Wooclap** leads for education breadth. **AhaSlides** wins on value. For organizations that need polling alongside genuine research and feedback analytics, **OnlineSurvey.ai** covers the lifecycle that Vevox's limited survey features can't.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best Vevox alternative for Microsoft Teams?

Slido has the deepest Teams integration. Microsoft Forms is free and native if you don't want to add a third-party tool. Vevox's own Teams integration is strong, but Slido is generally considered the more polished experience.

Is Vevox worth it over Slido?

Vevox is often preferred by UK-based organizations and universities that prioritize data sovereignty and SSO. For most corporate use cases, Slido has a larger feature set, wider integrations, and a more recognizable brand at a similar price.

Does OnlineSurvey.ai replace Vevox for live event polling?

OnlineSurvey.ai doesn't do real-time live presentation polling. It's the right replacement for the survey and research components of Vevox's use case: pre-session polls, post-event analysis, training evaluations, and ongoing feedback programs.
