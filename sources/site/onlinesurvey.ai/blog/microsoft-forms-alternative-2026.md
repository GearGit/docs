## The 12 Best Microsoft Forms Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the upgrade path for Microsoft Forms users who need actual research capabilities. Where Microsoft Forms gives you basic multiple-choice questions and a simple bar chart, OnlineSurvey.ai gives you AI-generated surveys, sentiment analysis, cross-tab reporting, and integrations that connect to your CRM and data stack.

**Best for:** Businesses, research teams, and HR departments that have hit the ceiling of Microsoft Forms.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder: describe your goal, get a complete survey
- Advanced branching logic and skip patterns
- Real-time analytics dashboard with sentiment analysis
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Higher response allowances on paid plans
- White-label options

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Typeform

Typeform's one-question-at-a-time format produces measurably higher completion rates than Microsoft Forms' traditional layout. It's particularly effective for customer feedback, lead gen forms, and research where respondent experience matters.

**Best for:** Marketing and customer experience teams that want beautiful, branded forms.

**Key features:**

- Conversational, one-at-a-time question flow
- Logic jumps and branching
- Custom themes and branding
- HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp integrations
- AI question suggestions
- Video response questions

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 3\. Google Forms

Google Forms is the most direct free replacement for Microsoft Forms, and for many users, it's just as capable. If your team uses Google Workspace, Forms integrates directly with Google Sheets and Drive, making it a seamless free upgrade.

**Best for:** Teams already on Google Workspace who want a free Microsoft Forms alternative.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited responses (free)
- Auto-sync to Google Sheets
- File upload questions
- Basic branching logic
- Real-time collaboration

**Pricing:** Free with any Google account.

### 4\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the most feature-rich alternative for professional survey needs. With 250+ templates, AI question suggestions, NPS benchmarking, and enterprise-grade team management, it's a significant step up from Microsoft Forms for business users.

**Best for:** HR, market research, and customer feedback teams that need templates, benchmarks, and team collaboration.

**Key features:**

- 250+ expert-designed templates
- AI-assisted survey builder
- NPS, CSAT, and CES benchmarking
- Team workspaces with shared results
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 questions, 40 responses). Paid from $25/month.

### 5\. Jotform

Jotform is the most versatile form builder in this list, with 10,000+ templates and deep support for payment processing, conditional logic, document signing, and workflow automation. If you use Microsoft Forms for internal business processes, Jotform can handle far more complex scenarios.

**Best for:** Operations teams, HR workflows, event registrations, and payment-enabled forms.

**Key features:**

- 10,000+ templates
- Payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal, Square)
- Conditional logic and calculations
- E-signature collection
- PDF form generation
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (100 monthly submissions). Paid from $34/month.

### 6\. Formstack

Formstack is a no-code form and workflow automation platform built for enterprise compliance. It supports HIPAA-compliant forms, document generation, and e-signatures, making it the go-to for healthcare, legal, and financial services firms.

**Best for:** Enterprise teams in regulated industries that need compliant forms and workflow automation.

**Key features:**

- HIPAA and SOC 2 compliant
- Document generation and e-signatures
- Workflow routing and approvals
- Salesforce native integration
- Advanced conversion analytics

**Pricing:** From $50/month. No free plan.

### 7\. Fillout

Fillout is the newest AI-native form builder in the market. You can describe the form you want in plain English and Fillout generates it, with logic, branching, and styling included. It's fast, modern, and integrates with Airtable, Notion, and HubSpot natively.

**Best for:** Product and ops teams that want fast AI-generated forms with database integrations.

**Key features:**

- AI form builder from a prompt
- Native Airtable, Notion, and HubSpot sync
- Scheduling and payment forms
- Conditional logic
- Custom domains

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $25/month.

### 8\. Cognito Forms

Cognito Forms excels at complex calculations: pricing calculators, registration forms with fees, and conditional pricing logic. It's especially useful for small businesses that need forms with built-in math.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need forms with calculations, totals, and conditional pricing.

**Key features:**

- Calculation fields with formula support
- Payment processing (Stripe)
- Repeating sections for multi-item orders
- Entry management dashboard
- HIPAA compliance on higher plans

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $15/month.

### 9\. Tally

Tally is a Notion-style free form builder with a clean, modern interface. It offers unlimited forms and responses on its free plan, making it one of the best completely-free Microsoft Forms alternatives.

**Best for:** Individuals, startups, and small teams who want a modern form builder with unlimited free responses.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited free forms and responses
- Notion-like editor
- Logic jumps
- Custom domains (paid)
- Zapier and Make integrations

**Pricing:** Free. Pro plan at $29/month.

### 10\. QuestionPro

QuestionPro is a full research platform with survey panels, advanced analytics, and dedicated research features like MaxDiff and conjoint analysis. It's the choice for market researchers who need a Microsoft Forms alternative with genuine research infrastructure.

**Best for:** Market research firms, academics, and enterprise research teams.

**Key features:**

- Advanced survey logic and piping
- Research-grade analytics (MaxDiff, conjoint)
- Survey panel access
- Offline data collection
- NPS and CX tracking

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Research plans from $99/month.

### 11\. Survicate

Survicate is purpose-built for in-product and website feedback. Instead of standalone survey links, it deploys targeted micro-surveys inside your web app, triggered by user behavior, NPS timing rules, or page visits.

**Best for:** SaaS and product teams that want in-app surveys and behavioral targeting.

**Key features:**

- In-app, in-product, and email surveys
- Behavioral targeting rules
- HubSpot, Intercom, and Segment integrations
- NPS and CSAT tracking
- AI-powered response tagging

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan.

### 12\. Wufoo

Wufoo is one of the original SaaS form builders, now owned by SurveyMonkey. It's not cutting-edge, but it has a loyal user base, reliable uptime, and a clean drag-and-drop builder that many teams stick with for its simplicity and Salesforce integration.

**Best for:** Small businesses that want a simple, reliable form builder with payment support.

**Key features:**

- Drag-and-drop form builder
- Payment integrations
- Rules-based branching
- Entry manager
- Salesforce integration

**Pricing:** Free (5 forms, 100 entries). Paid from $14.08/month.

## Conclusion

Microsoft Forms works fine for basic internal surveys, but it's not built for research, advanced logic, or meaningful analytics. If you need AI-assisted survey creation and research-grade reporting, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the standout upgrade. For free-forever with modern design, **Tally** is the best pick. For enterprise compliance and workflow automation, **Formstack** leads.

What's the best free Microsoft Forms alternative?

Google Forms and Tally are the strongest free alternatives. Google Forms is ideal if you're already on Google Workspace; Tally is better for a modern, Notion-style experience with unlimited responses.

Which Microsoft Forms alternative has the best analytics?

OnlineSurvey.ai offers the most advanced analytics (including AI sentiment analysis, cross-tabulation, and trend tracking) compared to any tool on this list, including Microsoft Forms itself.

Can I import my Microsoft Forms surveys?

Most platforms don't offer a direct Microsoft Forms import, but tools like SurveyMonkey and QuestionPro allow you to manually recreate surveys with copy-paste. OnlineSurvey.ai's AI builder can regenerate a similar survey from a plain-English description in seconds.
