## The 10 Best Survicate Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

Survicate excels at in-app behavioral triggers; OnlineSurvey.ai excels at research-grade survey design, AI analysis, and multi-channel feedback collection. For teams whose needs extend beyond in-product NPS into customer research, employee feedback, and market studies, OnlineSurvey.ai covers the full spectrum at significantly lower cost.

**Best for:** Product and CX teams that need more than in-app surveys, including research, employee feedback, and AI-powered analysis.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder for rapid research design
- Multi-channel survey delivery (email, link, embed)
- NPS, CSAT, and CES templates
- AI sentiment analysis and cross-tab reporting
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Flat, predictable pricing with no per-response fees

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Hotjar

Hotjar's Feedback and Survey tools cover many of the same use cases as Survicate's web widget surveys. For teams already using Hotjar for heatmaps and session recordings, its built-in on-page surveys eliminate the need for a separate Survicate subscription.

**Best for:** Product and UX teams already on Hotjar who want to consolidate behavioral analytics and in-page surveys.

**Key features:**

- On-page feedback widgets
- NPS survey with follow-up question
- Behavioral context alongside responses
- Heatmaps and session recordings
- Funnel analysis

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $32/month.

### 3\. Sprig

Sprig is Survicate's strongest direct competitor at the product research level. It goes beyond NPS to support moderated user interviews recruited from inside the product, AI analysis of open-ended responses, and heatmaps within specific product flows.

**Best for:** Product research teams at growth-stage SaaS companies that need in-product research beyond NPS.

**Key features:**

- In-product micro-surveys with millisecond precision triggering
- AI-powered response analysis
- In-product heatmaps within specific flows
- Participant recruitment from within the product
- Amplitude and Mixpanel integrations

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 4\. Pendo

Pendo is a product analytics platform that includes in-app surveys alongside usage analytics, NPS tracking, and in-app guides. For product teams that want to connect behavioral data (feature usage, adoption rates) with survey data (satisfaction, intent), Pendo is more integrated than Survicate.

**Best for:** Product managers who want in-app surveys connected to feature analytics and user guidance in one platform.

**Key features:**

- In-app NPS and custom surveys
- Product analytics (usage, retention, adoption)
- In-app guides and onboarding checklists
- Cohort analysis
- Salesforce and Mixpanel integrations

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 5\. Delighted

Delighted (now part of Qualtrics) is the simplest NPS tool in the market. Where Survicate allows complex behavioral triggers, Delighted focuses on clean, frictionless NPS delivery via email, SMS, web widget, and in-app, making it easier to implement but less configurable.

**Best for:** Teams that want the simplest possible NPS setup without behavioral complexity.

**Key features:**

- One-click NPS, CSAT, and CES surveys
- Email, SMS, web, and in-app delivery
- Real-time NPS dashboard
- Autopilot scheduling (recurring NPS sends)
- Slack and Salesforce integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan (25 responses/month). Paid from $17/month.

### 6\. Zonka Feedback

Zonka Feedback is the multichannel alternative. It covers web, email, SMS, kiosk, QR code, and in-app feedback in a single platform. For businesses that collect feedback across both digital and physical touchpoints, Zonka goes places that Survicate's web-only focus doesn't.

**Best for:** Retail, hospitality, and healthcare teams collecting feedback across physical and digital channels.

**Key features:**

- In-app, email, SMS, kiosk, and QR code surveys
- NPS, CSAT, and CES tracking
- Real-time alerts for negative responses
- AI sentiment analysis
- CRM and Slack integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $49/month.

### 7\. Typeform

Typeform's conversational format, even when embedded in web apps as a widget, produces higher completion rates than traditional survey widget popups. For teams that prioritize the quality of qualitative responses over volume, Typeform's approach yields richer open-ended data.

**Best for:** Product teams that prioritize response quality and want higher completion rates on follow-up survey questions.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question format
- Embed as widget or popup
- Logic jumps and branching
- Video questions
- HubSpot and Intercom integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 8\. UserPilot

UserPilot is a full product growth platform that includes in-app surveys alongside onboarding flows, feature announcements, and adoption analytics. It's significantly more expensive than Survicate but replaces multiple tools for product growth teams.

**Best for:** Product growth teams that want in-app surveys as part of a broader onboarding and adoption platform.

**Key features:**

- In-app NPS and custom surveys
- Onboarding checklists and tooltips
- Feature adoption analytics
- A/B testing for in-app content
- Segment integrations

**Pricing:** From $249/month. No free plan.

### 9\. Qualaroo

Qualaroo is a veteran in-context survey tool with a focus on "nudge" surveys, small pop-up questions triggered by specific user behaviors or exit intent. It predates Survicate and has a loyal base among SaaS and e-commerce teams.

**Best for:** SaaS and e-commerce teams that want intent-based and exit-intent survey nudges.

**Key features:**

- Behavioral and exit-intent triggered surveys
- AI sentiment analysis (IBM Watson-powered)
- Skip logic and branching
- Website and in-app delivery
- A/B testing for survey variants

**Pricing:** From $69/month. No free plan.

### 10\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey doesn't offer in-app behavioral triggering, but for teams that use Survicate primarily for email-delivered NPS and periodic feedback surveys rather than in-app micro-surveys, SurveyMonkey provides significantly more survey capability (templates, benchmarks, and team collaboration) at a lower starting price.

**Best for:** Teams using Survicate mainly for email surveys and NPS tracking rather than in-product behavioral surveys.

**Key features:**

- NPS and CSAT templates with industry benchmarks
- 250+ survey templates
- Team workspaces
- AI-assisted analysis
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

## Conclusion

Survicate is a strong in-product feedback tool but expensive for what it delivers. For **in-product behavioral surveys**, **Sprig** and **Pendo** are more capable. For **multichannel feedback**, **Zonka Feedback** covers more channels. For **budget in-page surveys**, **Hotjar** is the best-value alternative. For teams that need serious research capabilities alongside customer feedback, **OnlineSurvey.ai** delivers the most analytical depth at the lowest starting price.
