## Why Teams Are Switching from Alchemer (Formerly SurveyGizmo) in 2026

Alchemer, formerly known as SurveyGizmo before its 2021 rebrand, has long been a credible choice for survey-heavy organisations. It offers 43 question types, deep branching logic, and a reasonably broad integration library. For teams that built their research workflows around it years ago, it still works.

But in 2026, "still works" is no longer enough. Here's why research teams are actively looking for alternatives:

**Pricing that punishes growth.** Alchemer's pricing is tiered in a way that creates sharp cost jumps as your usage scales. Advanced features, including custom branding, certain question types, and anonymous survey capabilities, are locked behind higher tiers that most teams don't budget for at the outset.

**No AI-native analysis.** Alchemer collects responses and gives you raw data. What it doesn't do is turn that data into a narrative. You still export to Excel, run your own cross-tabs, and write your own summaries. For teams running multiple studies simultaneously, this is a serious time sink.

**A steep learning curve.** Alchemer is powerful, but it rewards users who invest time in learning it. For new team members or occasional researchers, the interface is dense and setup takes longer than it should.

**Legacy architecture.** The platform was built pre-AI. Its core design assumptions (blank form, manual distribution, data export) haven't fundamentally changed even as the product has added features around the edges.

If any of these friction points sound familiar, the ten tools below are worth evaluating.
