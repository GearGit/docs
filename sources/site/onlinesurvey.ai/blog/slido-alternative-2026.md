## The 10 Best Slido Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

While Slido focuses on real-time meeting interaction, OnlineSurvey.ai covers the entire feedback lifecycle: from pre-event surveys to live polls to post-event analysis. It's the right choice when you need data you can act on beyond the meeting room.

**Best for:** Teams that want both live event polling and post-event survey analytics in one platform.

**Key features:**

- AI-generated surveys and poll sets
- Real-time response dashboards
- Post-event analysis with AI sentiment tagging
- NPS and CSAT tracking across events
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Mentimeter

Mentimeter is the most direct Slido competitor. Where Slido is best for Q&A-heavy meetings, Mentimeter shines in presentation-style engagement with richer visualization options: word clouds, scales, open-ended responses, and quiz competitions.

**Best for:** Presenters and facilitators who want visually engaging live polls embedded in their slides.

**Key features:**

- Word clouds, rating scales, and multiple choice
- Presentation builder with poll slides
- Quiz and competition mode
- PDF and PowerPoint export
- Anonymous participation

**Pricing:** Free plan (up to 50 participants, 2 questions per quiz). Paid from $11.99/month.

### 3\. Poll Everywhere

Poll Everywhere predates Slido and remains a strong alternative, particularly for university lectures and hybrid corporate events. Its native integration with PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides is best-in-class, and it supports a broader range of question types than Slido.

**Best for:** Educators, corporate trainers, and conference presenters using slide decks.

**Key features:**

- PowerPoint, Keynote, and Google Slides plugins
- Clickable image polls
- Ranked choice and rating questions
- Moderation and profanity filtering
- LMS integrations (Canvas, Blackboard)

**Pricing:** Free (25 participants). Paid from $25/month.

### 4\. Vevox

Vevox is often described as the enterprise-grade Slido alternative. It offers similar meeting poll and Q&A capabilities with stronger security controls, SSO support, and compliance features that large organizations need when Slido's data handling doesn't meet their IT policies.

**Best for:** Enterprise IT and L&D teams that need secure, SSO-integrated polling for internal meetings.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, surveys, and Q&A
- SSO and SAML integration
- Microsoft Teams and Webex native support
- Anonymous participation and moderation
- Branded event microsites

**Pricing:** From $16.99/month per host. No free plan.

### 5\. AhaSlides

AhaSlides is the budget pick. It replicates almost everything Slido and Mentimeter do at a significantly lower price. For teams that need live polls, word clouds, Q&A, and quizzes without enterprise contracts, AhaSlides delivers strong value.

**Best for:** Small teams, educators, and freelancers who want Slido-level features at lower cost.

**Key features:**

- Live polls, quizzes, word clouds, and spinners
- Q&A with upvoting
- Spinner wheel for random selection
- Presentation slide builder
- Embed in existing websites

**Pricing:** Free (50 participants). Paid from $7.95/month.

### 6\. Wooclap

Wooclap is designed for the classroom and lecture hall. Its breadth of interactive question types (polls, quizzes, matching, fill-in-the-blank, brainstorming, and Venn diagrams) makes it the most versatile alternative for education contexts.

**Best for:** University instructors and corporate L&D teams running blended or in-person sessions.

**Key features:**

- 20+ question types
- Moodle, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom integration
- Offline participant mode
- Real-time results and reporting
- Group challenges and competitions

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $9.99/month.

### 7\. Kahoot!

Kahoot! brings game mechanics to live group polling and quizzes. It's not designed for meeting Q&A, but for training sessions, onboarding, and educational settings where engagement and knowledge retention are the goals, it's uniquely effective.

**Best for:** L&D teams, teachers, and facilitators who want gamified group learning.

**Key features:**

- Game-show format with real-time leaderboards
- Ghost Mode for asynchronous play
- Kahoot! Creator with shared question banks
- Teams and Zoom integrations
- Reports on participant performance

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Business from $20/month.

### 8\. Typeform

Typeform isn't a live event polling tool, but it's a strong Slido alternative for post-event and asynchronous feedback. Its conversational format drives much higher completion rates than traditional post-event surveys.

**Best for:** Post-event surveys, NPS follow-ups, and asynchronous participant feedback.

**Key features:**

- One-question-at-a-time conversational flow
- High completion rates
- Video response questions
- CRM and marketing integrations
- Custom branding

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 9\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey isn't a live polling tool, but it's the most capable choice for professional post-event surveys and multi-event feedback tracking. Its NPS benchmarks and team reporting make it useful for event organizers who track audience satisfaction over time.

**Best for:** Event professionals who need standardized post-event surveys with benchmarking.

**Key features:**

- Event feedback templates
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking
- Team workspaces for shared analysis
- 100+ integrations
- Custom branding

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 10\. Crowdpurr

Crowdpurr is a niche platform for live event trivia and gamified audience experiences. It's used heavily by event hosts, bar trivia nights, and corporate entertainment, not for business meetings, but for high-energy group events where competition and fun are the priority.

**Best for:** Event entertainment, trivia nights, and high-energy audience engagement experiences.

**Key features:**

- Trivia game builder
- Leaderboard display screen
- Mobile-first participant experience
- Custom branding
- Team vs. individual modes

**Pricing:** From $29/month. No free plan.

## Conclusion

Slido is excellent for Cisco-integrated enterprise meetings, but there are strong alternatives at every price point. **AhaSlides** is the best budget replacement. **Mentimeter** and **Poll Everywhere** lead for presentations and education. For teams that need polling AND ongoing feedback research, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most complete platform.
