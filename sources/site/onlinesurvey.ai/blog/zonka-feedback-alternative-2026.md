## The 10 Best Zonka Feedback Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the most capable Zonka Feedback alternative for teams that need multichannel CX surveys alongside research-grade analytics. Its AI-powered analysis, cross-tab reporting, and GDPR/HIPAA compliance make it suitable for healthcare, retail, and professional services teams that have outgrown Zonka's simpler reporting.

**Best for:** CX, research, and HR teams that need multichannel feedback collection with AI analysis and compliance built in.

**Key features:**

- NPS, CSAT, and CES survey templates
- Multi-channel delivery (email, link, embed, QR code)
- AI sentiment analysis and trend tracking
- Cross-tab analysis by customer segment
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- Real-time alerts for negative responses
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Survicate

Survicate is the in-product feedback alternative to Zonka's physical and email-first approach. Where Zonka excels at kiosk, SMS, and QR code surveys, Survicate leads in web app and SaaS behavioral survey delivery. For digital-first teams, Survicate's in-app triggering is superior.

**Best for:** SaaS and digital product teams that want behavioral in-app NPS rather than multichannel CX surveys.

**Key features:**

- In-app, email, and website survey triggers
- Behavioral targeting rules
- NPS, CSAT, and CES dashboards
- Intercom, HubSpot, and Segment integrations
- AI response tagging

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan.

### 3\. Delighted

Delighted is the simplest NPS tool in the market, designed to be up and running in minutes with minimal configuration. For teams that find Zonka Feedback's multichannel setup complex, Delighted's single-screen NPS dashboard and autopilot scheduling offer a much gentler entry point.

**Best for:** Teams that want the fastest, simplest NPS implementation without configuration complexity.

**Key features:**

- One-click NPS, CSAT, CES, and 5-star surveys
- Email, SMS, in-app, and web delivery
- Autopilot recurring survey scheduling
- Real-time NPS dashboard
- Slack and Salesforce notifications

**Pricing:** Free plan (25 responses/month). Paid from $17/month.

### 4\. AskNicely

AskNicely connects customer satisfaction scores to individual frontline employee performance, making it unique among CX tools. For service businesses where linking the frontline worker experience to customer outcomes is critical, AskNicely offers capabilities Zonka doesn't approach.

**Best for:** Service businesses (field services, retail, hospitality) that want to connect NPS to frontline employee coaching.

**Key features:**

- Real-time NPS collection post-interaction
- Frontline employee leaderboards
- Coaching workflow for managers
- Integration with field service CRMs
- Team-level NPS reporting

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 5\. Medallia

Medallia is the enterprise CX platform that Zonka Feedback customers graduate to when volume, complexity, and executive visibility requirements grow. It's significantly more expensive but offers omnichannel VoC capabilities, AI text analytics at scale, and executive dashboards that Zonka can't match.

**Best for:** Large enterprises running enterprise-scale VoC programs across thousands of customer touchpoints.

**Key features:**

- Omnichannel feedback collection
- AI text analytics at scale
- Executive and operational dashboards
- Kiosk, SMS, email, and in-app delivery
- Enterprise SSO and data governance

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 6\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the professional survey platform with NPS and CSAT capabilities that match Zonka's CX use cases at a lower price. Its benchmarking data, showing how your scores compare across industries, is a feature Zonka Feedback doesn't offer.

**Best for:** Mid-market CX and HR teams that need NPS benchmarking and professional survey templates alongside CX measurement.

**Key features:**

- NPS and CSAT with industry benchmarks
- 250+ templates including CX surveys
- Team workspaces
- AI-assisted analysis
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 7\. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is the enterprise XM platform that competes with Medallia at the top of the market. For large organizations considering Zonka Feedback for complex CX programs, Qualtrics offers the fullest feature set, including driver analysis, predictive modeling, and text analytics, at enterprise scale.

**Best for:** Large enterprises and universities running comprehensive customer and employee experience programs.

**Key features:**

- Omnichannel VoC collection
- Driver analysis and predictive modeling
- Employee XM alongside customer XM
- Advanced text analytics (iQ engine)
- Enterprise compliance and SSO

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan (limited trial available).

### 8\. CustomerGauge

CustomerGauge is purpose-built for B2B NPS account health tracking. Where Zonka treats all survey responses equally, CustomerGauge weights scores by account revenue, so you see which clients represent churn risk in revenue terms, not just response count.

**Best for:** B2B SaaS and professional services companies running revenue-weighted NPS programs.

**Key features:**

- Revenue-weighted NPS by account
- Account-level health dashboards
- Churn risk alerts
- Close-the-loop workflow for account managers
- Salesforce and HubSpot integration

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 9\. Hotjar

Hotjar's on-page feedback widgets and NPS surveys cover Zonka's digital survey use cases, with the added benefit of heatmaps and session recordings that give behavioral context to survey responses. For digital-first teams, Hotjar offers more context per response than Zonka.

**Best for:** Digital product and UX teams that want on-page surveys alongside behavioral analytics.

**Key features:**

- On-page feedback widgets
- NPS survey with follow-up questions
- Heatmaps and session recordings
- Funnel analysis
- Behavioral context on responses

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $32/month.

### 10\. Typeform

Typeform's conversational NPS and CSAT flows achieve higher completion rates than traditional survey widgets. For CX teams where response quality and verbatim richness matter more than multichannel delivery breadth, Typeform produces better qualitative data per response.

**Best for:** CX teams that prioritize response quality and rich verbatim feedback over high-volume multichannel delivery.

**Key features:**

- Conversational NPS and CSAT flows
- Logic jumps based on score
- Custom branding
- HubSpot, Salesforce, and Intercom integrations
- Video response questions

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

## Conclusion

Zonka Feedback is a capable multichannel CX platform, but it's not the only option. For **enterprise VoC programs**, **Medallia** or **Qualtrics** scale further. For **simple NPS**, **Delighted** is the easiest to deploy. For **B2B account health**, **CustomerGauge** is purpose-built. For teams that need multichannel feedback with research-grade analytics and AI analysis at an accessible price, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the best-value alternative.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Zonka Feedback alternative?

OnlineSurvey.ai and Delighted both offer free plans covering NPS and CSAT surveys. Delighted's free plan is the simplest; OnlineSurvey.ai's free plan offers more analytical capabilities.

Which Zonka Feedback alternative supports kiosk surveys?

Medallia and Qualtrics support kiosk-based survey collection at enterprise scale. OnlineSurvey.ai supports tablet-based survey modes. Most SMB tools (SurveyMonkey, Typeform) require a tablet browser approach rather than a dedicated kiosk mode.

Is Zonka Feedback HIPAA compliant?

Zonka Feedback offers HIPAA compliance on its paid plans, making it suitable for healthcare patient satisfaction surveys. OnlineSurvey.ai also offers HIPAA compliance at a lower starting price.
