## The 10 Best Wufoo Alternatives, Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

For teams using Wufoo for customer feedback, employee surveys, or any survey-adjacent form use case, OnlineSurvey.ai is the most capable modern replacement. Its AI survey builder, advanced analytics, and compliance features represent a generational leap beyond Wufoo's static form builder.

**Best for:** Organizations that use Wufoo primarily for surveys, feedback forms, and data collection, not just contact forms.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder generates complete surveys from a prompt
- Advanced branching logic and skip patterns
- Real-time analytics with sentiment analysis
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier)
- White-label and custom domain options
- Higher response allowances on paid plans

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Jotform

Jotform is the most direct Wufoo replacement. It offers the same core functionality (drag-and-drop form builder, conditional logic, payment processing, and entry management) but with 10,000+ templates, a much more modern interface, and integrations Wufoo doesn't support.

**Best for:** Businesses that use Wufoo for contact forms, event registration, payments, and internal workflows.

**Key features:**

- Drag-and-drop builder with 10,000+ templates
- Payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal, Square)
- Conditional logic and calculations
- E-signatures and document generation
- HIPAA compliance on paid plans
- 150+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (100 submissions/month). Paid from $34/month.

### 3\. Typeform

Typeform's one-question-at-a-time format is a significant UX upgrade over Wufoo's traditional form layout. For customer-facing forms where completion rate matters (lead capture, feedback, onboarding surveys), Typeform drives measurably better response rates.

**Best for:** Customer-facing forms, lead gen, and feedback collection where completion rate is critical.

**Key features:**

- Conversational single-question flow
- Logic jumps and branching
- Video questions and backgrounds
- Custom branding and themes
- HubSpot, Salesforce, and Mailchimp integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 4\. Google Forms

For teams that use Wufoo for basic internal forms and don't need payment processing or advanced logic, Google Forms is a completely free alternative that handles most simple use cases well.

**Best for:** Teams on Google Workspace that need simple, free forms for internal use.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited responses (free)
- Auto-sync to Google Sheets
- File upload questions
- Basic conditional logic
- Real-time collaboration

**Pricing:** Free with a Google account.

### 5\. Tally

Tally is a modern free form builder with a Notion-like editor. Its free plan includes unlimited forms and unlimited responses, a major advantage over Wufoo's response limits. For modern teams looking for clean design without complexity, Tally is hard to beat on value.

**Best for:** Startups, freelancers, and small teams who want unlimited free forms with a modern design.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited forms and responses on free plan
- Notion-style block editor
- Logic jumps and conditional questions
- Custom domains (paid)
- Zapier and Make integrations
- Airtable and Notion sync

**Pricing:** Free. Pro plan at $29/month.

### 6\. Fillout

Fillout is the AI-native challenger in the form building space. It generates complete forms from a plain-English description and integrates natively with Airtable, Notion, and Supabase, making it useful for product and ops teams that want forms connected directly to their data layer.

**Best for:** Product and engineering teams that want AI-built forms with native database sync.

**Key features:**

- AI form builder from text prompt
- Native Airtable, Notion, Supabase sync
- Scheduling and payment forms
- Conditional logic
- Custom subdomain hosting
- Approval workflows

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $25/month.

### 7\. Cognito Forms

Cognito Forms is the best Wufoo alternative for businesses that need forms with complex calculations: pricing estimators, fee calculators, and multi-item order forms. It's cheaper than Wufoo for equivalent features and handles math-heavy forms better than any tool in this category.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need forms with calculated totals, pricing logic, and payment processing.

**Key features:**

- Calculation fields with formula support
- Payment processing (Stripe)
- Repeating sections for multi-item forms
- HIPAA compliance (higher plans)
- Conditional logic and branching

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $15/month.

### 8\. Gravity Forms

Gravity Forms is the standard for WordPress-native forms. If your organization runs on WordPress and uses Wufoo via embed, switching to Gravity Forms gives you tighter CMS integration, a large add-on ecosystem, and flat annual pricing instead of monthly per-submission fees.

**Best for:** WordPress websites that want tightly integrated native forms with a large plugin ecosystem.

**Key features:**

- WordPress native with WP Admin integration
- Conditional logic and calculations
- Payment add-ons (Stripe, PayPal)
- Survey and quiz add-ons
- Developer-friendly with REST API

**Pricing:** From $59/year (single site). No free plan.

### 9\. Paperform

Paperform blends form building with landing page design. You can mix paragraphs, images, headings, and form fields on a single page. For businesses that use Wufoo for simple lead capture forms and want something more visually branded, Paperform is an elegant upgrade.

**Best for:** Coaches, creators, and small businesses who want forms that double as branded landing pages.

**Key features:**

- Mixed content and form design on one page
- Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
- Conditional questions
- Product and booking forms
- Zapier integrations

**Pricing:** From $24/month. 14-day trial. No free plan.

### 10\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey (which now owns Wufoo) is worth considering for teams that want to stay in the same corporate family but upgrade capabilities. Its professional survey templates, NPS benchmarks, and team collaboration features go well beyond what Wufoo offers.

**Best for:** Teams that already trust the SurveyMonkey/Wufoo ecosystem and want to upgrade within it.

**Key features:**

- 250+ survey templates
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking
- AI question suggestions
- Team workspaces
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (10 questions, 40 responses). Paid from $25/month.

## Conclusion

Wufoo served a generation of businesses well, but in 2026, much stronger alternatives exist at every price point. For **unlimited free forms**, **Tally** leads. For **full-featured form workflows**, **Jotform** is the natural Wufoo replacement. For **AI-powered survey research**, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most capable upgrade. For **WordPress teams**, **Gravity Forms** is the clear winner.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Wufoo alternative?

Tally and Google Forms are the strongest free Wufoo alternatives. Tally offers unlimited responses for free; Google Forms is best if you already use Google Workspace. Cognito Forms also has a capable free tier.

Is Jotform better than Wufoo?

In nearly every measurable way (templates, integrations, pricing, modern UI, and feature depth), Jotform is a stronger choice than Wufoo for most business use cases.

Does Wufoo still have a free plan?

Wufoo has a limited free plan (5 forms, 100 monthly entries, 10 fields per form). For most real business use cases, these limits are quickly hit, making the paid plans necessary. Competitors like Tally and Google Forms offer far more for free.
