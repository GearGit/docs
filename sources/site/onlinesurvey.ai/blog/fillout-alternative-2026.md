## The 10 Best Fillout Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

Fillout is built for connecting forms to databases; OnlineSurvey.ai is built for turning survey responses into research intelligence. If your form-building needs extend into NPS tracking, customer research, employee feedback, and AI-powered response analysis, OnlineSurvey.ai is the more capable platform.

**Best for:** Teams that need AI-powered survey creation with advanced analytics, compliance, and CRM integrations beyond what Fillout offers.

**Key features:**

- AI survey builder from a plain-English brief
- Advanced branching logic and skip patterns
- Sentiment analysis and cross-tab reporting
- NPS, CSAT, and employee engagement templates
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Typeform

Typeform is the most design-forward Fillout alternative. For customer-facing forms where respondent experience is critical (lead generation, onboarding, feedback), Typeform's conversational one-question format drives measurably higher completion rates than Fillout's traditional form layout.

**Best for:** Marketing and CX teams that prioritize form UX and completion rate over database connectivity.

**Key features:**

- Conversational one-question-at-a-time flow
- Logic jumps and branching
- Video questions and custom backgrounds
- HubSpot, Salesforce, and Klaviyo integrations
- Custom branding and white-label

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 3\. Jotform

Jotform is the most feature-complete form builder available. It covers conditional logic, payments, document generation, e-signatures, and approval workflows at scale, with 10,000+ templates and 150+ integrations. For teams that find Fillout's capabilities too narrow, Jotform handles far more complex business scenarios.

**Best for:** Operations, HR, and marketing teams that need forms with complex logic, payments, and workflow automation.

**Key features:**

- 10,000+ templates
- Conditional logic and calculations
- Payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal, Square)
- E-signatures and document generation
- 150+ integrations
- HIPAA compliance on paid plans

**Pricing:** Free (100 submissions/month). Paid from $34/month.

### 4\. Tally

Tally is the closest aesthetic alternative to Fillout. Both use a clean, Notion-inspired design. But Tally's free plan is far more generous: unlimited forms and unlimited responses at no cost. For teams that don't need Fillout's database sync features, Tally delivers a nearly identical user experience for free.

**Best for:** Individuals, startups, and small teams who want a modern form builder with unlimited free usage.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited free forms and responses
- Notion-style block editor
- Logic jumps
- Custom domain (paid)
- Airtable, Notion, and Zapier integrations (paid)

**Pricing:** Free. Pro at $29/month.

### 5\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is the professional survey standard for teams that need Fillout's form functionality alongside NPS benchmarking, team collaboration, and research templates. Its AI question suggestions and 250+ templates make form creation fast even without Fillout's AI prompt approach.

**Best for:** Professional research, HR, and CX teams that need templates, benchmarks, and team workspaces.

**Key features:**

- AI-assisted question suggestions
- 250+ survey and form templates
- NPS and CSAT benchmarking
- Team workspaces
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 6\. Google Forms

For teams that use Fillout for simple internal forms, Google Forms handles the basics completely free, with unlimited responses, Google Sheets sync, and basic branching logic included.

**Best for:** Teams on Google Workspace that need simple, free forms for internal data collection.

**Key features:**

- Unlimited responses (free)
- Auto-sync to Google Sheets
- File upload questions
- Basic conditional logic
- Google Classroom integration

**Pricing:** Free with a Google account.

### 7\. Cognito Forms

Cognito Forms excels at forms with complex calculation logic: pricing estimators, registration forms with fees, and multi-item order forms. For teams that use Fillout for data-collection forms but need calculated totals and pricing logic, Cognito Forms handles the math better.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need forms with complex calculation fields and payment processing.

**Key features:**

- Formula-based calculation fields
- Payment processing (Stripe)
- Repeating sections
- Conditional logic
- HIPAA compliance on higher plans

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $15/month.

### 8\. Paperform

Paperform blends form and landing page design: you write text, add images, and embed form fields on a single flowing page. For coaches, consultants, and creators who use Fillout for lead capture and booking forms and want something more visually branded, Paperform is the design-forward alternative.

**Best for:** Coaches, creators, and small businesses who want forms that look and feel like branded landing pages.

**Key features:**

- Mixed text, media, and form fields on one page
- Payment processing
- Booking and scheduling forms
- Conditional questions
- Zapier integrations

**Pricing:** From $24/month. 14-day trial. No free plan.

### 9\. Airtable Forms

For teams already using Airtable as their database, Airtable's native form builder is the simplest path: data goes directly into Airtable tables without any integration setup. It's not as capable as Fillout's forms, but it eliminates integration friction entirely.

**Best for:** Teams already on Airtable who want the simplest native form-to-database connection without a third-party tool.

**Key features:**

- Native Airtable database sync
- Linked record fields
- Conditional field visibility
- Custom branding on higher plans
- Email notifications on submission

**Pricing:** Free (with Airtable). Paid Airtable from $20/month.

### 10\. Formstack

Formstack is the enterprise alternative with stronger compliance, document generation, and workflow routing than Fillout. For large organizations that need forms to trigger multi-step approval workflows, e-signatures, and document generation, Formstack covers use cases that Fillout can't.

**Best for:** Enterprise teams in regulated industries that need forms with compliance documentation and approval workflows.

**Key features:**

- HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance
- Document generation and e-signatures
- Workflow routing and multi-step approvals
- Salesforce native integration
- Conversion analytics

**Pricing:** From $50/month. No free plan.

## Conclusion

Fillout is an excellent choice for database-connected forms, but it's narrow in research and analytics capabilities. For **maximum free usage**, **Tally** is the best alternative. For **enterprise workflow automation**, **Formstack** leads. For **conversational UX and lead gen**, **Typeform** is the market standard. For teams that need AI-powered survey research with compliance and analytics, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most complete upgrade.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Fillout alternative?

Tally is the closest free equivalent in design and usability. Google Forms is free with unlimited responses but a more traditional interface. OnlineSurvey.ai's free plan covers research surveys beyond Fillout's form capabilities.

Which Fillout alternative works best with Airtable?

Airtable's native forms are the simplest integration. Tally (paid) and Jotform also support Airtable sync. OnlineSurvey.ai can export to CSV for Airtable import if native sync isn't required.

Is Fillout better than Typeform?

Fillout excels at database connectivity (Airtable, Notion, Supabase). Typeform excels at respondent experience and completion rates. They serve different primary use cases: database-first operations teams vs. customer-facing survey experiences.
