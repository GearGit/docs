## The 10 Best Delighted Alternatives: Reviewed

### 1\. OnlineSurvey.ai

OnlineSurvey.ai is the most versatile Delighted alternative, covering NPS, CSAT, and CES alongside research surveys, employee feedback, and AI-powered analysis. Where Delighted locks you into simple metric tracking, OnlineSurvey.ai lets you go deep on the "why" behind your scores.

**Best for:** CX teams and researchers who want NPS/CSAT tracking with full survey capabilities and AI insights in one platform.

**Key features:**

- NPS, CSAT, and CES survey templates
- AI-powered follow-up question generation
- Sentiment analysis and trend tracking
- Cross-tab analysis by segment
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- GDPR and HIPAA compliance
- Flat, predictable pricing on paid plans

**Pricing:** Free plan with 250 responses/month. Paid from $49/month.

### 2\. Survicate

Survicate is a direct Delighted competitor built for SaaS and product teams. It deploys NPS and micro-surveys inside web apps triggered by user behavior, whether a user completes an action, reaches a certain usage threshold, or hits a time-based trigger, driving higher response rates than email-based NPS.

**Best for:** SaaS product teams that want in-app NPS and behavioral micro-surveys.

**Key features:**

- In-app, email, and web widget surveys
- Behavioral triggering rules
- NPS, CSAT, and CES tracking
- Intercom, HubSpot, and Segment integrations
- AI response tagging and categorization

**Pricing:** From $99/month. No free plan.

### 3\. Zonka Feedback

Zonka Feedback is a multichannel CX platform covering in-app, email, SMS, kiosk, and website surveys. It supports NPS, CSAT, and CES alongside richer custom survey formats, and is popular in retail, hospitality, and healthcare for multi-touchpoint feedback.

**Best for:** Businesses that collect feedback across multiple channels: web, mobile, email, SMS, and in-person kiosks.

**Key features:**

- NPS, CSAT, and CES surveys
- Multichannel delivery (email, SMS, in-app, kiosk, QR code)
- Real-time alerts for detractor responses
- AI sentiment analysis
- CRM and Slack integrations
- Offline survey mode

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $49/month.

### 4\. AskNicely

AskNicely is a specialized frontline experience platform that connects customer NPS scores to individual employee performance. It's used by service businesses (field services, hospitality, retail) where connecting frontline worker behavior to customer satisfaction metrics matters.

**Best for:** Service businesses that want to link customer NPS directly to frontline employee coaching and performance.

**Key features:**

- Real-time NPS collection after service interactions
- Employee leaderboards and coaching workflows
- Frontline worker app for real-time score visibility
- Integration with CRM and field service platforms
- Team-level NPS reporting

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 5\. CustomerGauge

CustomerGauge is a B2B-specific NPS and account experience platform. Unlike Delighted, which treats all customers equally, CustomerGauge weights NPS scores by revenue, so you see which accounts are at churn risk before it impacts ARR.

**Best for:** B2B SaaS and enterprise companies that need revenue-weighted NPS and account health tracking.

**Key features:**

- Revenue-weighted NPS scoring
- Account-level CX dashboards
- Churn prediction and early warning signals
- Close-the-loop workflow for account managers
- Integration with Salesforce and HubSpot

**Pricing:** Custom pricing. No free plan.

### 6\. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey's NPS features include industry benchmarking, showing how your score compares to competitors in your sector. For teams that find Delighted too simple, SurveyMonkey adds templates, benchmarks, and team collaboration at a lower enterprise price point.

**Best for:** Mid-market CX and HR teams that need NPS benchmarking alongside professional survey management.

**Key features:**

- NPS survey templates with benchmarking data
- CSAT and CES measurement
- Team workspaces for shared results
- AI-assisted analysis
- 100+ integrations

**Pricing:** Free (limited). Paid from $25/month.

### 7\. Typeform

Typeform's conversational format boosts NPS response rates. The one-question-at-a-time flow feels less like a form and more like a natural check-in. For teams that want higher-quality follow-up responses alongside their NPS score, Typeform's open-ended follow-up questions produce richer qualitative data.

**Best for:** CX teams that prioritize the quality of follow-up responses over volume of NPS data.

**Key features:**

- Conversational NPS flow with follow-up questions
- Logic jumps based on NPS score
- Custom branding and themes
- HubSpot and Salesforce integrations
- High completion rates

**Pricing:** Free (10 responses/month). Paid from $29/month.

### 8\. Medallia

Medallia is the enterprise CX platform that Qualtrics (Delighted's parent) competes with at the top of the market. For large organizations that need omnichannel VoC programs, AI-powered text analytics, and enterprise-grade security, Medallia offers capabilities that Delighted's simplicity doesn't touch.

**Best for:** Large enterprises running omnichannel VoC programs that need advanced AI text analytics.

**Key features:**

- Omnichannel feedback collection
- AI-powered text and sentiment analytics
- Role-based dashboards for executives vs. frontline
- Closed-loop action management
- Enterprise SSO and compliance

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing. No free plan.

### 9\. QuestionPro

QuestionPro's CX module combines NPS, CSAT, and CES with research-grade survey capabilities and panel access. For organizations that need VoC measurement alongside customer research studies, QuestionPro eliminates the need for multiple platforms.

**Best for:** Research and CX teams that run both ongoing NPS programs and periodic customer research studies.

**Key features:**

- NPS, CSAT, and CES dashboards
- Advanced survey logic and piping
- Text analytics and sentiment scoring
- Survey panel access for research
- Enterprise compliance

**Pricing:** Free plan available. CX plans from $99/month.

### 10\. Hotjar

Hotjar isn't primarily a CX survey tool, but its on-page micro-surveys and NPS widget are widely used by product and UX teams to capture in-context feedback. For teams that already use Hotjar for heatmaps and session recordings, its feedback tools are a natural complement.

**Best for:** Product and UX teams that want NPS and micro-surveys integrated with heatmaps and session recordings.

**Key features:**

- On-page micro-survey widgets
- NPS survey with follow-up question
- Behavioral context (see what the user was doing when they responded)
- Heatmaps and session recordings
- Funnel analysis

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Paid from $32/month.

## Conclusion

Delighted's simplicity is its strength and its limitation. For **in-app behavioral NPS**, **Survicate** is the leader. For **B2B account health**, **CustomerGauge** is purpose-built. For **multichannel CX**, **Zonka Feedback** covers more touchpoints. For teams that want NPS alongside full research and AI analysis in one platform, **OnlineSurvey.ai** is the most complete upgrade path.

## Frequently asked questions

What's the best free Delighted alternative?

OnlineSurvey.ai and Zonka Feedback both offer free plans that include NPS survey functionality. For pure NPS with no other features needed, Google Forms with manual NPS calculation is also free but requires more setup.

Which Delighted alternative is best for B2B companies?

CustomerGauge is built specifically for B2B account-level NPS with revenue weighting. For B2B teams that also need research capabilities, OnlineSurvey.ai or QuestionPro cover both.

Is Delighted still worth using after the Qualtrics acquisition?

Delighted remains a clean, simple NPS tool, but Qualtrics' pricing direction has pushed many SMBs toward alternatives. For teams that need more than a single NPS metric, the alternatives above offer significantly more capability at comparable cost.
