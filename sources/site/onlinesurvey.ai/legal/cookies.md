# Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies onlinesurvey.ai uses, why we use them, and how to control them.

Last updated September 1, 2026

## 1\. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store and send back on later visits. Cookies let a site recognize your browser, keep you signed in between pages, and remember choices you made.

This policy also covers similar technologies that work in the same way, including local storage and session storage in your browser, and small pieces of tracking code such as pixels. Where this policy says "cookies", it means all of them.

This policy is part of the Privacy Policy at [/legal/privacy](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/privacy), which explains what we do with the data these technologies produce.

## 2\. Why we use them

We use cookies to keep you signed in, to keep the product working across page loads, to protect accounts against fraud and abuse, and to understand in aggregate how the product and the website are used so that we can improve them.

We do not use cookies to build an advertising profile of you, and we do not sell the data they produce.

## 3\. Essential cookies

Essential cookies are required for the Service to work. They cannot be turned off through a consent control, because without them you cannot sign in and the product cannot function. They are used to:

- keep you authenticated after you sign in, and keep your session alive as you move between pages;
- protect forms and requests against cross-site request forgery;
- apply load balancing, routing and basic security checks at the network edge;
- remember interface preferences such as language and layout choices;
- keep your place in a survey you are answering, so that progress is not lost between screens.

If you block these, you will not be able to sign in and parts of the Service will not work.

## 4\. Analytics cookies

We use analytics to understand how many people use the site and the product, which pages and features they use, where they arrive from, and where they run into trouble. This is used in aggregate, to make product decisions.

The analytics providers we use are:

- PostHog, for product analytics inside the application.
- Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, for website measurement on our public pages.

These providers set their own cookies and receive the usage data described in the Privacy Policy. Where the law requires consent for analytics cookies, we ask for it before setting them, and you can change your mind at any time.

## 5\. No advertising cookies

We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies, we do not run third-party ad networks on the Service, and we do not share cookie data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and consent will be requested where the law requires it.

## 6\. Cookies when you answer a survey

Surveys are served from a separate address to the main application. When you answer a survey, strictly necessary storage may be used to keep your place, to prevent a single session from being submitted twice, and to protect against automated abuse.

The customer who built the survey decides what is asked and what is done with the answers. It is the customer, not us, who is responsible for the notices and the consent that apply to their research. See section 2 of the Privacy Policy at [/legal/privacy](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/privacy).

## 7\. First-party and third-party cookies

First-party cookies are set by us, on our own domains. Third-party cookies are set by the providers listed in section 4, and by Google where you choose to sign in with a Google account.

We do not control how third parties operate their own cookies. Their policies apply to that processing, alongside our contracts with them.

## 8\. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay until they expire or until you delete them. Sign-in cookies last for the length of a session and are refreshed while you stay active. Analytics cookies typically persist for a period set by the provider so that a returning visitor is not counted twice.

## 9\. Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you see the cookies stored, delete them, block third-party cookies, or block cookies from a specific site. The controls are usually under privacy or site settings. Browser help pages describe the exact steps.

Blocking analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the Service. Blocking essential cookies does: you will not be able to sign in, sessions will not persist, and survey progress may be lost.

Clearing your cookies signs you out and resets any preference stored in your browser, including a previous consent choice.

## 10\. Do Not Track and global privacy controls

There is no common standard for how a site should respond to a browser Do Not Track signal, so we do not act on it. Where the law requires us to honor a recognized opt-out preference signal, we do.

We update this page when the cookies we use change. The current version is always the one on this page, with the date at the top.
