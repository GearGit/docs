# Privacy Policy

How onlinesurvey.ai collects, uses, shares and protects personal data. This policy covers visitors to the website, account holders, and respondents who answer surveys built on the platform.

Last updated September 1, 2026

## 1\. Who we are and what this policy covers

onlinesurvey.ai (the "Service") is operated as a sole proprietorship trading under the name onlinesurvey.ai. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to that operator.

This policy covers three groups of people:

- Visitors, who browse the public website without an account.
- Account holders, meaning customers and the members of their workspaces who sign in and build surveys.
- Respondents, meaning people who answer a survey that a customer built and distributed.

It does not cover third-party websites or services that link to us or that you choose to connect to your account. Their own policies apply to them.

## 2\. Our two roles: controller and processor

For data about visitors and account holders, including your name, your email address, your billing metadata and how you use the product, we are the controller. We decide why and how that data is processed, and this policy describes it.

For respondent data collected through a customer survey, the customer is the controller and we are the processor. The customer decides what to ask, whom to ask, why, on what lawful basis, and how long the answers are kept. We hold and process that data on the customer instructions in order to run the Service for them. The Data Processing Addendum at [/legal/dpa](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/dpa) sets out those obligations in contractual form.

If you are a respondent with a question about a survey you answered, or you want your answers corrected or deleted, contact the survey author first. They are identified in the survey or in the invitation you received, they control the data, and they can act on it directly. If you cannot reach them, write to [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) and we will route your request to them and assist as the law requires.

## 3\. Information we collect

What we hold depends on who you are and what you do. Visitors generate only usage and cookie data. Account holders generate everything in this list. Respondents generate response data, which belongs to the customer who collected it.

- Account and profile data: your name, email address, password in hashed form, organization and workspace names, roles and permissions, invitations you send or accept, and your preferences such as language.
- Customer content: everything you create or upload into the Service, including survey questions and logic, themes and branding, uploaded images, video and other media, contact lists you import for distribution, notes, reports and shared report settings.
- Response data: the answers respondents submit, along with the technical metadata a survey session produces, such as timestamps, the progress through the survey, the completion status, the approximate location derived from the network address, the device and browser type, and any parameters the customer chose to attach to the invitation link.
- Usage and device data: pages and screens viewed, features used, actions taken, referring pages, browser and operating system, device type, language, network address, and diagnostic information such as error reports and performance traces.
- Support data: the content of the messages you send us, and the records of our replies.
- Payment metadata: the plan you bought, the amount, the currency, the billing period, the status of the subscription, the last four digits and the brand of the payment instrument where the payment processor exposes them, and the invoice records. We never receive or store full card numbers, card verification values, or bank credentials. Those are handled entirely by our payment processor.
- Cookies and similar technologies: described in the Cookie Policy at [/legal/cookies](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/cookies).

## 4\. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- provide, operate and maintain the Service, including creating your account, rendering your surveys, collecting and storing responses, and producing analyses and reports;
- run the AI features you choose to invoke, as described in section 7;
- authenticate you, keep your account secure, and detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, spam and security incidents;
- process payments, manage subscriptions, apply plan entitlements and meter usage;
- provide support and respond to your requests;
- send service messages about your account, your subscription, security matters and material changes to the Service or to this policy;
- send product updates and marketing where you have asked for them or where the law otherwise allows it, always with a way to unsubscribe;
- understand how the product is used, in aggregate, so that we can improve it and prioritize work;
- meet our legal obligations, enforce our terms, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use the response data our customers collect to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not sell it.

## 5\. AI processing

The Service offers optional AI features for generating surveys, editing them and analyzing responses. When you invoke one, the content needed for that request, which may include your survey text and the response data you asked to analyze, is sent through our gateway to a third-party large language model provider, and the generated output is returned to you.

Our current provider is OpenAI, accessed through its API. Under the API terms that apply to us, content sent through the API is not used by the provider to train its models. Providers may retain content for a limited period for abuse monitoring under their own policies. We may change or add providers, and the subprocessor list in section 7 is updated when we do.

AI features run only when you invoke them. We do not send your surveys or your response data to a model provider in the background.

AI output is generative and can be inaccurate or biased. The Terms of Service at [/legal/terms](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/terms) set out your obligation to review it before relying on it.

## 6\. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or a comparable law applies to our processing as a controller, we rely on the following bases:

- Performance of a contract, to provide the Service you signed up for and to bill you for it.
- Legitimate interests, to secure the platform, prevent abuse, understand product usage in aggregate, support customers, and grow the business, balanced against your rights.
- Consent, for non-essential cookies and analytics where consent is required, and for marketing messages where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- Legal obligation, to keep tax and accounting records and to respond to lawful requests.

Where we act as a processor for respondent data, the lawful basis for that collection is the customer responsibility, not ours.

## 7\. Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only in the ways described here.

Service providers, also called subprocessors, process data on our behalf under contracts that restrict them to our instructions. Our current subprocessors are:

- Amazon Web Services: cloud hosting, compute, databases and backups, in the United States.
- Cloudflare: content delivery, edge routing, security and media storage.
- Dodo Payments: payment processing, subscription billing and invoicing, acting as merchant of record.
- AutoSend: transactional and campaign email delivery.
- OpenAI: large language model processing for AI features you invoke.
- PostHog: product analytics, to understand how the product is used.
- Google: sign-in with a Google account, and Google Tag Manager with Google Analytics for website measurement.

This list is kept current on this page. Customers who need advance notice of changes should read section 8 of the Data Processing Addendum at [/legal/dpa](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/dpa).

We also share data with your own organization, since workspace members and owners can see the workspace content and membership, and with anyone you deliberately share a report or a survey link with.

We may disclose data where we believe in good faith that it is required by law, regulation, legal process or a governmental request, or where it is reasonably necessary to enforce our terms, to investigate abuse, or to protect the rights, property or safety of any person.

If the business is involved in a merger, an acquisition, a financing or a sale of assets, data may be transferred as part of that transaction. The recipient remains bound by this policy until it is replaced by a policy that gives comparable protection, and we will give notice before any such change takes effect.

## 8\. Hosting and international transfers

The Service is hosted in the United States on Amazon Web Services, with content delivery, edge services and media storage provided by Cloudflare. There is no option to choose a different storage region.

If you or your respondents are outside the United States, personal data will be transferred to and processed in the United States, and possibly in other countries where our subprocessors operate. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws where you live.

Where a transfer requires a safeguard under the General Data Protection Regulation, the United Kingdom regime or a comparable law, we rely on the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, the United Kingdom Addendum where relevant, and equivalent mechanisms, together with technical measures including encryption in transit and at rest. See section 15 of the Data Processing Addendum at [/legal/dpa](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/dpa).

## 9\. Retention

We keep account data and customer content for as long as the account is open, because that is what makes the Service work.

After an account is closed, we keep its data for 30 days so that you can request an export, and then we delete it from our live systems. Backups are held on their own rotation and age out after that; deleted data remains in a backup only until that backup expires, and it is not restored to live systems except as part of a full disaster recovery.

Response data is retained according to the retention decisions of the customer who collected it. Customers can delete responses, surveys and workspaces at any time.

We keep some records for longer where the law requires it, for example billing and tax records, and records connected with a legal claim, an investigation or an enforcement action. We keep limited records of terminated accounts in order to enforce the terms and prevent repeat abuse.

## 10\. Security

We take measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption of data in transit using current TLS, encryption at rest for stored data and backups, role-based access control within workspaces, restricted and audited internal access on a need-to-know basis, hashed password storage, network and edge protections, and logging and monitoring for anomalous activity.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee that data will never be accessed, disclosed, altered or destroyed by a breach of our safeguards. You also play a part: use a strong and unique password, protect your credentials, manage who has access to your workspace, and remove members who no longer need it.

If a personal data breach affects your data, we will notify you without undue delay as the law and the Data Processing Addendum require.

## 11\. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access the personal data we hold about you, to receive a portable copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to certain processing, to withdraw a consent you gave, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

Account holders can exercise most of these directly in the product: view and edit your profile, export surveys and response data, delete individual responses and surveys, and close the account. For anything the product does not cover, write to [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) from the address on your account.

We respond to rights requests within the time the applicable law allows. We may need to verify your identity first, and we may decline a request where the law permits it, for example where it would reveal another person data or where we are required to keep the record.

You can opt out of marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link in the message or by contacting us. You cannot opt out of service messages about your account, your subscription or a security matter, for as long as you hold an account.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to address the concern first.

## 12\. Respondents

If you answered a survey and want your data corrected or deleted, the fastest route is the survey author, who controls that data and can act immediately. Their identity should be stated in the survey or in the invitation you received.

If you cannot identify or reach the author, write to [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) with the survey link and any detail that would let us locate the response. We will pass the request to the customer and support them in honoring it, as our processor obligations require. We generally cannot delete a customer response data on our own initiative, because it is theirs and not ours.

We do not use the answers you give in a survey for our own purposes beyond running the platform, and we do not build a profile of you across the surveys you answer.

## 13\. Children

The Service is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 as a visitor or an account holder, and account holders must be at least 18 or the local age of majority.

A customer may lawfully survey minors as respondents, with the consents and approvals that this requires. That collection is the customer responsibility, not ours, and is governed by their own privacy notice.

If you believe a child has given us personal data directly, write to [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) and we will delete it.

We may update this policy as the Service changes or as the law requires. The current version is always on this page, with the date at the top. Where a change is material, we will make reasonable efforts to notify account holders by email or inside the Service before it takes effect.

For any privacy question, rights request, subprocessor question or complaint, write to [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai). It reaches the person responsible for privacy matters.
