# Data Processing Addendum

The contractual terms under which onlinesurvey.ai processes respondent personal data on behalf of a customer. This addendum applies where data protection law requires it.

Last updated September 1, 2026

## 1\. Scope and precedence

This Data Processing Addendum (the "Addendum") forms part of the Terms of Service at [/legal/terms](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/terms) between the customer ("Customer") and onlinesurvey.ai, operated as a sole proprietorship ("Operator"). It applies to the processing of personal data that the Operator carries out on the Customer behalf in providing the Service.

It takes effect automatically when the Customer accepts the Terms of Service, without further signature, for every Customer to whom applicable data protection law gives the right to a written processing agreement.

Where this Addendum conflicts with the Terms of Service on the subject of processing personal data, this Addendum controls. Where it conflicts with the Standard Contractual Clauses, those Clauses control.

## 2\. Definitions

"Applicable Data Protection Law" means every privacy and data protection law that applies to a party in respect of the processing, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the United Kingdom GDPR and Data Protection Act, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and United States state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended.

"Customer Personal Data" means personal data contained in Customer content and in response data, which the Operator processes on the Customer behalf.

"Controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data", "processing", "personal data breach" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR, and equivalent terms in other laws are read accordingly. A "business" and a "service provider" under United States state law map to controller and processor.

"Standard Contractual Clauses" means the clauses approved by the European Commission in Decision 2021/914, and the United Kingdom International Data Transfer Addendum where the transfer is subject to United Kingdom law.

## 3\. Roles of the parties

The Customer is the controller of Customer Personal Data. The Operator is the processor. Where the Customer is itself acting as a processor for a third-party controller, the Operator is a subprocessor, and the Customer confirms it has the authority from that controller to appoint the Operator on these terms.

The Operator is an independent controller for the limited data described in the Privacy Policy at [/legal/privacy](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/privacy) as controller data: account details, billing metadata, usage and device data, and support correspondence. This Addendum does not govern that processing.

The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of the collection, for the notices given to data subjects, for the lawful basis and any consent required, and for the accuracy and legality of its instructions.

## 4\. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

Subject matter: the provision of the Service, meaning survey design, distribution, response collection, storage, analysis and reporting.

Duration: for as long as the Customer holds an account, plus the retention window described in section 13.

Nature and purpose: hosting, storing, transmitting, structuring, analyzing and displaying Customer Personal Data, and any other processing needed to provide the Service and the features the Customer chooses to use, including AI features the Customer invokes.

Categories of data subject: respondents to the Customer surveys, contacts on lists the Customer imports for distribution, and members of the Customer workspace.

Categories of personal data: whatever the Customer chooses to collect. Typically this includes contact details, survey answers, and technical session metadata such as timestamps, device and browser information, approximate location derived from the network address, and parameters attached to the invitation link. Special categories are processed only where the Customer chooses to collect them, subject to the restrictions in the Acceptable Use and Fair Use Policy at [/legal/acceptable-use](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/acceptable-use).

## 5\. Processing on documented instructions

The Operator processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer documented instructions, including with regard to international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by law to which the Operator is subject. Where such a legal requirement applies, the Operator will inform the Customer before processing, unless the law prohibits that on important grounds of public interest.

The Terms of Service, this Addendum, and the configuration the Customer chooses within the Service together constitute the Customer complete documented instructions. Additional instructions require agreement in writing and may attract a fee where they are outside the ordinary operation of the Service.

The Operator will inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law. The Operator does not review Customer surveys and has no obligation to detect an unlawful instruction.

The Operator will not sell Customer Personal Data, will not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, will not retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the Service, and will not combine it with data from another source except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 6\. Confidentiality

The Operator keeps Customer Personal Data confidential. Access is limited to personnel and subprocessors who need it in order to provide the Service, and every such person is bound by a duty of confidentiality, whether by contract or by professional obligation, that survives the end of their engagement.

## 7\. Security measures

The Operator implements and maintains technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, taking account of the state of the art, the cost of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing. These currently include:

- encryption of personal data in transit using current TLS, and encryption at rest for stored data and backups;
- role-based access control within customer workspaces, and least-privilege, need-to-know internal access;
- hashed storage of authentication credentials;
- network, edge and application-layer protections against unauthorized access, denial of service and common web attacks;
- logging and monitoring designed to detect anomalous access and activity;
- separation of environments, so that production data is not used for development;
- regular backups and a documented restore process, tested against the platform recovery objectives;
- a process for identifying, escalating and responding to security incidents.

The Operator may change these measures as technology and threats change, provided the level of protection is not reduced.

## 8\. Subprocessors

The Customer gives general written authorization for the Operator to appoint subprocessors to process Customer Personal Data.

The Operator imposes on each subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations that are no less protective than those in this Addendum, and remains fully liable to the Customer for the performance of each subprocessor obligations.

The current list of subprocessors is published in section 7 of the Privacy Policy at [/legal/privacy](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/privacy), and is maintained on that page. The Operator will update that list before a new subprocessor begins processing Customer Personal Data. Customers who want advance notice by email of an addition or a replacement may request it at [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai), and the Operator will give at least 14 days notice to those who have asked.

The Customer may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds within that notice period, in writing and with reasons. The parties will discuss the objection in good faith. If it cannot be resolved and the Operator cannot reasonably provide the Service without that subprocessor, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service, with a pro rata refund of prepaid fees for the unused remainder of the term as the sole remedy.

## 10\. Assistance with data subject requests

The Service gives the Customer the ability to access, correct, export and delete Customer Personal Data directly, which is the primary way the Operator assists with data subject requests.

Where a data subject contacts the Operator directly about Customer Personal Data, the Operator will not respond substantively on the Customer behalf. It will inform the data subject to contact the Customer, and where it can identify the Customer it will forward the request without undue delay.

Taking account of the nature of the processing, the Operator will provide reasonable assistance, by appropriate technical and organizational measures, to help the Customer fulfil its obligation to respond to requests to exercise data subject rights. Where the Customer needs assistance beyond the tools the Service provides, the Operator may charge a reasonable fee for the effort involved.

## 11\. Assistance with assessments and consultation

Taking account of the nature of the processing and the information available to it, the Operator will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer with data protection impact assessments and with prior consultation of a supervisory authority, insofar as they relate to the Operator processing of Customer Personal Data. The information in this Addendum and in the Privacy Policy is intended to satisfy most of that need.

## 12\. Personal data breach notification

The Operator will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

The notification will describe, to the extent known at the time and updated as the investigation proceeds, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it and to mitigate its effects. The Operator will provide reasonable cooperation and information to help the Customer meet its own notification obligations.

Notification is sent to the email address on the Customer account. The Customer is responsible for keeping that address current and monitored. A notification is not an admission of fault or liability by the Operator.

It is the Customer, as controller, that decides whether and how to notify a supervisory authority or a data subject.

## 13\. Deletion or return at the end of the Service

The Service provides export tools that let the Customer retrieve Customer Personal Data at any time while the account is open.

On termination or expiry, the Operator retains Customer Personal Data for 30 days so that the Customer can export it, and then deletes it from live systems. On the Customer written request within that window, the Operator will delete it sooner.

Backups are deleted in accordance with the Operator backup rotation. Until a backup expires, data within it remains subject to this Addendum and is not restored to live systems except as part of a full disaster recovery.

The Operator may retain Customer Personal Data where a law to which it is subject requires it, for the period that law requires, and will continue to protect it under this Addendum for as long as it is held.

## 14\. Audits and information rights

The Operator will make available to the Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum, and will contribute to audits and inspections conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates.

That obligation is ordinarily satisfied by the Operator responding to a written security questionnaire or providing a written description of its measures, no more than once in any 12 month period, unless a supervisory authority requires more or a personal data breach has occurred.

Where an on-site audit is legally required and cannot be satisfied by the means above, the parties will agree in advance on its scope, timing, duration and the confidentiality that applies to it. The audit must not disrupt the Service or compromise the confidentiality or security of another customer data, it must be conducted during business hours, it requires at least 30 days written notice, and the Customer bears its own costs and the Operator reasonable costs of supporting it.

## 15\. International transfers

The Service is hosted in the United States, and Customer Personal Data will be transferred to and processed there and in the countries where the subprocessors in section 9 operate.

Where a transfer of Customer Personal Data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland to the Operator or a subprocessor requires an appropriate safeguard, the Standard Contractual Clauses are incorporated into this Addendum by reference and apply, with Module Two where the Customer is a controller and Module Three where the Customer is itself a processor. The Customer is the data exporter and the Operator is the data importer. The optional docking clause applies, the governing law and forum are as stated in the Terms of Service to the extent the Clauses permit, and the appendices to the Clauses are populated by sections 4, 7 and 9 of this Addendum. The United Kingdom International Data Transfer Addendum applies to United Kingdom transfers, and references in the Clauses are read to include Switzerland and the Swiss authority for Swiss transfers.

The Operator applies supplementary technical measures to transferred data, including encryption in transit and at rest, and will inform the Customer if it becomes unable to comply with the Clauses.

If a transfer mechanism relied on here is invalidated, the parties will work in good faith to put a valid alternative in place.

Each party liability under or in connection with this Addendum is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Terms of Service at [/legal/terms](https://onlinesurvey.ai/legal/terms), and those limits apply in aggregate across the Terms of Service and this Addendum, except where Applicable Data Protection Law does not permit it.
