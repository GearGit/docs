## About this tool

One of the most common mistakes in survey research is collecting too few responses to draw meaningful conclusions. With too small a sample, your results could be off by 10, 15, or even 20 percentage points, and you’d never know it. Our free Sample Size Calculator eliminates the guesswork. Enter your population size, desired confidence level, and acceptable margin of error, and the calculator tells you exactly how many responses you need for statistically valid results.

Sample size calculation depends on three key inputs: (1) your population size: the total number of people you could survey; (2) your confidence level, typically 90%, 95%, or 99%, which represents how certain you want to be that your results reflect the true population; and (3) your margin of error: the acceptable range of variation in your results, usually ±3% to ±5%. The calculator applies the standard statistical formula to return the minimum number of responses needed.

Use this calculator before every survey you launch, whether you’re running customer research, employee surveys, academic studies, market sizing projects, or product validation studies. Knowing your required sample size upfront saves time, money, and prevents publishing results that don’t hold up under scrutiny.

## Sample Size Calculator FAQ

What is sample size in survey research?

Sample size is the number of respondents you need to survey in order to draw statistically valid conclusions about a larger population. A properly calculated sample size ensures your results reflect the true attitudes or behaviors of your target group within a defined margin of error and confidence level. Too few respondents produces unreliable results; far more than necessary wastes time and budget.
