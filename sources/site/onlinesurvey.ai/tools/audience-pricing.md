## About this tool

Running a survey to a targeted audience isn’t free, and knowing what it will cost before you commit your research budget is essential for smart planning. The onlinesurvey.ai Audience Pricing Calculator gives you an instant estimate of how much it will cost to collect survey responses from your specific target demographic. Enter your audience criteria (age, location, profession, industry, household income, or any other targeting parameter) and see a projected cost estimate in seconds.

Audience pricing in survey research depends on several factors: the size and availability of the audience panel, the specificity of your targeting criteria, the number of responses you need, and the length of your survey. More specialized or hard-to-reach audiences (senior executives, medical professionals, high-income households, or niche B2B roles) typically cost significantly more per completed response than general consumer audiences.

Use this calculator to plan your research budget before you commit, compare the cost of reaching different audience segments, and identify the most cost-effective path to statistically meaningful responses from exactly the group you care about.

## Audience Pricing Calculator FAQ

What factors affect the cost of surveying a target audience?

Audience pricing is driven by five main factors: (1) audience specificity: niche or hard-to-reach groups cost more per response; (2) sample size: more responses mean higher total cost, though per-response rates often decrease at volume; (3) survey length: longer surveys have lower completion rates, raising cost per complete; (4) geography: certain countries and regions have different panel pricing; and (5) targeting complexity: layering multiple demographic filters increases the cost of qualifying respondents.
