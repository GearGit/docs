QUESTIONS

## The _honest_ answers.

What teams ask before they pick a plan.

How many responses do I get?

Free includes 250 responses a month across up to 3 active surveys. Basic raises that to 5,000 responses a month and Pro to 20,000, both with unlimited surveys. Pricing is flat per month, not per response, so there is no overage meter running while you field a study.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. 250 responses a month, all 50 question types, full logic and 10 AI credits to try the AI builder. No card required, and no expiry. Emails are not on Free.

How do AI credits work?

Basic includes 250 credits a month and Pro 1,000; Free has 10 to try it. A credit is spent when the AI drafts a survey or reads your results, and we always show what an action costs before you spend it.

Basic or Pro?

Basic is for a team running its own research: 5,000 responses, 250 credits, 1,200 emails, 3 seats, branding removal and full logic. Pro is for serious researchers and client work: 20,000 responses, 1,000 credits, 5,000 emails, 10 seats, quotas, loops, scoring, multiple endings, password-protected surveys, your own share preview and priority support.

What does Custom mean?

A plan we build with you on a call: your own response, email, seat and AI credit volumes, plus custom domain, SSO, regional hosting and compliance paperwork where your procurement needs them. Everything in Pro is included.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes, up or down, prorated. Annual saves about two months versus monthly.

Do you offer a respondent panel?

No. You bring your own respondents, and we give you every channel to reach them: link, QR, embed and email.
