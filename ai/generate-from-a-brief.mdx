**Create with AI** turns a written brief into a complete draft survey, logic included, that you then edit like any other.

{/* screenshot: The build screen mid-run: the rail on the left listing what was understood from the brief, the draft growing on the canvas. */}

## What it does

On the surveys list, beside **New survey**, sits **Create with AI**, a tinted button with the sparkles mark. It opens the **Create with AI** dialog with one field, **Describe the survey you want**. Its placeholder reads "Describe the survey you want, in your own words." and the hint under it says "This becomes the survey's name until the AI renames it." A counter shows how much of the 8,000 characters you have used. **Draft the survey** starts the run, and the dialog says "Taking you to the build."

The build screen has a rail on the left and the canvas on the right. The rail's **What I understood** lists what it took from your brief before any writing starts, then counts progress as "6 of 14 elements". The canvas shows the stage it is in: **Reading your brief**, **Writing the questionnaire**, **Checking every route through the survey**, **Fixing 2 problems it found** when the check turns something up, and **Assembling the pages**. The questionnaire is written in one pass, so nothing appears until it lands, and the screen says a couple of minutes is normal. Then the draft grows on the canvas, marked **Read-only** until you accept it.

You can leave. The bar says "You can leave this page, the build carries on without you." and "It stays on the Surveys list while it runs." A card on the surveys list tracks the run and reads "ready to review" when it finishes.

When the run ends the bar offers **Use this survey**, **Discard** and **Start again**, with "7 credits used" beside them. While it runs, **Stop** is there; stopping after questions have landed offers **Keep these 9**. If the brief asked for something it could not build, the rail says "2 things cannot be built" and names each with a reason: **Not on your plan**, **Not supported yet** or **Not allowed**. **Brief** reopens what you wrote.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    On the surveys list, click **Create with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the brief">
    Say who you are asking, what you need to learn, and any rules it must follow, such as who is screened out. Plain sentences work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft it">
    Click **Draft the survey**. Watch the build, or go back to your list and return when the card reads "ready to review".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Read the draft on the canvas. Click **Use this survey** to keep it, **Start again** to run the same brief afresh, or **Discard** to drop it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit as usual">
    The accepted survey is an ordinary draft. Open **1 Build** and change anything. See [Build your first survey](/start/build-your-first-survey).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**The result is a draft, and nothing is published.** You review, edit and publish it yourself, exactly as you would a survey you typed. See [Publish and versions](/share/publish-and-versions).

**It builds only what your plan allows.** A construct outside your plan is listed under "things cannot be built" as **Not on your plan** and left out. The rest of the survey is built as asked.

**It costs credits.** The bar reports the number afterwards as "7 credits used". **Start again** costs credits again; the confirmation says so. Free has 10 credits a period, so a survey of this size runs once or twice before the allowance is gone. See [Credits](/ai/credits).

**Stopping while it writes keeps nothing.** A stop during the writing stage leaves no survey. Your brief is saved and **Start again** uses it as it is.

**The brief stays with the survey.** **Brief** on the build screen shows it again. It is not part of the questionnaire respondents see.

When a run cannot start, the dialog says why. Out of credits: "Your organisation has no AI credits left." with "A survey of this size usually costs 5 to 8 credits." and **Add credits**. Not on your plan: "Your plan does not include AI survey generation." with "You can still build this survey by hand." and **Compare plans**. Paused on our side: "AI generation is paused across the platform." with "This is on our side, not yours. Nothing is lost, and your brief is kept." Too many starts: "You have started several runs in the last minute." with "This one can start in 20 seconds."
