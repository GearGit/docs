The Generate from Brief feature lets you describe your research goal in everyday language and have OnlineSurvey's AI produce a ready-to-review questionnaire in seconds. This page covers how to write an effective brief, what the AI builds for you, and how to review and finalize the draft before publishing.

## What the AI Produces

When you submit a brief, the AI reads your intent and assembles a complete study structure. You do not need to specify question types, logic, or flow yourself. The AI proposes:

- A **screener** to qualify the right respondents before they enter the main survey.
- A **quota plan** that defines how many responses to collect per segment (for example, by country, age group, or customer tier).
- **Branching logic** so respondents only see questions relevant to their earlier answers.
- **Scoring questions** where your research goal calls for them, such as NPS, CSAT, or custom scales.
- A **full questionnaire** ordered to follow a natural research narrative.

Every element the AI produces is a proposal. You decide what to keep, what to change, and what to remove before anyone sees your survey.

## Writing a Strong Brief

The brief is a plain-English description of what you want to learn. You do not need to use any special format or keywords. Write as you would explain the study to a colleague.

A brief can be as short as one sentence or as detailed as a paragraph. More context produces a more targeted draft, but even a short prompt generates a usable starting point.

**Example brief:**
> Understand why repeat orders are slowing in the UK. Compare satisfaction and delivery experience against Germany. Focus on customers who have placed at least two orders in the past six months.

<Tip>
Include the audience, the core research question, and any comparisons or segments you care about. The AI uses all of this to shape the screener, quotas, and question flow.
</Tip>

## Generating Your Survey

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open the New Survey Dialog">
    From your dashboard, click **New Survey** in the top-right corner. The new-survey dialog opens with a choice of starting points.

    ![The new-survey dialog showing starting options including 'Describe your study'](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose 'Describe Your Study'">
    Select **Describe your study** from the list of options. This opens the AI brief input panel instead of the blank canvas.

    ![The AI brief input panel with a text field ready for your research description](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Brief">
    Type your research goal into the brief field. You can write one sentence or several. The AI reads the full text before generating anything.

    When you are ready, click **Generate Survey**. The AI processes your brief and builds the draft. This usually takes under 30 seconds.

    ![The brief input field with an example research goal typed in, and the Generate Survey button highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the AI's Proposed Structure">
    After generation, the AI presents a structured outline of what it built. You will see each section labeled: Screener, Quota Plan, Main Questionnaire, and any Scoring or Branching sections the AI added.

    Read through the outline before opening the full draft. If the structure looks wrong at a high level, you can revise your brief and regenerate at this point without spending an extra credit.

    ![The post-generation outline view showing the screener, quota plan, and questionnaire sections](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Full Draft in the Builder">
    Click **Open in Builder** to load the complete questionnaire onto the canvas. Every question, logic rule, and quota the AI proposed appears exactly as it would in a survey you built manually.

    ![The builder canvas showing the AI-generated questionnaire with questions and logic visible](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept, Edit, or Discard Each Element">
    Work through the draft question by question. For each AI-generated element you can:

    - **Keep it as-is** by leaving it unchanged.
    - **Edit it** by clicking directly into the question text, options, or logic settings.
    - **Delete it** by selecting the question and pressing the delete key or using the context menu.

    There is no obligation to keep anything the AI suggested. The draft is a starting point, not a final product.

    ![A question card on the canvas with the edit and delete options visible in the context menu](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finalize and Publish">
    Once you are satisfied with the draft, preview the survey using the **Preview** button to check the respondent experience end to end. When everything looks right, click **Publish** to create a version-pinned snapshot and start collecting responses.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Regenerating or Starting Over

If the initial draft does not match your intent, you can return to the brief panel at any time before publishing. Click **Regenerate from Brief** in the builder toolbar to open your original brief, update it, and generate a new draft. The previous draft is replaced.

<Warning>
Regenerating from a brief replaces the current draft canvas. If you have made manual edits you want to keep, export or copy them before regenerating.
</Warning>

## Credit Usage

Generating a survey from a brief spends one AI credit. The credit cost is displayed in the brief panel before you click **Generate Survey**, so you always know what will be spent before you confirm. See [How AI Credits Work](/ai/how-credits-work) for details on allowances per plan.
