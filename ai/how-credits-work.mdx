AI credits are the unit of measure for AI-powered actions in OnlineSurvey. Every time the AI does meaningful work on your behalf, such as generating a survey from a brief or producing an insight report, it draws from your monthly credit balance. This page explains what spends credits, how many you get on each plan, and how the reset cycle works.

## What Is an AI Credit?

A credit represents one AI generation or analysis action. Think of it as a token: you have a monthly balance, and specific actions draw from that balance. Credits are shared across your workspace, so all members of your team draw from the same pool.

The cost of each action in credits is always shown to you before you confirm, so you will never spend credits by accident.

## Actions That Spend Credits

Not everything in OnlineSurvey costs credits. Building surveys manually, editing question text directly, and viewing results are all free. Credits are spent only when the AI performs a generation or analysis task on your behalf.

| Action | Credits Spent |
|---|---|
| Generate a survey from a brief | 1 credit |
| Inline question edit (per instruction submitted) | 1 credit |
| Generate a narrated insight report | 1 credit |

<Info>
Reviewing an AI proposal and then discarding it does not refund the credit. The AI completed the work when it produced the proposal.
</Info>

## How the Monthly Reset Works

Your credit balance resets on your billing anniversary date each month. If you subscribed on the 15th, your credits reset on the 15th of every month. Your anniversary date is shown in **Workspace Settings** under the **Plan and Usage** tab.

<Note>
Credits do not roll over. Any unused credits at the end of the month are not carried into the next month. Plan your AI usage accordingly, especially if you are on a lower-tier plan.
</Note>

## Checking Your Credit Balance

You can see your current balance at any time by opening **Workspace Settings** and navigating to the **Plan and Usage** tab. The balance shown reflects credits remaining for the current month across all workspace members.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="What if I run out of credits?">
Running out of credits does not lock you out of OnlineSurvey. You can continue to build, edit, and publish surveys manually using the full builder. You can also distribute surveys and view results without any credit. The only thing you lose access to is AI-powered generation and analysis actions until your balance resets or you upgrade your plan.

To get more credits immediately, upgrade to a higher plan from **Workspace Settings**, under **Plan and Usage**. The new allowance takes effect as soon as the upgrade is confirmed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do credits carry over to the next month?">
No. Credits reset on your billing anniversary date each month and any unused balance is cleared. There is no way to bank credits from a light month and use them during a heavy month. If you regularly find yourself running out before the reset, consider upgrading to a plan with a higher monthly allowance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are credits shared across my whole workspace?">
Yes. Every member of your workspace draws from the same monthly credit pool. If you are on the Basic plan with 250 credits and your team has five members, all five members share those 250 credits. Keep this in mind when planning AI usage across larger teams.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the credit cost shown before I spend?">
Yes. Wherever you initiate an AI action, the cost in credits is displayed before you confirm. You will see a line such as "This action costs 1 credit (you have 47 remaining)" before you click the final confirm button. You can cancel at that point without spending anything.
</Accordion>
