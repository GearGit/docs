AI credits are the allowance your plan gives for the two AI features: drafting a survey from a brief and generating insights. This page says what each costs and where to watch the balance.

{/* screenshot: Plan and billing, the Usage this period panel with the AI credits row and its projection. */}

## What it does

Credits belong to the organization and refill every billing period. Free has none. Basic has 50 a period, Pro has 200, and Custom is set in your agreement. Both AI features draw from the same pool.

Two things cost credits today.

**Create with AI** spends credits to draft a survey from a brief. The out-of-credits notice puts a typical survey at 5 to 8 credits, and the build screen reports the exact number afterwards as "7 credits used". **Start again** runs the whole brief again and costs again. See [Generate from a brief](/ai/generate-from-a-brief).

**Generate** on **AI Insights** spends credits to analyze your responses. The **Analysis plan** shows **Est. cost** before you run it. Setting up the plan reads your survey's structure only. See [AI insights](/results/ai-insights).

A run reserves credits when it starts and settles to what it actually used when it ends. When generation is paused on our side, the message says "Nothing was saved and no credits were used."

The balance lives on **Plan and billing**, under **Usage this period**. The **AI credits** row shows what is used of the cap, how much is left, and, once the pace warrants it, a projection: "At this rate you run out of AI credits around 24 Sep."

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan and billing">
    In the sidebar, under your organization, click **Plan & billing**. Only organization admins see this entry; a member who needs a change asks an admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the AI credits row">
    Under **Usage this period**, find **AI credits**. The row reads how many are used of the cap and how many are left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add credits by moving the plan up">
    Credits come with the plan. Click **Compare plans**, pick a plan, and confirm with **Confirm change**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**Free has no credits.** On Free, neither **Create with AI** nor **AI Insights** can run. Both unlock on Basic and above. See [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits).

**Running out stops the next run, not the current one.** On Basic and Pro the cap is soft: a run that crosses zero is allowed to finish, and the overrun is taken out of the next period's allowance. Once you are overdrawn, a new run does not start until the period resets or the plan moves up.

**Out of credits reads the same everywhere.** The dialog says "Your organisation has no AI credits left." with an **Add credits** button that opens **Plan and billing**. A run that runs dry partway says "Your AI credits ran out partway through" and "The run stopped and nothing was saved. Adding credits lets you run the same brief again from the beginning."

**The projection is a pace, not a promise.** "At this rate you run out of AI credits around" a date is read off this period's use so far. A quiet week moves it out; a busy one pulls it in.

**Credits are the only AI meter.** Reading analytics, exporting data, generating test responses and building reports use no credits.
