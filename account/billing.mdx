OnlineSurvey billing is straightforward: you pay monthly or annually for the plan that fits your team, and you can change plans at any time. This page covers how charges work, how upgrades and downgrades are handled, and what to expect if you cancel.

## Monthly vs. Annual Billing

You can pay for any paid plan on a monthly or annual basis. Annual billing is set at checkout and saves you approximately two months compared to paying month by month.

<Note>
  Annual billing must be selected at the time you first subscribe or upgrade. You cannot switch between monthly and annual billing mid-cycle. If you want to change your billing frequency, contact [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) and the team will assist at your next renewal date.
</Note>

## The Free Plan

The Free plan does not require a credit card. You can use it indefinitely within the Free plan limits. A card is only required when you upgrade to a paid plan.

## Upgrading Your Plan

You can upgrade from Free to Basic, from Free to Pro, or from Basic to Pro at any time from **Settings > Plan**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan Settings">
    Click your account avatar in the top-right corner, select **Settings**, and then click **Plan** in the left sidebar.

    ![The Plan settings page showing the current plan and an Upgrade button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new plan">
    Click **Upgrade** next to the plan you want. A checkout dialog appears showing the new price and the prorated amount due today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Enter your card details if you have not added one before, then click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new plan limits take effect immediately.

    ![The checkout dialog showing the prorated charge and a payment form](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Upgrades are **prorated**. If you upgrade halfway through a monthly cycle, you are charged only for the remaining days in that cycle at the new plan rate, minus the unused portion of your current plan.

## Downgrading Your Plan

To downgrade, go to **Settings > Plan** and click **Change Plan**, then select a lower tier.

A downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then, you retain your current plan's features and limits. At renewal, your plan switches to the lower tier.

<Warning>
  Before downgrading, check that your usage fits within the lower plan's limits. For example, if you have 10 workspaces on Pro and downgrade to Basic (3 workspaces), you will need to reduce your workspaces to 3 before the downgrade completes. The platform will prompt you to do this before confirming the change.
</Warning>

## Cancelling Your Subscription

To cancel, go to **Settings > Plan** and click **Cancel Plan**. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period. After that, your account moves to the Free plan automatically. Your surveys, data, and settings are retained, but access is limited to Free plan conditions.

<Note>
  Cancelling does not delete your account or your data. If you decide to re-subscribe later, everything is still there waiting for you.
</Note>

## Billing FAQ

<Accordion title="Can I switch plans mid-month?">
  Yes. You can upgrade at any time and the charge is prorated immediately. You can initiate a downgrade at any time, but it takes effect at the end of your current billing period, not on the day you request it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do unused responses carry over to the next month?">
  No. Response limits reset on your billing anniversary each month. Unused responses do not roll over to the following month. The same applies to AI credits and email sends.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my response limit?">
  When you reach your monthly response limit, new responses stop being recorded. Respondents see a standard survey-closed message. Your existing collected responses are not affected. Responses resume recording as soon as your limit resets at the start of the next billing cycle, or immediately if you upgrade to a plan with a higher response limit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I find my invoices?">
  All invoices are available in **Settings > Billing**. Each invoice is downloadable as a PDF. Invoices are also emailed to the billing address on file after each successful charge.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
  Prorated refunds are not issued for downgrades or cancellations made mid-cycle. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) and the support team will review your case.
</Accordion>
