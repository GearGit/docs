Organizations and workspaces are the two layers that structure everything in OnlineSurvey. This page explains what each layer does, how they relate to your billing plan, and how to set them up for your team.

## Organizations

An organization is the top-level account in OnlineSurvey. It is tied directly to your billing plan, so your response limits, AI credits, and seat count all belong to the organization as a whole. Every person you invite joins the same organization, and all workspaces inside it draw from the same plan resources.

Each organization has exactly one Owner, who is responsible for billing and top-level settings. Admins and Members work within the organization but have different levels of access. See [Roles and Seats](/account/roles-and-seats) for a full breakdown.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a shared folder inside your organization where surveys live. Workspaces let different teams or projects stay organized without mixing surveys together. For example, you might keep a "Product Research" workspace separate from a "Customer Feedback" workspace.

Every survey belongs to exactly one workspace. Moving a survey between workspaces is done from the survey's settings panel.

## How Many Workspaces You Get

The number of workspaces available depends on your plan.

| Plan | Workspaces |
|------|------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Basic | 3 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Custom | Configurable |

If you need more workspaces than your current plan allows, you can upgrade from the **Settings > Plan** page or contact [support@onlinesurvey.ai](mailto:support@onlinesurvey.ai) if you are on a Custom plan.

## Creating a Workspace

Only Owners and Admins can create workspaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace switcher">
    Click your organization name in the top-left corner of the dashboard. A dropdown shows all workspaces you have access to.

    ![The workspace switcher dropdown open in the top navigation bar](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'New Workspace'">
    At the bottom of the dropdown, click **New Workspace**. A dialog box appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Type a name for the workspace, then click **Create**. The workspace is created immediately and you are switched into it.

    ![The 'Create workspace' dialog with a name field and a Create button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the **New Workspace** option is greyed out, you have reached the workspace limit for your plan. Upgrade your plan to create additional workspaces.
</Note>

## Switching Between Workspaces

Click your organization name in the top-left corner of the dashboard at any time. The dropdown lists every workspace you have access to. Click any workspace name to switch to it. The survey list updates immediately to show only the surveys that belong to that workspace.

![The workspace switcher showing three workspaces with the active one highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)

## Renaming or Deleting a Workspace

Go to **Settings > Workspaces**, hover over the workspace you want to change, and click the three-dot menu. From there you can rename the workspace or delete it.

<Warning>
  Deleting a workspace permanently deletes all surveys inside it, including their collected responses. This action cannot be undone. Export any data you need before deleting.
</Warning>
