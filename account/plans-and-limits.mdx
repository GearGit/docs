OnlineSurvey offers four plans to fit teams from solo researchers to enterprise insights departments. This page summarises what each plan includes so you can choose the right fit for your team.

<Tip>
  All 50 question types are available on every plan, including Free. Core logic tools such as branching, display logic, piping, and carry-forward are also available on all plans. Advanced operational features including quotas, loops, scoring, and multiple endings require the Pro plan or higher.
</Tip>

## Understanding Monthly Limits

**Responses** count every completed or partial submission recorded against your account in a calendar month. If you hit your response limit, new responses stop being recorded until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade.

**AI credits** are consumed each time you use an AI feature: generating a survey from a brief, editing a question with an AI instruction, or generating a narrated insights report. Credits reset on your billing anniversary each month.

**Emails** count each individual email sent from an email campaign within the platform. Unused emails do not carry over to the next month.

<Info>
  Limits reset on your billing anniversary date each month, not on the first of the calendar month. Your anniversary date is shown in **Settings > Plan**.
</Info>

## Upgrading Your Plan

To upgrade, go to **Settings > Plan** and click **Upgrade**. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of your current billing period. See [Billing, Upgrades, and Plan Changes](/account/billing) for full details.
