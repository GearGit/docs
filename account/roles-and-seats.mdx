Roles and seats control who can access your organization and what each person can do. This page explains the three roles, how to invite and manage team members, and how seat limits apply to each plan.

## The Three Roles

Every person in your organization is assigned one of three roles.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full control over the organization. Manages billing, can delete the organization, and holds all Admin permissions. There is exactly one Owner per organization. Ownership can be transferred to another Admin from **Settings > Team**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield">
    Can invite and remove team members, change roles, manage workspaces, and access billing settings. Admins can do everything a Member can do, plus team and account management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Member" icon="user">
    Can build and run surveys within the workspaces they have been assigned to. Members cannot access billing, invite new users, or manage workspaces.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Seat Limits by Plan

Each plan includes a maximum number of seats. A seat is used whenever a person accepts an invitation and joins your organization.

| Plan | Seats Included |
|------|---------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Basic | 3 |
| Pro | 10 |
| Custom | Configurable |

<Tip>
  On the Free plan, the single seat belongs to the Owner. To collaborate with teammates, upgrade to Basic or higher.
</Tip>

If all seats are filled, new invitations will not be sent until you either remove an existing member or upgrade to a plan with more seats.

## Inviting a Team Member

Only Owners and Admins can invite new members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team Settings">
    Click your account avatar in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**. In the left sidebar, click **Team**.

    ![The Team settings page showing current members and an Invite Member button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Invite Member'">
    Click the **Invite Member** button. A dialog appears asking for an email address and a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email address and choose a role">
    Type the invitee's email address, select either **Admin** or **Member** from the role dropdown, and click **Send Invite**.

    ![The invite dialog with an email field, role selector, and Send Invite button](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invitee accepts the invitation">
    The invitee receives an email with a link to join your organization. Once they accept, they appear in your team list and a seat is consumed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invitation links expire after 72 hours. If an invitee misses the window, resend the invitation from **Settings > Team** by clicking the three-dot menu next to their pending entry.
</Note>

## Changing a Member's Role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Locate the person whose role you want to change in the member list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new role">
    Click the role badge next to their name to open the role selector, then choose the new role. The change takes effect immediately.

    ![The member list with the role badge dropdown open showing Admin and Member options](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Owner role cannot be assigned through the role selector. To transfer ownership, use the **Transfer Ownership** option in **Settings > Team**, which requires the current Owner to confirm the action.
</Note>

## Removing a Seat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the member's menu">
    Click the three-dot menu to the right of the member you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Remove from organization'">
    Click **Remove from organization** and confirm. The seat is freed immediately. Surveys created by the removed member remain in the workspace and are not deleted.

    ![The three-dot menu on a member row with 'Remove from organization' highlighted](/images/placeholder.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a member revokes their access immediately. They will not receive a notification. Make sure any in-progress surveys they own are handed off before removal.
</Warning>
