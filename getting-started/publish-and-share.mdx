Publishing is the step that takes your survey from a private draft to a live study that respondents can fill in. This page shows you how to publish your survey, get your shareable link, and control who can access it.

## How Publishing Works

When you click Publish, OnlineSurvey creates an immutable, version-pinned snapshot of your questionnaire. That snapshot is what respondents see, and it never changes mid-fieldwork, even if you keep editing the draft. This protects the integrity of your data: every respondent answers the exact same questionnaire.

You can continue refining the draft in the builder at any time after publishing. When you are ready to release those changes, you publish again, which creates a new snapshot.

<Note>
  Responses collected against an older published version are always labelled with that version, so you can tell them apart in the results.
</Note>

<Steps>

  ### Click Publish

  From the builder canvas, click the **Publish** button in the top-right toolbar. OnlineSurvey runs a quick validation check to flag any incomplete questions or broken logic before it publishes.

  If the check finds issues, a list appears with links to each problem. Fix them and click Publish again. If everything is clear, the survey goes live immediately.

  ![The Publish button in the top-right toolbar and the validation summary dialog](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Copy the Share Link

  Once your survey is published, the **Share** panel opens automatically. At the top you will see the direct survey link, a short URL that you can send to anyone.

  Click **Copy link** to copy it to your clipboard. You can also click **Download QR code** to get a QR image suitable for print materials, posters, or presentation slides.

  ![The Share panel showing the survey link, the Copy link button, and the QR code download option](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Set Optional Access Controls

  Before distributing the link, you can apply two optional controls in the Share panel.

  **Password protection** requires respondents to enter a password before they see the first question. This feature is available on the Pro plan and above. It is useful for internal studies or surveys meant for a specific audience. Type a password into the Password field and click Save.

  **Response cap** automatically closes the survey once a set number of responses have been collected. Enter the number in the Response cap field. When the cap is reached, new visitors see a closed message instead of the questionnaire.

  ![The access controls section of the Share panel with the Password and Response cap fields](/images/placeholder.png)

  <Tip>
    You can change or remove the password and response cap at any time without republishing the survey.
  </Tip>

  ### Send or Embed the Survey

  With your link copied, you have several ways to reach respondents.

  - **Direct link or QR code.** Paste the link into an email, a Slack message, or a social post, or display the QR code wherever your audience can scan it.
  - **Built-in email campaign.** Select the **Email** tab in the Share panel to upload a contact list and send a branded invitation directly from OnlineSurvey.
  - **Embed on a web page.** Select the **Embed** tab to get a snippet you can paste into any website or CMS. The survey renders inline without redirecting visitors to another page.
  - **Panel handoff.** If you are using a third-party research panel, select the **Panel** tab for the redirect URLs and instructions your panel provider needs.

  ![The Share panel tabs showing Link, Email, Embed, and Panel options](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>

## Editing After Publishing

After you publish, the builder stays open and fully editable. Any changes you make update the draft only, not the live survey. Respondents continue to fill the published snapshot until you publish again. This means you can refine question wording, test new logic, or add sections to the next version without interrupting fieldwork that is already under way.

When you are ready to view incoming responses, see [Read Your First Survey Responses](/getting-started/read-your-first-responses).
