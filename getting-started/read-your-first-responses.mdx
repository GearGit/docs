Once your survey is live and respondents start submitting answers, OnlineSurvey gives you several ways to explore what they said. This page introduces the four main views you will use first: the Results tab, the Responses grid, the respondent path replay, and the data export.

<Note>
  Results update in real time. You do not need to refresh the page to see new responses arrive.
</Note>

<Steps>

  ### Open the Results Tab

  From the builder, click the **Results** tab in the top navigation bar. This is your live dashboard for the study.

  The Results tab shows a summary card for every question in the survey. Closed questions display bar or donut charts with percentage breakdowns. Scale questions show an average score and a distribution chart. Open-ended questions show a scrollable list of verbatim responses.

  ![The Results tab showing a mix of bar charts, a scale summary, and a verbatim list](/images/placeholder.png)

  The response count at the top of the page updates as new submissions come in. You can filter the entire Results tab by any answer, date range, or custom variable using the **Filter** button above the summary cards.

  ### Browse the Responses Grid

  Click **Responses** in the left sidebar to switch to the grid view. The grid shows one row per respondent, with each column representing a question in your survey.

  Scroll horizontally to see every column, or use the column picker to hide questions you do not need right now. Click any column header to sort the grid by that question's answers. Use the search bar to find a specific respondent by their ID or by text they typed in an open-ended answer.

  ![The Responses grid with one row per respondent and column headers matching question numbers](/images/placeholder.png)

  <Tip>
    You can flag individual responses for follow-up by clicking the bookmark icon at the start of a row. Flagged responses are easy to filter later.
  </Tip>

  ### Replay a Respondent Path

  To see exactly how one respondent moved through the survey, click their row in the grid. A detail panel opens on the right showing their answers in sequence, including any questions they were skipped to by logic rules.

  This view is especially useful when your survey has branching or screen-out logic, because it lets you confirm that a respondent took the path you intended.

  ![The respondent detail panel showing a sequential list of questions and answers for a single respondent](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Export to CSV

  When you are ready to analyse the data outside OnlineSurvey, click **Export** in the top-right corner of the Results or Responses view. Choose your preferred format from the menu.

  - **CSV** produces a flat file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any analysis tool.
  - **Excel (.xlsx)** includes light formatting and a separate sheet for open-ended responses.
  - **SPSS (.sav)** exports labelled variable data ready for SPSS or compatible statistical software.
  - **Data dictionary** produces a reference file listing every variable name, question text, and answer code.

  Click **Download** and the file saves to your browser's default download folder.

  ![The Export menu showing the four format options with a Download button at the bottom](/images/placeholder.png)

</Steps>
