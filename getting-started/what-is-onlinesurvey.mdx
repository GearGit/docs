OnlineSurvey is a research-grade survey platform built for B2B research and insights teams. This page explains what the platform does, who it is designed for, and how a study flows from your first question all the way to a finished report.

## What OnlineSurvey Does

OnlineSurvey lets your team design a study, publish it, send it to respondents, and read back the results, all in one place. A study can be as simple as a single-question NPS pulse or as complex as a branching, quota-managed market-research questionnaire with dozens of logic rules.

The platform sits in a deliberate sweet spot: it goes much deeper than a form builder, giving you 50 question types, 13 logic tools, and export formats that analysts actually use. At the same time, it costs a fraction of traditional enterprise research suites and takes hours to set up rather than weeks.

## Who It Is For

OnlineSurvey is built for research managers, insights leads, customer experience teams, and anyone who needs to run structured studies without relying on a developer or a research agency. If your work involves collecting structured feedback, measuring opinions, or running trackers, OnlineSurvey is designed for you.

## How a Study Flows

Every study on OnlineSurvey follows the same natural arc.

1. **Brief.** You describe what you want to learn, either by typing a plain-English brief for the AI to turn into a draft survey, or by building from scratch.
2. **Survey.** You refine the questionnaire in the builder, choosing question types, writing answer options, and adding logic.
3. **Fieldwork.** You publish the survey and share a link, run an email campaign, or hand off to a panel provider. Respondents complete the live version.
4. **Insight.** As responses arrive, live charts update in real time. The AI narrates the results as plain-English insights so you can spot patterns without digging through cross-tabs.
5. **Report.** You export the data to CSV, Excel, or SPSS, or share a results link directly with stakeholders.

You can run a complete study on the free plan with no credit card required. It includes 250 responses, all 50 question types, the full logic toolset, and 10 AI credits. When your research needs grow, paid plans start at $49 per month.
