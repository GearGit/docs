The builder is where every OnlineSurvey study begins. This page walks you through creating a new survey, adding your first questions, and previewing the experience before you send it to anyone.

<Steps>

  ### Create a New Survey

  From your OnlineSurvey dashboard, click **New Survey** in the top-right corner. A dialog appears asking you to name the survey and choose how to start.

  Give the survey a clear, descriptive name so your team can find it later. Then choose one of two starting options:

  - **Start blank** to open an empty builder canvas and add questions yourself.
  - **Draft with AI** to type a plain-English brief and let the AI generate a first draft for you.

  If you choose AI, type a sentence or two describing what you want to learn, for example: "I want to understand why enterprise customers churn in the first 90 days." The AI will return a structured questionnaire you can edit straight away.

  ![The new survey dialog showing the name field and the blank vs AI options](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Open the Builder Canvas

  After you name your survey and choose a starting point, OnlineSurvey opens the builder canvas. The canvas is the main editing space where all your questions live.

  On the left you will see the question list, which shows every question in order. The central area displays the question you are currently editing. The right panel shows settings for that question, such as whether it is required and whether it has a character limit.

  ![The builder canvas with the question list on the left, the edit area in the center, and settings on the right](/images/placeholder.png)

  <Tip>
    Your whole team can work on the same survey at the same time. If a colleague is editing a question, you will see their cursor appear next to it in the question list.
  </Tip>

  ### Add Your First Question

  Click **Add question** at the bottom of the question list. A panel slides in showing all 50 question types, organized into six families: Closed, Scales, Grids, Ranking, Open Ends, and Dates and Times.

  Select the type that fits your first question. For most surveys, a good starting point is **Single choice** (one answer from a list) or **Short text** (a free-text response). Click the type and it is added to the canvas.

  ![The question-type picker showing the six question families](/images/placeholder.png)

  Click into the question text area and type your question. Then add your answer options if the question type requires them. Each option appears as a row you can edit, reorder by dragging, or delete with the trash icon.

  Mark the question as **Required** in the right-hand settings panel if you want to prevent respondents from skipping it.

  ![A single-choice question being edited, with answer options listed and the Required toggle turned on](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Add a Second Question

  Click **Add question** again and repeat the process for your second question. You can also duplicate an existing question by hovering over it in the question list and clicking the copy icon, which is useful when questions share the same answer scale.

  Drag questions up or down in the question list to reorder them at any time. There is no save button; OnlineSurvey saves your work automatically after every change.

  ![Two questions visible in the question list, with drag handles on the left of each row](/images/placeholder.png)

  ### Preview the Survey

  Before publishing, click **Preview** in the top toolbar. This opens a live, interactive preview of your survey exactly as respondents will see it.

  Work through each question as if you were a respondent. Check that the question wording is clear, the answer options are complete, and the flow feels natural. Close the preview tab when you are done to return to the builder.

  ![The survey preview showing the first question as a respondent would see it on a desktop browser](/images/placeholder.png)

  <Note>
    Preview responses are not counted toward your plan's response limit and do not appear in your results.
  </Note>

</Steps>

## What to Do Next

Your survey is now ready to publish. You can add logic rules at any point before or after publishing, such as branching respondents to different questions based on their answers. When you are happy with the questionnaire, head to [Publish Your Survey and Share the Link](/getting-started/publish-and-share) to make it live.
