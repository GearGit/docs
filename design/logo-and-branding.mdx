Your logo sits in a band at the top of every respondent screen, and on a paid plan the survey carries your button wording and none of ours.

{/* screenshot: The Survey header band on the canvas with a logo, and the Logo panel open beside it. */}

## What it does

The logo is the **Survey header**, the band at the top of the builder canvas above the first screen. It is not part of the [theme](/design/themes). Click the band and the panel opens with:

- **Logo**, with **Add logo** to pick an image from your [media library](/design/media-library).
- **Alignment**: **Left**, **Centre** or **Right**.
- **Shows on**: three switches, **Welcome screen**, **Question screens** and **End screens**. A new logo shows on all three.
- **Alt text**, with the placeholder "What the mark says". This is what a screen reader announces.
- **Remove logo**.

Sizing happens on the canvas. The panel says it in one line: "Drag the logo on the canvas to size it". Drag the mark's corner and the band grows or shrinks with it. There is no size field and no preset.

Before a logo is added, the panel explains the band: "The logo sits at the top of the screen. It starts out on all of them, the welcome, the questions and the endings, and you can switch any of those off once it is here. Drag its corner on the canvas to size it."

The mark stands alone. There is no brand name beside it; if you want your name on the screen, put it in the image.

**Branding** is the "Powered by" badge at the foot of every screen and the button that ends each screen. Click the footer strip on the canvas to open the **Footer** panel, which has one switch, **Show Powered by**. On a plan that cannot remove branding the switch is locked and labeled "Paid feature", with the title "Removing branding is a paid feature". The same switch appears in the theme's **Footer** section as **Show “Powered by”** once your plan allows it.

The button is the other half. Click a screen's button and the **Button** panel opens. On a paid plan you write your own **Button text**, or choose **Auto redirect** to a URL on an ending. On the free plan the panel reads "Your plan ends every screen with our button. Alignment is yours to set." and its title says "Write your own button, or redirect instead, on a paid plan".

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Survey header band">
    At the top of the canvas, click the band above your first screen. The panel titled **Survey header** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the logo">
    Click **Add logo** and pick an image from the library, upload one, or paste a link. PNG, JPG, WEBP and SVG all work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size and align it">
    Drag the logo's corner on the canvas until it is the size you want. Set **Alignment** in the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it shows">
    Under **Shows on**, switch off **Welcome screen**, **Question screens** or **End screens** if the logo should not appear there. The band's chip states the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the alt text">
    Fill **Alt text** with what the mark says, your company name for example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove our badge">
    On a paid plan, click the footer strip and switch off **Show Powered by**. Then click a button and write your own **Button text**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**A screen with the logo switched off still keeps its top band when the progress rail needs it.** The band disappears entirely only when nothing is left in it.

**Preview shows what your plan serves.** If your plan cannot remove the badge, preview shows it, so what you see is what respondents get.

**Reports have their own branding switch.** A shared report carries its own logo and badge setting under **Branding** in the report's settings. "Custom logo and removing branding are available on a paid plan." See [Reports](/results/reports).

**Which plans are paid is on [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits).**
