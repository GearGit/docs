Preview opens your draft in its own tab and runs it exactly as a respondent would run it, rules and all, so you can test a survey before it goes live.

{/* screenshot: A preview tab: the survey on its theme, with the Preview mode strip along the top. */}

## What it does

**Preview** in the builder toolbar opens a new tab. What you see is your draft as it stands at that moment, not the published version, on your theme, with your logo, your endings and your logic. The same engine that serves respondents drives the walk: branching, show and hide, piping, carry-forward, randomization, variables, scoring, quotas and loops all behave as they will in the field. A strip along the top reads "Preview mode. Your walk is saved as a preview response." and **Close preview** closes the tab.

Preview can be narrowed to one part of the survey:

- **Preview this question**, at the foot of the panel for a selected question, shows that question on its own. Nothing follows it, and the strip says nothing here is saved.
- **Preview this block**, in a block's panel, walks that block from its first screen to its last and stops.
- **Preview this ending**, in an ending's panel, shows that end screen by itself.

On the **2 Publish** tab, **Preview as respondent** does something different. It opens your live link in a new tab, so you see the published version, exactly as someone with the link sees it, and a run you finish there is recorded like any other response.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preview">
    In the builder, click **Preview** in the toolbar. A new tab opens on the welcome screen of your draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the survey">
    Answer as a respondent would. Try the answers that should branch, skip or screen someone out, and check that each ending reads the way you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check one question">
    Select a question on the canvas and click **Preview this question** in its panel to see that question alone. Anything it borrows from an earlier question shows everything it could show, since no earlier answer exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close and fix">
    Click **Close preview** to close the tab, edit in the builder, and preview again. The next preview always picks up your latest edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

**A full preview walk is kept as a preview response.** It is filed apart from live responses under its own type, counts toward nothing, fills no quota, and uses none of your plan's responses. A single question or a single ending previewed on its own records nothing.

**Preview shows your plan's branding.** If your plan cannot remove the "Powered by" badge, the preview shows it too. What you see is what you can serve.

**For data, use test responses.** Preview is for walking the survey yourself. To fill your results with realistic data that covers every path, use **Generate test responses** on the **4 Responses** tab, then open any of them to follow its journey. See [Responses grid](/results/responses-grid).

**Publish is a separate step.** Nothing you do in preview publishes anything. When the draft is right, publish it and respondents get that version. See [Publish and versions](/share/publish-and-versions) and [What respondents see](/share/what-respondents-see).
